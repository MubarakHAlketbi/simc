Arms Warrior Flasks
The best flask for Arms Warriors is  Flask of the Shattered Sun  or  Flask of the Blood Knights .
Arms Warrior Combat Potion
 Potion of Recklessness  is the best combat potion in all content for Arms Warriors, though  Light's Potential  is a very close alternative.  Draught of Rampant Abandon  can be powerful, but the frequent need to move to avoid being silenced makes it less useful in practice.
Arms Warrior Health Potion
 Silvermoon Health Potion  is the best healing potion in Midnight Season 1.
Arms Warrior Weapon Buff
Arms Warriors should apply  Thalassian Phoenix Oil  to their weapon.
Arms Warrior Augment Rune
 Void-Touched Augment Rune is the only new Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1.
Arms Warrior Food
 Blooming Feast,  Champion's Bento, and  Flora Frenzy all provide the maximum benefit.
