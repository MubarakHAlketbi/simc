#### Aligning Cooldowns
PullSudden DeathExecute timing varies, depending on  Sudden Death procs. Storm Bolt is an extremely minor damage increase when used during Bladestorm, though it is not worth talenting at the expense of survivability or mobility.

PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any form of combat, whether raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content, ensure that  Battle Shout is active on yourself and all relevant party members. In higher-end content, applying a food buff, flask, weapon oils, and preparing a combat potion is also a good idea.

A talented attack in the class tree, which serves more of a utility role in keeping enemies within an area, though its damage is rather mediocre. The ability can be reactivated to leap to the spear, though this has very niche applications.

Arms Warriors have several cooldowns, but they're all used more or less the same way - on cooldown as much as possible. When situations or timers allow, cooldowns can be aligned, but they should not be delayed by a significant amount (typically no less than 10-15% of their actual cooldown) unless the delay will allow them to hit multiple targets when they would otherwise hit one, or it will be your final use of the cooldown before the end of the fight regardless.

The reason is that losing an entire use of a cooldown is typically a bigger loss than what is gained by stacking the cooldown with something else to amplify it. Some cooldowns will always align naturally, though others will not, so anticipating those timers and knowing when adds or important damage phases are coming is key.

#### Aligning Cooldowns
PullSudden DeathExecute timing varies, depending on  Sudden Death procs. Storm Bolt is an extremely minor damage increase when used during Bladestorm, though it is not worth talenting at the expense of survivability or mobility.

PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any form of combat, whether raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content, ensure that  Battle Shout is active on yourself and all relevant party members. In higher-end content, applying a food buff, flask, weapon oils, and preparing a combat potion is also a good idea.

A talented attack in the class tree, which serves more of a utility role in keeping enemies within an area, though its damage is rather mediocre. The ability can be reactivated to leap to the spear, though this has very niche applications.

Arms Warriors have several cooldowns, but they're all used more or less the same way - on cooldown as much as possible. When situations or timers allow, cooldowns can be aligned, but they should not be delayed by a significant amount (typically no less than 10-15% of their actual cooldown) unless the delay will allow them to hit multiple targets when they would otherwise hit one, or it will be your final use of the cooldown before the end of the fight regardless.

The reason is that losing an entire use of a cooldown is typically a bigger loss than what is gained by stacking the cooldown with something else to amplify it. Some cooldowns will always align naturally, though others will not, so anticipating those timers and knowing when adds or important damage phases are coming is key.
