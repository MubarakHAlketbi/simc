## ~~~~ Arms Warrior Overview

Arms Warriors are high-intensity damage dealers who specialize in Bleeds, proc gameplay, and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_48.gif)Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=163201/execute) damage. The rotation leads off with a combination of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_savageblow.gif)Mortal Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=12294/mortal-strike), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_meleedamage.gif)Overpower](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=7384/overpower), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_gouge.gif)Rend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=772/rend), punctuated by periods of burst damage from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_warbreaker.gif)Colossus Smash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=167105/colossus-smash), before ending the encounter by way of heavy [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_48.gif)Execute](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=163201/execute) s. Their rotation is quite intensive in both resource generation and spending, leading to spiky gameplay where [![Rage](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_misc_emotionangry.gif)Rage](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/rage-1) quickly fills and empties in large chunks. The trick to carrying this out properly is to spend enough Rage to keep from overcapping while not spending so much that you starve and are unable to fill global cooldowns. Since nearly every ability is a resource spender, Arms Warriors must always plan ahead.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Arms Warrior

Both Colossus and Slayer Arms Warrior stat priorities emphasize weapon damage and Strength (via item level), along with Critical Strike and Haste. Secondary stats can vary greatly depending on the player's level of gear, however, as the value of heavily stacked stats tends to drop as you gear beyond the point of [diminishing returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft).

Critical Strike and Haste also have a complicated relationship - both increase rage generation, but Haste also increases the rate at which that rage can be spent by speeding up the GCD, thereby allowing more abilities to fit into de/buff windows such as Colossus Smash. While the two can frequently flip position, players will generally just want both.

## Arms Warrior Rotation in Midnight Season 1

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| Arms Warriors revolve around using abilities to increase the damage of their attacks, inflict bleeds, and maximize Execute damage. Although the standard rotation is not very complex, there are several synergistic talents that create more complex priorities.

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid Downtime** \- Arms generates the majority of its rage from auto-attacks, so reducing the amount of time spent chasing, running out mechanics, or otherwise not hitting the boss is critical to maximizing performance. There are several abilities to help with this, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_charge.gif)Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100/charge) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_heroicleap.gif)Heroic Leap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6544/heroic-leap), so use them smartly to stay on target as much as possible! |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Keep Abilities on cooldown** \- Arms generates and spends resources in small amounts, but its ability cooldowns are what really drive the rotation. Keeping Colossus Smash, Avatar, and Bladestorm/Demolish on cooldown is crucial, as delaying any one of them can easily cause the others to desync as well, so avoid doing it without good reason - such as a quickly approaching add or increased damage phase. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid wasting Rage** \- Unlike most resources, Rage starts from zero and decays out of combat. Making efficient use of it will help maintain the rotation and eliminate downtime spent waiting for more resources, both in combat and by reducing the amount of time spent moving from one combat to another. | | |

* * *

**Reading Priority Lists**

Abilities and talents are listed from highest to lowest priority in the Arms Warrior rotation. Every time an ability is cast, begin again at the top of the list and move down until there is something that can be cast, then return to the top once more.
