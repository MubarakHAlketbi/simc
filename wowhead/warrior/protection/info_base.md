## ~~~~Protection Warrior Overview

Protection Warriors are World of Warcraft’s original Tank spec, representing the typical "sword and board" character archetype with an aggressive playstyle -- the more you fight, the stronger you are!

As a spec, Protection focuses on dealing direct damage coupled with Bleed applications for passive damage output. Our defensive capabilities are tied to Block, which is arguably one of the most powerful defensive mechanics in the game. We can sometimes even block magic attacks, thus reducing our damage intake by a significant amount.
