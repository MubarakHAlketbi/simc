#### Opener
BEST FURY WARRIOR OPENER
Differing from many specializations, the Fury opener is virtually identical to its single-target priority, with the main goal of triggering Enrage as soon as possible. While pre-casting  Recklessness was an important technique in the past,  Odyn's Fury and new Apex talents make it no longer necessary.

Unless there are upcoming adds or an increased damage phase, stat increasing trinkets and combat potions are best used on the pull aligned with Recklessness/Avatar, heroism, and other cooldowns. In the case of longer fights with two potions, the second should be saved for a following set of adds, increased damage phase, or Execute whenever possible, though take care to use it early enough that combat won't end before those buffs do.

A talented attack in the class tree, which serves more of a utility role in keeping enemies within an area, though its damage is rather mediocre. The ability can be reactivated to leap to the spear, though this has very niche applications.

Fury Warriors have several cooldowns, but they're all used more or less the same way - on cooldown as much as possible. When situations or timers allow, cooldowns can be aligned, but they should not be delayed by a significant amount (typically no less than 10-15% of their actual cooldown) unless the delay will allow them to hit multiple targets when they would otherwise hit one, or it will be your final use of the cooldown before the end of the fight regardless.

The reason is that losing an entire use of a cooldown is typically a bigger loss than what is gained by stacking the cooldown with something else to amplify it. Some cooldowns, like  Recklessness and  Avatar will always align naturally, though others will not, so anticipating those timers and knowing when adds or important damage phases are coming is key.

PRE-COMBAT CHECKS

A talented attack in the class tree, which serves more of a utility role in keeping enemies within an area, though its damage is rather mediocre. The ability can be reactivated to leap to the spear, though this has very niche applications.

Fury Warriors have several cooldowns, but they're all used more or less the same way - on cooldown as much as possible. When situations or timers allow, cooldowns can be aligned, but they should not be delayed by a significant amount (typically no less than 10-15% of their actual cooldown) unless the delay will allow them to hit multiple targets when they would otherwise hit one, or it will be your final use of the cooldown before the end of the fight regardless.

The reason is that losing an entire use of a cooldown is typically a bigger loss than what is gained by stacking the cooldown with something else to amplify it. Some cooldowns, like  Recklessness and  Avatar will always align naturally, though others will not, so anticipating those timers and knowing when adds or important damage phases are coming is key.
