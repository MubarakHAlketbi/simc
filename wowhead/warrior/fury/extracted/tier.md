Tier sets are unique class armor sets that provide powerful spec-specific bonuses when multiple pieces of the set are worn. These bonuses will change based on your character's active talent specialization, ensuring that the bonus is appropriate and valuable to your spec.

This guide provides relevant information about your Fury Warrior tier, including how the tier bonuses work, their effect on the Fury Warrior playstyle, how to acquire tier set pieces, and what gearing strategy you should be using.

 TIER SET BONUSES FOR FURY WARRIOR
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Fury Warrior gear page!

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Fury Warrior
The Season 1 tier set bonus is a direct increase to the power of  Odyn's Fury and  Rampage. Due to both abilities already being highly prioritized, it does not have any particular influence on the rotation, though it is also a fairly mediocre bonus, worth only ~4%.
Rage of the Night Ender
 Warrior Arms 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Rampage damage increased by 10% and Odyn's Fury damage increased by 10%.
 Warrior Fury 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Rampage reduces the cooldown of Odyn's Fury by 2.5 sec and Odyn's Fury damage is increased by an additional 10%.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR FURY WARRIOR TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
