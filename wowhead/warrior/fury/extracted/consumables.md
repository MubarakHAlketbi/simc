Fury Warrior Flasks
The best flask for Fury Warriors is  Flask of the Magisters  or  Flask of the Blood Knights .
Fury Warrior Combat Potion
 Potion of Recklessness  is the best combat potion in all content for Fury Warriors, though  Potion of Recklessness  is a very close alternative.  Draught of Rampant Abandon  can be powerful, but the frequent need to move to avoid being silenced makes it weaker in practice.
Fury Warrior Health Potion
 Silvermoon Health Potion  is the best healing potion in Midnight Season 1.
Fury Warrior Weapon Buff
Fury Warriors should apply  Thalassian Phoenix Oil  to both of their weapons.
Fury Warrior Augment Rune
 Void-Touched Augment Rune is the only new Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1.
Fury Warrior Food
 Blooming Feast,  Champion's Bento, and  Flora Frenzy all provide the maximum benefit.
