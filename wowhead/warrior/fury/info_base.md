## ~~~~ Fury Warrior Overview

Fury is a spec with a fast-paced playstyle that focuses on sustained damage, rather than spikes of big bursts. With only one [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_misc_emotionangry.gif)Rage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100415/rage) spending ability, the goal of the rotation is simple: Generate enough Rage to cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_rampage.gif)Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367/rampage) and maintain [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_unholyfrenzy.gif)Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184361/enrage), along with a multitude of different abilities that will also be used along the way.

Fury Warrior is a good spec for players looking for a playstyle that is always active, since every other ability is a resource generator that has a short or no cooldown, without any downtime spent waiting for something to do. This makes it highly engaging and proves that a specialization doesn't need to be complicated in order to be fun.

| ~~~~ | **Avoid Downtime** \- Fury generates the majority of its rage from abilities, so reducing the amount of time spent chasing, running out mechanics, or otherwise not hitting the boss is critical to maximizing performance. There are several abilities to help with this, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_charge.gif)Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100/charge) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_heroicleap.gif)Heroic Leap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6544/heroic-leap), so use them smartly to stay on target as much as possible! |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Keep Abilities on cooldown** \- nearly every rotational Fury ability generates rage, though only [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_rampage.gif)Rampage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184367/rampage) spends it, and in large amounts. This makes it very important to ensure that abilities are constantly being used to ensure that [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_unholyfrenzy.gif)Enrage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=184361/enrage) is constantly maintained. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid wasting Rage** \- unlike most resources, rage starts from zero and decays out of combat. Making efficient use of it, both in combat and by reducing the amount of time spent moving from one combat to another, will help maintain the rotation and eliminate downtime spent waiting for more resources. | | |

**Reading Priority Lists**

Abilities and talents are listed from highest to lowest priority in the Fury Warrior rotation. Every time an ability is cast, begin again at the top of the list and move down until there is something that can be cast, then return to the top once more.
