#### Single-Target Priority
BEST MARKSMANSHIP HUNTER SINGLE TARGET ROTATION
Marksmanship has a consistent flow to its rotation, where one button leads into the next in a logical way. The main times you will have to worry about the priority are when choosing how to prioritize abilities like  Aimed Shot,  Rapid Fire, and  Moonlight Chakram, particularly when they are all active at the same time.
With  Sentinel selected, the priority is as follows:
The builds used below are covered in the Talents page. Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes.
PRIORITY
Trinket Usage We usually want to pair stat-granting on-use trinkets with  Trueshot.
Cast  Volley on cooldown. On single-target, its main role is doing a bit of damage for us, while activating  Double Tap. Never cast Volley directly before or after  Trueshot, as that wastes the Double Tap generated by either.
Activate  Trueshot, your major cooldown. We ideally press Trueshot right before we normally intend to  Aimed Shot, and ideally, right or shortly after a  Rapid Fire, so that you carry a fresh  Bulletstorm into your cooldowns. It is extremely important that you never cast  Trueshot and  Volley back-to-back in any order, to avoid wasting  Double Tap. Always fire, ideally, an  Aimed Shot between them.
Cast  Arcane Shot to spend the  Precise Shots stacks generated by  Aimed Shot and  Rapid Fire. As a rule of thumb, always fire  Arcane Shot immediately after every  Aimed Shot and  Rapid Fire.
Cast  Rapid Fire if you cannot fit another  Aimed Shot into your  Bulletstorm, or if Bulletstorm is down.
Cast  Aimed Shot whenever it's available.
Cast  Moonlight Chakram as a filler in your cooldowns when you have run out of  Aimed Shots.
Cast  Rapid Fire whenever it's available.
Cast  Steady Shot as a filler. Do not overlook the importance of filling every "empty" moment with these, as they generate significant Focus and  Aimed Shot cooldown reduction.

TRUESHOT
 Trueshot massively increases our critical strike chance, damage, and the cooldown rates of  Aimed Shot and  Rapid Fire, as well as  Aimed Shot's cast time. It makes us do everything stronger and faster.

Our rotational priorities are the same inside of  Trueshot as they are outside of it. The main way we think about it is how we set up for it. The best use of our time, and our greatest damaging ability that we want Trueshot to amplify, is  Aimed Shot. For this reason, a good rule of thumb is to activate Trueshot right before an Aimed Shot comes up in your rotation naturally.

Another good rule of thumb is to come into the Trueshot window with  Bulletstorm already active with a healthy duration. For this reason, we like to cast  Rapid Fire right before entering Trueshot, letting us focus as much of our time as possible on heavily buffed  Aimed Shot.

For  Dark Ranger, activating Trueshot also gives us a  Black Arrow proc, to be spent immediately.

For both Hero Talents, activating  Trueshot turns the Trueshot button into another ability:  Moonlight Chakram for  Sentinel, and  Wailing Arrow for  Dark Ranger. These abilities have one use only and should be used as a "filler" in your cooldowns, at a time where they would not interfere with your other rotational priorities.

ALWAYS BE CASTING
The problem is not so much that people forget that  Steady Shot exists, however. It is understanding the value of time in the DPS equation. Most people press their buttons rather slowly, they press buttons that are not available to them, their DPS rotation stops when they are focused on a mechanic, or they just have bad reaction times. All of these things lead to lost time where you are not casting anything, often for very small windows of time, but these small windows of time add up very quickly, and they are the difference between a good and a great player. Always be casting something, even if it is just  Steady Shot, and you will see a noticeable increase in your DPS.

#### AoE Priority
BEST MARKSMANSHIP HUNTER AOE ROTATION
The primary change for AoE is simply replacing  Arcane Shot with  Multi-Shot, at least while we run  Trick Shots Talent, which we do not always do. While  Trick Shots is a core and crucial mechanic when we run the talent, it actually takes care of itself, because it is generated from  Multi-Shot, a button that we already press to spend our  Precise Shots procs. It is essential that we never cast  Aimed Shot or  Rapid Fire without the  Trick Shots buff, but by just following the same principles as our single-target rotation of always following up all of our  Aimed Shots and  Rapid Fires with a  Multi-Shot, we will always have  Trick Shots uptime without needing to think about it at all.

Note: The current Mythic+/AoE-focused build runs  Aspect of the Hydra over  Trick Shots because Hydra'd  Arcane Shot can proc multiple  Spotter's Marks, which in turn can proc multiple  Lunar Storm. This means you only ever press  Arcane Shot and not  Multi-Shot, no matter how many targets.

TRUESHOT
 Trueshot massively increases our critical strike chance, damage, and the cooldown rates of  Aimed Shot and  Rapid Fire, as well as  Aimed Shot's cast time. It makes us do everything stronger and faster.

Our rotational priorities are the same inside of  Trueshot as they are outside of it. The main way we think about it is how we set up for it. The best use of our time, and our greatest damaging ability that we want Trueshot to amplify, is  Aimed Shot. For this reason, a good rule of thumb is to activate Trueshot right before an Aimed Shot comes up in your rotation naturally.

Another good rule of thumb is to come into the Trueshot window with  Bulletstorm already active with a healthy duration. For this reason, we like to cast  Rapid Fire right before entering Trueshot, letting us focus as much of our time as possible on heavily buffed  Aimed Shot.

For  Dark Ranger, activating Trueshot also gives us a  Black Arrow proc, to be spent immediately.

For both Hero Talents, activating  Trueshot turns the Trueshot button into another ability:  Moonlight Chakram for  Sentinel, and  Wailing Arrow for  Dark Ranger. These abilities have one use only and should be used as a "filler" in your cooldowns, at a time where they would not interfere with your other rotational priorities.

ALWAYS BE CASTING
The problem is not so much that people forget that  Steady Shot exists, however. It is understanding the value of time in the DPS equation. Most people press their buttons rather slowly, they press buttons that are not available to them, their DPS rotation stops when they are focused on a mechanic, or they just have bad reaction times. All of these things lead to lost time where you are not casting anything, often for very small windows of time, but these small windows of time add up very quickly, and they are the difference between a good and a great player. Always be casting something, even if it is just  Steady Shot, and you will see a noticeable increase in your DPS.
