## ~~~~Marksmanship Hunter Overview

Marksmanship Hunter is a ranged, mail-wearing DPS specialization that focuses on long-range, slow-ish gameplay interspersed with periods of high burst damage that is delivered quickly. If you like taking down your enemies from afar with carefully considered, powerful shots while focusing on moving as little as possible, Marksmanship is the spec for you.

## Marksmanship Hunter Rotation in Midnight Season 1

The rotation for Marksmanship Hunter focuses on maximizing your use of certain buffs, and maximizing your number of uses of cooldown-limited abilities. Marksmanship has a fairly consistent flow, where one ability logically flows into the next, with a few priority shifts based on procs and buff uptimes.
