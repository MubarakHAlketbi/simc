 TIER SET BONUSES FOR BEAST MASTERY HUNTER
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Beast Mastery Hunter gear page!

Beast Mastery Hunter Gear Guide
Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Beast Mastery Hunter
The Beast Mastery Tier set bonus is very straight forward, the 2-set buffs  Bestial Wrath (and  Bloodshed) direct damage and the 4-set summons a  Dire Beast whenever you use Bestial Wrath. The most notable thing the tier set does is make Dire Beast setups more viable, as it generates more  Huntmaster's Call beasts. The 2-set is worth roughly 4.5% damage and the 4-set is worth 3% damage.

 Primal Sentry's Maw (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)

Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to their itemization, Beast Mastery Hunter prefer equipping tier pieces on these slots:

Ideally, your off-piece for tier is your shoulders, as has relatively poor stats for both raiding and Mythic+. You also have access to  Pauldrons of the Void Hunter, which provide a small yet relevant bonus.

This is only a minor optimization; wearing five pieces of the tier is fine if you don't have a high item-level alternative. Fully Best-In-Slot setups will often choose to replace the Chest piece instead.  HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR BEAST MASTERY HUNTER TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
