#### Light Cleave
This build offers up a cheap way to do a lot of AoE by sacrificing 2% of your single-target damage for  Wild Thrash, which is very efficient for AoE damage even without the other talents to support it. Use it when there are some adds but you do not want to sacrifice all of your single-target for it.

#### Pack Leader Delve Build
This build is for when you want to do as much AoE damage as possible in raid. This is primarily for the Lightblinded Vanguard Encounter. It sacrifices further single-target damage for the remainder of your AoE talents.

 Further Details

Dark Ranger in Mythic+ is generally weaker on AoE and priority damage, but provides better single-target and defensives. It might therefore be a stronger build if you're looking to push high mythic+, but won't do as well in your weekly keys.

In this generic build, utility talents are chosen to provide the best overall package, but a few choices, such as  Emergency Salve, are not useful in every dungeon, so you should ideally be looking to swap them based on the dungeon. Please see the Further Details section below.

 Further Details

Utility choices for this build are generally very flexible, as solo content allows for more personal expression in terms of how you approach each encounter. We therefore recommend you experiment a bit with choices and adjust them to your own playstyle. This build should offer a solid starting point.
