Below is a list of the recommended gems and enchants to use on your gear during Midnight Season 1.

Beast Mastery Hunter Gems
You should use one  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  and then a mix of  Flawless Quick Amethyst ,  Flawless Deadly Amethyst  and  Flawless Masterful Peridot .
Beast Mastery Hunter Weapon Enchant
All weapon enchants which provide stats are very close.  Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei  is however, the most universal of the bunch, and is therefore the main recommendation here.
 BEST BEAST MASTERY HUNTER CONSUMABLES IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below, we've listed the best flasks, potions, and weapon buffs that Beast Mastery Hunters can use in PvE content.

Beast Mastery Hunter Flask
The best flask for Beast Mastery Hunters is  Flask of the Magisters , for all scenarios, as it is our best stat.
Beast Mastery Hunter Combat Potions
The best choice combat potion in Midnight Season 1 is  Light's Potential , for most scenarios.

 Draught of Rampant Abandon  performs slightly better if you are able to move constantly while it's active. The void zone it spawns will silence you, so you can easily lose the extra value it provides.
Beast Mastery Hunter Health Potions
The best healing potion for Beast Mastery Hunters is  Silvermoon Health Potion , as this is Healing Potion with the highest amount of healing.
Beast Mastery Hunter Weapon Buff
Beast Mastery Hunters use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil  as their temporary weapon buff in all scenarios.
Beast Mastery Hunter Augment Rune
With Midnight Season 1, the new and best Augment Rune is the  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Beast Mastery Hunter Food
Feasts that give secondary stats (like  Quel'dorei Medley) and feasts that give Agility (like  Silvermoon Parade) are very similar to one another, so you are generally happy with either option while in a group. If you want personal food you can similarly use either  Flora Frenzy or  Royal Roast, but there are more similar alternatives.
