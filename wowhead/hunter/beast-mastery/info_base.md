## ~~~~ Beast Mastery Hunter Overview

Beast Mastery Hunter is a capable spec that uses a combination of different animal companions to ravage its foes, fulfilling a unique and well-defined class fantasy. If you are looking for a highly mobile ranged DPS specialization with a strong, pet-based class identity, then consider giving it a try.

Possibly the biggest attraction towards playing Beast Mastery is the spec's mobility. _Beast Mastery is the only ranged DPS specialization in the game that can deal 100% of its damage while moving, with no restrictions whatsoever_. This provides a considerable benefit in any sort of PvE content, as you can always deal with mechanics or whatever else is going on without wasting any damage - something no other ranged DPS can pull off. ## ~~~~ Best Stats for Beast Mastery Hunter

The stat priority is almost identical between both Hero Trees. For all situations, your top three priorities are **Weapon damage**, **Agility** and **Mastery**, in that order.

In single-target, your next priority is **Haste**, followed by **Critical Strike** and finally **Versatility** for both Hero Trees.

In AoE, your next priority is **Critical Strike**, followed by **Haste** and **Versatility** at a similar level if playing Dark Ranger, but with **Versatility** a bit ahead of **Haste** if playing Pack Leader.

## Beast Mastery Hunter Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Beats Mastery Hunter generates most of its resources passively, either through passive focus regeneration or through your abilities coming off cooldown. Your goal is to spend your focus wisely while optimizing the cooldowns of your abilities effectively. The current design of the spec is also heavily based around your main cooldown, so optimizing the amount of powerful abilities in your cooldown window is important.
