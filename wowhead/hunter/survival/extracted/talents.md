 Further Details

Pack Leader builds revolve around  Howl of the Pack Leader and the beasts it will spawn in combat, granting various positive effects. Pack Leader favours buttons such as  Raptor Swipe and effects like  Strike as One paired with  Two Against Many to deal its AoE, alongside  Wildfire Bomb to round out the rotation. Because of the focus on the former effects, the AoE (soft)cap is on the low side.

Playing Pack Leader with a 2 handed weapon is generally not recommended, but if you do want to give it a try you should swap  Twin Fangs for  Flanker's Advantage.

 Further Details

Pack Leader builds revolve around  Howl of the Pack Leader and the beasts it will spawn in combat, granting various positive effects. Pack Leader favours buttons such as  Raptor Swipe and effects like  Strike as One paired with  Two Against Many to deal its AoE, alongside  Wildfire Bomb to round out the rotation. Because of the focus on the former effects, the AoE (soft)cap is on the low side.

Playing Pack Leader with a 2 handed weapon is generally not recommended, but if you do want to give it a try you should swap  Twin Fangs for  Flanker's Advantage.
