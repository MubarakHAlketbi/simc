## ~~~~Survival Hunter Overview

Survival is a fast-paced melee spec with an attack or ability to use nearly every second. Your gameplay involves juggling managing your Focus, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossmannoroth_glaivethrust.gif)Tip of the Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=260285/tip-of-the-spear), as well as managing the cooldowns of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_wildfirebomb.gif)Wildfire Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259495/wildfire-bomb) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_musket_04.gif)Boomstick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1261193/boomstick). Your main focus is an overwhelming onslaught of fighting in tandem with your pet with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_raptorstrike.gif)Raptor Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186270/raptor-strike) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_coordinatedassault.gif)Strike as One](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1251717/strike-as-one), while leveraging your adaptability as a Hunter with extra tools. Survival is well-suited for players who enjoy a dynamic gameplay loop, pets, and using every tool at your disposal to survive in Azeroth.

## ~~~~ Baseline Survival Hunter Spells

### Mastery - Spirit Bond

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_beastwithin.gif)Mastery: Spirit Bond](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263135/mastery-spirit-bond) \- Increases the damage you and your pet deal, and additionally reduces the damage you and your pet take by 3%. The damage reduction does not scale with Mastery. If you are within 25 yards of your pet, the values of both of these are doubled. There is a 10 second mercy window - If, for whatever reason, you are far away from your pet, you have 10 seconds to get near them before the doubled bonuses are reduced to normal values. Once you're near your pet, it's an immediate double of the Mastery value as well. Something to keep in mind is that the game checks for your pet being far away at a very slow interval, around three seconds or so.


### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_steadyshot.gif)Steady Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=56641/steady-shot) \- Aside from leveling 1-11, you will never use this ability as Survival and a mandatory talent at level 11 replaces this. This requires a ranged weapon to use.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_hatchettoss.gif)Hatchet Toss](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=193265/hatchet-toss)\- Tags an enemy from far away, dealing a very small amount of damage to them. This is not rotational (with one specific caveat for Pack Leader hero talents).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_impalingbolt.gif)Arcane Shot](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185358/arcane-shot) Aside from leveling 1-12, you will never use this ability as Survival and Hatchet Toss at level 12 replaces this. This requires a ranged weapon to use.


### Offensive Cooldowns

- _None_


### Utility Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_chainsofice.gif)Freezing Trap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187650/freezing-trap) \- ( **Trap**) Freezes any normal enemy that steps over the trap, preventing them from moving or acting for one minute. Damage will break this. This can be used as an interrupt, and can even interrupt casts that usually are non-interruptible if the target can be Crowd Controlled (CC'd)!

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_feint.gif)Disengage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=781/disengage) \- Movement ability that launches you backwards a moderate distance. As an airborne mobility tool, you can use this to negate fall damage if you use it before you hit the ground, or to counter a knock back if you position right.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_feigndeath.gif)Feign Death](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5384/feign-death) \- ( **Feign**) Immediately drops your threat to zero from all targets. It also takes you out of combat, if possible. This often negates any targeted mechanics, and also prevents you from being seen as a valid target for such mechanics while in effect.

## Survival Hunter Rotation in Midnight Season 1

The rotation for Survival Hunter focuses on a streamlined resource management loop. In simplest terms, you'll use as many as possible [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_killcommand.gif)Kill Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259489/kill-command) in any given fight to generate enough [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossmannoroth_glaivethrust.gif)Tip of the Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=260285/tip-of-the-spear) to buff most, if not all, of your other abilities. The important thing to keep in mind is that you don't want to use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_killcommand.gif)Kill Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259489/kill-command) too aggressively, as that means you'll waste the Focus it generates and waste Tip of the Spear stacks.

| ~~~~ | **Resource Management:** By far, the most important core to learn as a Survival Hunter is your resource management. Your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_killcommand.gif)Kill Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259489/kill-command) ability is limited by charges and cooldown, and Kill Command is responsible for generating both your resources: Focus and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossmannoroth_glaivethrust.gif)Tip of the Spear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=260285/tip-of-the-spear). The best way to improve your play is to use Kill Command as much as possible in any given encounter, without over-capping on Focus or Tip of the Spear stacks. In practice, with the optimal talent build, this means you are using two abilities between each use of Kill Command. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Resource Spending:** Tying into the above point, it's very important you're spending your resources in a controlled way, while also maximizing the uses of your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_raptorstrike.gif)Raptor Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=186270/raptor-strike) without idling too much. Due to our Apex node, more Raptor Strike means more [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_hunter_raptorswipe.gif)Raptor Swipe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1259003/raptor-swipe), which means you're missing tons of free damage if you're either spending too much (or too little!) Focus. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Cooldown Management:** Two of your strongest abilities, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_wildfirebomb.gif)Wildfire Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=259495/wildfire-bomb) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_musket_04.gif)Boomstick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1261193/boomstick) are limited by cooldowns. It's important you're keeping these cooldowns in mind, as if you miss a use this can have a cascading effect further down the line and greatly lower your damage over any given encounter, especially in AoE. | |
