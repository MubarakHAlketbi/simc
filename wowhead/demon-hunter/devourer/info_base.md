## ~~~~Devourer Demon Hunter Overview

Devourer is a 25-yard caster spec that uses the destructive powers of the Void to destroy its enemies. Devourer features a build and spender playstyle, building Fury and Soul Fragments with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_consume.gif)Consume](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473662/consume). It spends Fury on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidray.gif)Void Ray](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473728/void-ray), and uses Soul Fragments to access the powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=471306/void-metamorphosis) transformation, granting you increased damage to all your abilities and the ability to cast the powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star). Devourer can also flex into a suite of melee talents such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_voidblade.gif)Voidblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245412/voidblade) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246167/the-hunt). Devourer is well-suited for players who enjoy highly mobile casters, with an emphasis on powerful, fluid cooldowns, or who like to bounce into and out of melee combat.

## Devourer Demon Hunter Hero Talents: Annihilator and Void-Scarred

For Devourer Demon Hunter, our first Hero Talent option is Annihilator, which focuses on calling down void meteors through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidfall.gif)Voidfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256301/voidfall). This tree focuses on generating more resources through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_raiddimensius_brokenworld.gif)World Killer](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256353/world-killer) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_voidscythe_d_01.gif)Celestial Echoes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253415/celestial-echoes), as well as passive Haste bonuses from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_priest_darkarchangel.gif)Swift Erasure](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253668/swift-erasure), which help to speed up the rotation. Conversely, Void-Scarred focuses on the melee hybrid aspects of Devourer, utilizing the entire melee suit of talents ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_voidblade.gif)Voidblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245412/voidblade), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reapersslice.gif)Hungering Slash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239519/hungering-slash), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246167/the-hunt)) to trigger powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_demonsurge.gif)Voidsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246160/voidsurge) and amplify your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis). Void-Scarred also empowers you outside of Void Metamorphosis, granting benefits such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felimmolation.gif)Enduring Torment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452410/enduring-torment) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_demonicpower.gif)Monster Rising](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452414/monster-rising).

Between the two options, in the prepatch Void-Scarred firmly beats out Annihilator in all content due to higher throughput. The two trees are close in Mythic+, but Void-Scarred still wins out due to more powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_eradicate.gif)Eradicate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226033/eradicate). For some quick side-by-side comparisons in a few key areas:

- **Throughput & Damage Profiles:** For single target damage, both hero talent trees are quite close, and you can pick your favourite! Genearlly speaking, Void-Scarred wins out, as its profile is less reliant on ramp than Annihilator. In AOE, the two trees are quite close, and you can pick your favourite. Void-Scarred will generally perform better at lower keystone levels, while Annihilator will perform better at higher keystone levels, but both are quite viable at all levels!

- **Defense:** Unfortunately, both of our Hero trees are weak in this department. Annihilator has [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_blur.gif)Phase Shift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256245/phase-shift), providing a fluctuating 2-8% damage reduction, as well as more Soul Fragment generation, which provides a minor healing boost. Void-Scarred has [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_empowerwards.gif)Set Fire to the Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452406/set-fire-to-the-pain), which is a minor (0.5%) damage reduction as well as a small smoothing of damage intake, or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave2.gif)Improved Soul Rending](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452407/improved-soul-rending), which is a minor increase to Leech. There is very little that separates these trees in this department.

- **Utility:** Annihilator gains access to mobility increases, either through more of your movement cooldown with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_arcane_buff.gif)State of Matter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253402/state-of-matter) or a fluctuating speed increase with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_socererking_arcaneacceleration.gif)Path to Oblivion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253399/path-to-oblivion). Void-Scarred also gains access to a fluctuating movement speed increase with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_felarakkoa_focusedblast.gif)Pursuit of Angriness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452404/pursuit-of-angriness). It also gains access to minor group utility when enemies can be stunned with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felfirenova.gif)Wave of Debilitation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452403/wave-of-debilitation). The bonuses for both trees are mostly minor.

## ~~~~ Playing a Devourer Demon Hunter in Midnight Season 1

Devourer Demon Hunter operates on a dual resource builder spender rotation, building its resources to utilize its powerful cooldowns. As a modern spec, it follows a priority list rather than a strict rotation. You can check how it works in detail in our [rotation guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/demon-hunter/devourer/rotation-cooldowns-pve-dps). Having a priority list means that all actions should be used in an ordered list of highest value from top to bottom, with you needing to judge your current state throughout an encounter with some predictions. It follows 3 key rules:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid resource waste:** Devourer revolves around ramping into powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=471306/void-metamorphosis) windows. It's access is entirely based on your Soul Fragment and Fury generation, so make sure to minimize waste of both! |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Plan Cooldown Windows:** The majority of Devourer damage comes during Void Metamorphosis, making it a very important window to optimize. Planning your windows in advance will allow you to extract maximum value from spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star). |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Be aware of your surroundings:** Devourer is a 25-yard ranged spec, but requires being stationary for some of its strongest spells. Using movement such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_shift.gif)Shift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1234796/shift) proactively to position yourself so your casts won't be interrupted is important. Likewise, if using Void-Scarred, knowing when and where to use abilities such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_voidblade.gif)Voidblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245412/voidblade) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246167/the-hunt) safely is essential. | |

## ~~~~ Gearing Devourer Demon Hunter in Midnight Season 1

As a Devourer Demon Hunter, a general stat priority of **Mastery > Haste > Critical Strike >>> Versatility** is best for both Annihilator and Void-Scarred, although Annihilator does prefer more Haste than Void-Scarred.

**Intellect** remains strong; the value of item level upgrades still tends to pull ahead of perfectly itemized pieces. Look out for trinkets with passive stat procs such as **[![Gaze of the Alnseer](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_trinket_raid_dreamrift_gazeofthealnseer.gif)Gaze of the Alnseer](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249343/gaze-of-the-alnseer?bonus=12806:13335)**, as well as a strong active trinket such as **[![Vaelgor's Final Stare](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_trinket_raid_voidspire_int1_voiddragoneye.gif)Vaelgor's Final Stare](https://www.wowhead.com/item=249346/vaelgors-final-stare?bonus=12806:13335)** to pair with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis). We also always recommended using [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear) to sim your own character, since every character has different circumstances. Check our [Great Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/guide=17592) to learn how to increase the chances of obtaining the bis gear for your Devourer Demon Hunter.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Devourer Demon Hunter?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Devourer Demon Hunter players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Leatherworking](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/leatherworking-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/demon-hunter/devourer/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ What Race is Best for Devourer Demon Hunter?

While there are few racial options for Demon Hunter, there is little difference between the performance benefits granted by different racial attributes. Pick your race based on aesthetic preferences or to go for a specific form of utility that you desire. If you're still intent on min-maxing, there are a few options depending on your goal:

- **Damage & Performance:** Currently, [![Void Elf](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/race_voidelf_male.gif)Void Elf](https://www.wowhead.com/race=29/void-elf) offers the highest damage due to its powerful passive [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_entropicembrace.gif)Entropic Embrace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=255669/entropic-embrace). Both [![Blood Elf](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/race_bloodelf_male.gif)Blood Elf](https://www.wowhead.com/race=10/blood-elf) and [![Night Elf](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/race_nightelf_male.gif)Night Elf](https://www.wowhead.com/race=4/night-elf) are incredibly close however, and offer passive stat bonuses.

- **Mythic+ & Utility:** Night Elf offers situational utility in Mythic Plus with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ambush.gif)Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984/shadowmeld), sometimes enabling some skips or the ability to dodge some mechanics, while not using your combat potion to do so. The other options bring utility as well, with Blood Elf bringing the ability to mass purge enemies due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_teleport.gif)Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=28730/arcane-torrent), and Void Elf bringing the ability to dodge some mechanics and reposition easily with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_spatialrift.gif)Spatial Rift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=256948/spatial-rift). The differences among these options is negligible.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

**Intellect** is your Primary stat, found on leather gear. It increases your Spell Power and damage done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Devourer Demon Hunter gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike**: Chance for attacks and heals to deal double damage. Advanced Interactions

Due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_demonhunter_midnight.gif)Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242486/midnight) causing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221162/collapsing-star) to always critically strike, the stat looses a good deal of value. The talents [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_voidweaverpriest_entropicrift.gif)Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1250088/midnight) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_argus_soulbombdebuffsmall.gif)Calamitous](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242495/calamitous) attempt to remedy this downside by increasing the power of critical strikes, but are not enough to offset the lose of value.



Overall, it's value does increase in M+, where Collapsing Star contributes a much larger percentage of your DPS, but it still lags behind Mastery and Haste. Its a fine stat to have when free stats are given, but is not something we want to stack.

- **Haste**: Increases cast speed, reduces your global cooldown, and reduces the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidray.gif)Void Ray](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473728/void-ray) while inside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis). More Haste helps to increase your time spent inside of Void Metamorphosis, and reduce the time spent outside of it.

- **Mastery**: Increases the your Cosmic damage done. This strength of this effect more than doubles while inside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis).

- **Versatility**: Increases damage and healing, while also reducing damage taken.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

### Void Metamorphosis‎

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways.

Unlike most specializations, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossgorefiend_gorefiendscorruption.gif)Soul Glutton](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247534/soul-glutton)). Due to the talent [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_raidtrinkets_voidprism.gif)Entropy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1261684/entropy), we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis), its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below.

While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:

- Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.

- Your Mastery more than doubles in power

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_consume.gif)Consume](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473662/consume) is upgraded into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_feast.gif)Devour](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217610/devour), dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reap.gif)Reap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226019/reap) by 1 sec per cast

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reap.gif)Reap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226019/reap) is upgraded into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_empoweredreap.gif)Cull](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245453/cull), dealing more damage

It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis

- You gain access to the powerful spell [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star)

### Collapsing Star

While not technically a cooldown in the traditional sense, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) is quite important for Annihilator at all target counts, and for Void-Scarred, depending on chosen build. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) is only accessible inside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis), and requires the collection of 30 Soul Fragments to be able to cast it. This spell deals a high amount of Single Target and AoE damage, and you should aim to cast as many as possible while inside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis).

* * *

A tip to do this is known as Soul Smuggling. Soul Smuggling is the technique of leaving Soul Fragments on the ground when you go into or out of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis). By doing this, you are effectively "smuggling" those souls, allowing them to carry over and increase the speed to which you can reach your next [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis).

* * *

For Annihilator, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) is especially important due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_babyvoidwalker_blue.gif)Otherworldly Focus](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253817/otherworldly-focus) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_cosmicvoid_nova.gif)Dark Matter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256307/dark-matter). As Annihilator, you should aim to cast at least 5 [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) per [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis).

You are looking to cast at least 4 per [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) window.

Aim to use these abilities prior to casting [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving) buffed [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_empoweredreap.gif)Cull](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245453/cull) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star)

## ~~~~ Devourer Demon Hunter Advanced Tips

As a Devourer Demon Hunter, most of your high-level optimizations occur around getting to, and subsequently optimizing, your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) windows. These are where the majority of your damage comes from, and ensuring proper optimization of these is best for increasing your overall. Some advanced tips follow in the tabs below.

### Smuggling

If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is **NOT** related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_soulfragments.gif)Shattered Souls](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227619/shattered-souls)

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reapersslice.gif)Hungering Slash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239519/hungering-slash)

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_eradicate.gif)Eradicate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226033/eradicate) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving)


Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and **why** you'd want to do it!

### Shattered Souls

Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!

### Hungering Slash

This is predominantly done in the M+ Void-Scarred build, but is still quite strong! Simply put Blizzard fixed a bug during the Pre-Patch which allowed us to carry any [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reapersslice.gif)Hungering Slash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239519/hungering-slash) proc we had outside of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) into it, which would then cause it to upgrade into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_glaive_1h_artifactazgalor_d_05dual.gif)Reaper's Toll](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245470/reapers-toll), gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_reversemagic.gif)Mastery: Monster Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238855/mastery-monster-within), further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_glaive_1h_artifactazgalor_d_05dual.gif)Reaper's Toll](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245470/reapers-toll) per Void Metamorphosis, up from 3.

### Eradicate and Moment of Craving

Similar to smuggling [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reapersslice.gif)Hungering Slash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239519/hungering-slash), by smuggling [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_eradicate.gif)Eradicate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226033/eradicate) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving) into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis), we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_empoweredreap.gif)Cull](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245453/cull) gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_reversemagic.gif)Mastery: Monster Within](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238855/mastery-monster-within). By smuggling [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving) into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reap.gif)Reap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226019/reap). Likewise, since [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidray.gif)Void Ray](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473728/void-ray) is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_eradicate.gif)Eradicate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226033/eradicate) works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result.

### Devourer's Bite

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_devourerbite.gif)Devourer's Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240201/devourers-bite) is a very powerful buff window for Devourer Single Target builds, and is very important to optimize to unlock as much of your damage as possible. Simply put, whenever you use a melee ability, you leave a 10s debuff on your target, which increases their damage taken from you by 12%. These stacks can overlap, which means that for Void-Scarred specifically, you're able to stack large damage increases around your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) windows.

Because of this, it's recommended to always hold your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_voidblade.gif)Voidblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245412/voidblade) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246167/the-hunt) untill right before you enter Void Metamorphosis, to get the most out of the upfront damage you gain from entering this form. In this window, you want to try and fit in as much of your damage as possible, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidray.gif)Void Ray](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473728/void-ray), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_voiddh_ability_demonsurge.gif)Voidsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246161/voidsurge), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidfall.gif)Voidfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256302/voidfall), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_empoweredreap.gif)Cull](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245453/cull) buffed with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving).

Since the best builds play [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_cosmicvoid_buff.gif)Emptiness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242492/emptiness) this season, you will often find yourself in Void Metamorphosis long enough to get a second Devourer's Bite window. For these additional windows, try to fit in either a Collapsing Star, or a Moment of Craving buffed Cull, ideally both!

### Reap and Cull Optimization

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_reap.gif)Reap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226019/reap) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_empoweredreap.gif)Cull](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1245453/cull) serve very important roles in Devourer Demon Hunters rotation, working as both fury generators (for Void-Scarred) and as large damage sources. Due to this, I feel its important to break down how they work.

Mechanically, Reap and Cull are identical, and anything that buffs Reap will also buff Cull. The only actual difference between the two is that Cull does 30% more damage baseline, and you also get more of them as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_feast.gif)Devour](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217610/devour) reduces its cooldown by 1s each time it is cast, and you get more resets from more casts of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidray.gif)Void Ray](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=473728/void-ray).

At a baseline, Reap and Cull are on an 8s recharge, and will deal damage and consume up to 4 Soul Fragments you have on the ground. However, they can be augmented in a variety of ways, and this is where the optimizations come in. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_sunder.gif)Soulshaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238739/soulshaper) is the first notable buff, and causes Reap and Cull to do significantly more damage when consuming Soul Fragments. Due to this, its important to ensure you're always consuming the maximum number of Soul Fragments (4 normally, 10 with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_momentofcraving.gif)Moment of Craving](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238488/moment-of-craving)). This means you can wait a few seconds to cast it, as long as you aren't overcapping on charges.

This is especially important for [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_eradicate.gif)Eradicate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1226033/eradicate), as this is a large portion of your damage in AoE and cleave. In these situations it is VERY important you are always casting it at 10 Souls, and never less. Cull in AoE is also a very important source of priority damage, and can be used freely since Void Ray will always provide a charge of Eradicate. In AoE situations where priority is important and you aren't fighting a large group of enemies (generally 8-10+, but the exact number will change on a pull to pull basis and on how important the prio mob is to kill), you should look to use Cull whenever you have 4 or more Soul Fragments available. While this will be slightly less casts of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) overall, it will be a noticeable increase to priority damage, and will help you time your keys faster!

Finally, Annihilator has some small optimizations which can be made with Reap and Cull. The usage of these abilities are much more important as they are used to proc [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidfall.gif)Voidfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256302/voidfall). Because of this, they are not used on cooldown, and are instead used to proc Voidfall once you reach three stacks!

For Devourer Demon Hunters, the Annihilator Hero talent tree features a more streamlined rotation, focusing more on reacting to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_voidfall.gif)Voidfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1256301/voidfall) procs, while introducing no extra buttons to the rotation. The build below is a great place to start from for Devourer Demon Hunter, and will serve well to teach you the basics of the spec while providing solid DPS.

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Devourer Demon Hunter, these tools are fairly lack luster, as they do not use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star). While Assisted Highlight can be a good alternative if you are willing and know when to press your Void Metamorphosis and Collapsing Star binds, Single-Button Assistant should be avoided if possible. The GCD penalty it imposes on your rotation is significant, as it means you will take longer to reach Void Metamorphosis, and spend less time inside of it when you do reach it.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Devourer Demon Hunter Beginners

Devourer mostly desires a large amount of Mastery and Haste, with Critical Strike being an alright third stat. Versatility should be avoided at all costs. Mastery is incredibly powerful, as it more than doubles in strength during [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_112_ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisvoid.gif)Void Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1217607/void-metamorphosis), where all our damage happens. Haste condenses our rotation, speeding up the time to get into Void Metamorphosis, and allowing us to stay in it stronger. Finally, Critical Strike massively increases the damage of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_dh_void_ability_collapsingstar.gif)Collapsing Star](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221167/collapsing-star) due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_demonhunter_midnight.gif)Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242486/midnight) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_voidweaverpriest_entropicrift.gif)Midnight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1250088/midnight).

For more details, check our [Devourer Demon Hunter Stats guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/demon-hunter/devourer/stat-priority-pve-dps).
