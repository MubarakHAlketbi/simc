VOID METAMORPHOSIS‎
 Void Metamorphosis is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways.

Unlike most specializations,  Void Metamorphosis does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent  Soul Glutton). Due to the talent  Entropy, we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below.

While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:
Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.
Your Mastery more than doubles in power
 Consume is upgraded into  Devour, dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of  Reap by 1 sec per cast
 Reap is upgraded into  Cull, dealing more damage
 Void Ray is upgraded, dealing more damage, costing no fury, and pausing your Fury drain while channeling it. It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis
You gain access to the powerful spell  Collapsing Star
While inside of Void Metamorphosis, the following spells will briefly pause your fury drain:
 Void Ray
 Collapsing Star
 Voidblade with the talent  Voidrush
 The Hunt with the talent  Voidrush
 Annihilator resets the cooldown of  Reap, and automatically proc  Voidfall.
 Void-Scarred resets the cooldown  The Hunt and  Voidblade. Additionally, your abilities are further upgraded:
 Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil
 Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll
 The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
Furthermore, the first cast of each melee ability triggers a  Voidsurge
For  Void-Scarred, your goal depends on your build. For the melee build, you look to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis as fast as possible, leveraging the resets of powerful abilities and the high damage of  Voidsurge. For the caster build, you look to extend Void Metamorphosis, making use of  Burning Blades buffed  Cull and the Apex buffed  Collapsing Star.

 Annihilator instead look to extend  Void Metamorphosis, casting as many  Collapsing Star as possible. While it still likes to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis due to the free proc of  Voidfall, it places a lot more value on being inside of  Void Metamorphosis.

SMUGGLING
If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is NOT related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of  Void Metamorphosis where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:
 Shattered Souls
 Hungering Slash
 Eradicate and  Moment of Craving
Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and why you'd want to do it!
SHATTERED SOULS
Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in  Void Metamorphosis while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful  Collapsing Star when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!
HUNGERING SLASH
This is predominantly done in the M+  Void-Scarred build, but is still quite strong! Simply put Blizzard fixed a bug during the Pre-Patch which allowed us to carry any  Hungering Slash proc we had outside of  Void Metamorphosis into it, which would then cause it to upgrade into  Reaper's Toll, gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within, further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged  Reaper's Toll per Void Metamorphosis, up from 3.
ERADICATE AND MOMENT OF CRAVING
Similar to smuggling  Hungering Slash, by smuggling  Eradicate and  Moment of Craving into  Void Metamorphosis, we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that  Cull gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within. By smuggling  Moment of Craving into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a  Reap. Likewise, since  Void Ray is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1.  Eradicate works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result.

#### Pre-Combat Checks
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Devourer Demon Hunter does not have any specialization-specific checks you must complete before combat, as our main utility,  Chaos Brand, is automatically applied once we damage an enemy. Instead, ensure you have all your consumables active! This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Apply Weapon Oils
Eat Food
Apply Flask
Apply Augment Runes
Repair Armour

VOID METAMORPHOSIS‎
 Void Metamorphosis is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways.

Unlike most specializations,  Void Metamorphosis does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent  Soul Glutton). Due to the talent  Entropy, we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below.

While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:
Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.
Your Mastery more than doubles in power
 Consume is upgraded into  Devour, dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of  Reap by 1 sec per cast
 Reap is upgraded into  Cull, dealing more damage
 Void Ray is upgraded, dealing more damage, costing no fury, and pausing your Fury drain while channeling it. It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis
You gain access to the powerful spell  Collapsing Star
While inside of Void Metamorphosis, the following spells will briefly pause your fury drain:
 Void Ray
 Collapsing Star
 Voidblade with the talent  Voidrush
 The Hunt with the talent  Voidrush
 Annihilator resets the cooldown of  Reap, and automatically proc  Voidfall.
 Void-Scarred resets the cooldown  The Hunt and  Voidblade. Additionally, your abilities are further upgraded:
 Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil
 Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll
 The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
Furthermore, the first cast of each melee ability triggers a  Voidsurge
For  Void-Scarred, your goal depends on your build. For the melee build, you look to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis as fast as possible, leveraging the resets of powerful abilities and the high damage of  Voidsurge. For the caster build, you look to extend Void Metamorphosis, making use of  Burning Blades buffed  Cull and the Apex buffed  Collapsing Star.

 Annihilator instead look to extend  Void Metamorphosis, casting as many  Collapsing Star as possible. While it still likes to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis due to the free proc of  Voidfall, it places a lot more value on being inside of  Void Metamorphosis.

SMUGGLING
If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is NOT related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of  Void Metamorphosis where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:
 Shattered Souls
 Hungering Slash
 Eradicate and  Moment of Craving
Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and why you'd want to do it!
SHATTERED SOULS
Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in  Void Metamorphosis while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful  Collapsing Star when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!
HUNGERING SLASH
This is predominantly done in the M+  Void-Scarred build, but is still quite strong! Simply put Blizzard fixed a bug during the Pre-Patch which allowed us to carry any  Hungering Slash proc we had outside of  Void Metamorphosis into it, which would then cause it to upgrade into  Reaper's Toll, gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within, further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged  Reaper's Toll per Void Metamorphosis, up from 3.
ERADICATE AND MOMENT OF CRAVING
Similar to smuggling  Hungering Slash, by smuggling  Eradicate and  Moment of Craving into  Void Metamorphosis, we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that  Cull gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within. By smuggling  Moment of Craving into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a  Reap. Likewise, since  Void Ray is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1.  Eradicate works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result.

Eat Food

Repair Armour

THE MELEE TALENTS
Unique for a caster specialization, Devourer Demon Hunter can choose a package of Melee Talents. These Melee talents form a package in the bottom left of your talent tree, and have special synergy with the  Void-Scarred Hero Tree. The Melee Talents are as follows:
 Voidblade
 Hungering Slash
 Soulforged Blades
 Singular Strikes
 Demonic Instinct
 Voidrush
 Devourer's Bite
 The Hunt
When selected, these talents form what is known as the 'Melee Combo' for Devourer, which is as follows:
After dealing damage with  Voidblade or  The Hunt, your Voidblade is replaced with  Hungering Slash.
 Hungering Slash is an active, which deals Cosmic damage to enemies in 8 yards, generating 10 Fury and 2 Lesser Soul Shards.
After using  Hungering Slash, you gain a free use of  Vengeful Retreat, and your next Vengeful Retreat within 6 seconds triggers  Voidstep, dealing minor Cosmic damage to enemies within 8 yards.
This loop of abilities serves as a core, smaller cooldown loop for Hybrid builds of Devourer, but has extra synergy with the  Void-Scarred tree.
With  Void-Scarred, the melee combo becomes a core part of the rotation, being buffed by multiple different talents. For  Void-Scarred the following bonuses apply to the melee combo:
While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, your  Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil, your  Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll, and your  The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
The first cast of each empowered ability triggers a powerful  Voidsurge
Entering  Void Metamorphosis causes the cooldowns of  The Hunt and  Voidblade to reset with  Violent Transformation
 Burning Blades causes  Voidblade to deal additional damage as a DOT effect
 Flamebound increases the critical strike damage and radius of  Hungering Slash
These effects lead to the melee abilities being essential for  Void-Scarred, as the Hero Tree does not work without them.
For  Void-Scarred and  Annihilator the melee abilities are predominantly used as a damage amp due to the powerful  Devourer's Bite talent. Aim to use these abilities prior to casting  Moment of Craving buffed  Cull or  Collapsing Star

SHATTERED SOULS

Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!

#### Reap and Cull Optimization
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Devourer Demon Hunter does not have any specialization-specific checks you must complete before combat, as our main utility,  Chaos Brand, is automatically applied once we damage an enemy. Instead, ensure you have all your consumables active! This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Apply Weapon Oils
Eat Food
Apply Flask
Apply Augment Runes
Repair Armour

THE MELEE TALENTS
Unique for a caster specialization, Devourer Demon Hunter can choose a package of Melee Talents. These Melee talents form a package in the bottom left of your talent tree, and have special synergy with the  Void-Scarred Hero Tree. The Melee Talents are as follows:
 Voidblade
 Hungering Slash
 Soulforged Blades
 Singular Strikes
 Demonic Instinct
 Voidrush
 Devourer's Bite
 The Hunt
When selected, these talents form what is known as the 'Melee Combo' for Devourer, which is as follows:
After dealing damage with  Voidblade or  The Hunt, your Voidblade is replaced with  Hungering Slash.
 Hungering Slash is an active, which deals Cosmic damage to enemies in 8 yards, generating 10 Fury and 2 Lesser Soul Shards.
After using  Hungering Slash, you gain a free use of  Vengeful Retreat, and your next Vengeful Retreat within 6 seconds triggers  Voidstep, dealing minor Cosmic damage to enemies within 8 yards.
This loop of abilities serves as a core, smaller cooldown loop for Hybrid builds of Devourer, but has extra synergy with the  Void-Scarred tree.
With  Void-Scarred, the melee combo becomes a core part of the rotation, being buffed by multiple different talents. For  Void-Scarred the following bonuses apply to the melee combo:
While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, your  Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil, your  Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll, and your  The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
The first cast of each empowered ability triggers a powerful  Voidsurge
Entering  Void Metamorphosis causes the cooldowns of  The Hunt and  Voidblade to reset with  Violent Transformation
 Burning Blades causes  Voidblade to deal additional damage as a DOT effect
 Flamebound increases the critical strike damage and radius of  Hungering Slash
These effects lead to the melee abilities being essential for  Void-Scarred, as the Hero Tree does not work without them.
For  Void-Scarred and  Annihilator the melee abilities are predominantly used as a damage amp due to the powerful  Devourer's Bite talent. Aim to use these abilities prior to casting  Moment of Craving buffed  Cull or  Collapsing Star

REAP AND CULL OPTIMIZATION
 Reap and  Cull serve very important roles in Devourer Demon Hunters rotation, working as both fury generators (for  Void-Scarred) and as large damage sources. Due to this, I feel its important to break down how they work.

Mechanically, Reap and Cull are identical, and anything that buffs Reap will also buff Cull. The only actual difference between the two is that Cull does 30% more damage baseline, and you also get more of them as  Devour reduces its cooldown by 1s each time it is cast, and you get more resets from more casts of  Void Ray.

At a baseline, Reap and Cull are on an 8s recharge, and will deal damage and consume up to 4 Soul Fragments you have on the ground. However, they can be augmented in a variety of ways, and this is where the optimizations come in.  Soulshaper is the first notable buff, and causes Reap and Cull to do significantly more damage when consuming Soul Fragments. Due to this, its important to ensure you're always consuming the maximum number of Soul Fragments (4 normally, 10 with  Moment of Craving). This means you can wait a few seconds to cast it, as long as you aren't overcapping on charges.

This is especially important for  Eradicate, as this is a large portion of your damage in AoE and cleave. In these situations it is VERY important you are always casting it at 10 Souls, and never less. Cull in AoE is also a very important source of priority damage, and can be used freely since Void Ray will always provide a charge of Eradicate. In AoE situations where priority is important and you aren't fighting a large group of enemies (generally 8-10+, but the exact number will change on a pull to pull basis and on how important the prio mob is to kill), you should look to use Cull whenever you have 4 or more Soul Fragments available. While this will be slightly less casts of  Collapsing Star overall, it will be a noticeable increase to priority damage, and will help you time your keys faster!

Finally,  Annihilator has some small optimizations which can be made with Reap and Cull. The usage of these abilities are much more important as they are used to proc  Voidfall. Because of this, they are not used on cooldown, and are instead used to proc Voidfall once you reach three stacks!

VOID METAMORPHOSIS‎
 Void Metamorphosis is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways.

Unlike most specializations,  Void Metamorphosis does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent  Soul Glutton). Due to the talent  Entropy, we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below.

While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:
Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.
Your Mastery more than doubles in power
 Consume is upgraded into  Devour, dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of  Reap by 1 sec per cast
 Reap is upgraded into  Cull, dealing more damage
 Void Ray is upgraded, dealing more damage, costing no fury, and pausing your Fury drain while channeling it. It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis
You gain access to the powerful spell  Collapsing Star
While inside of Void Metamorphosis, the following spells will briefly pause your fury drain:
 Void Ray
 Collapsing Star
 Voidblade with the talent  Voidrush
 The Hunt with the talent  Voidrush
 Annihilator resets the cooldown of  Reap, and automatically proc  Voidfall.
 Void-Scarred resets the cooldown  The Hunt and  Voidblade. Additionally, your abilities are further upgraded:
 Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil
 Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll
 The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
Furthermore, the first cast of each melee ability triggers a  Voidsurge
For  Void-Scarred, your goal depends on your build. For the melee build, you look to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis as fast as possible, leveraging the resets of powerful abilities and the high damage of  Voidsurge. For the caster build, you look to extend Void Metamorphosis, making use of  Burning Blades buffed  Cull and the Apex buffed  Collapsing Star.

 Annihilator instead look to extend  Void Metamorphosis, casting as many  Collapsing Star as possible. While it still likes to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis due to the free proc of  Voidfall, it places a lot more value on being inside of  Void Metamorphosis.

SMUGGLING
If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is NOT related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of  Void Metamorphosis where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:
 Shattered Souls
 Hungering Slash
 Eradicate and  Moment of Craving
Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and why you'd want to do it!
SHATTERED SOULS
Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in  Void Metamorphosis while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful  Collapsing Star when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!
HUNGERING SLASH
This is predominantly done in the M+  Void-Scarred build, but is still quite strong! Simply put Blizzard fixed a bug during the Pre-Patch which allowed us to carry any  Hungering Slash proc we had outside of  Void Metamorphosis into it, which would then cause it to upgrade into  Reaper's Toll, gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within, further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged  Reaper's Toll per Void Metamorphosis, up from 3.
ERADICATE AND MOMENT OF CRAVING
Similar to smuggling  Hungering Slash, by smuggling  Eradicate and  Moment of Craving into  Void Metamorphosis, we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that  Cull gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within. By smuggling  Moment of Craving into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a  Reap. Likewise, since  Void Ray is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1.  Eradicate works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result.

VOID METAMORPHOSIS‎
 Void Metamorphosis is your main damaging cooldown as a Devourer Demon Hunter, transforming you into a Void Empowered Demon form, and empowering your character in a variety of ways.

Unlike most specializations,  Void Metamorphosis does not have a cooldown or a duration. Instead of a cooldown, it is available when you collect 50 Soul Fragments (or 35 Soul Fragments if you have the talent  Soul Glutton). Due to the talent  Entropy, we start combat with 25 Soul Fragments already collected, so a small period of ramp is needed before we have access to our cooldown. While inside of  Void Metamorphosis, its duration is dependent on your Fury bar. Your Fury bar is instantly refilled when you enter and drains over time, ramping in drain intensity over time. Some of your abilities will briefly pause this drain, which we outline below.

While in Void Metamorphosis, the following benefits apply:
Your Fury bar is refilled, and drains over time, increasing in drain speed the longer you are in Void Metamorphosis. Once your Fury bar reaches 0, you drop out of Void Metamorphosis.
Your Mastery more than doubles in power
 Consume is upgraded into  Devour, dealing more damage, having a shorter cast time, and reducing the cooldown of  Reap by 1 sec per cast
 Reap is upgraded into  Cull, dealing more damage
 Void Ray is upgraded, dealing more damage, costing no fury, and pausing your Fury drain while channeling it. It also gains a 14s hasted cooldown while inside of Void Metamorphosis. This cooldown does not persist outside of Void Metamorphosis
You gain access to the powerful spell  Collapsing Star
While inside of Void Metamorphosis, the following spells will briefly pause your fury drain:
 Void Ray
 Collapsing Star
 Voidblade with the talent  Voidrush
 The Hunt with the talent  Voidrush
 Annihilator resets the cooldown of  Reap, and automatically proc  Voidfall.
 Void-Scarred resets the cooldown  The Hunt and  Voidblade. Additionally, your abilities are further upgraded:
 Voidblade is upgraded into  Pierce the Veil
 Hungering Slash is upgraded into  Reaper's Toll
 The Hunt is upgraded into  Predator's Wake
Furthermore, the first cast of each melee ability triggers a  Voidsurge
For  Void-Scarred, your goal depends on your build. For the melee build, you look to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis as fast as possible, leveraging the resets of powerful abilities and the high damage of  Voidsurge. For the caster build, you look to extend Void Metamorphosis, making use of  Burning Blades buffed  Cull and the Apex buffed  Collapsing Star.

 Annihilator instead look to extend  Void Metamorphosis, casting as many  Collapsing Star as possible. While it still likes to cycle through  Void Metamorphosis due to the free proc of  Voidfall, it places a lot more value on being inside of  Void Metamorphosis.

For  Void-Scarred and  Annihilator the melee abilities are predominantly used as a damage amp due to the powerful  Devourer's Bite talent. Aim to use these abilities prior to casting  Moment of Craving buffed  Cull or  Collapsing Star

SMUGGLING
If you've been around the Devourer Demon Hunter community for any length of time, you may have heard of an action called smuggling. While this is NOT related to real life smuggling (this guide doesn't teach that sorry), what it does describe is the action of taking a proc or resource into or out of  Void Metamorphosis where you otherwise wouldn't be able to. Generally speaking, Smuggling is often done with the following things:
 Shattered Souls
 Hungering Slash
 Eradicate and  Moment of Craving
Let's breakdown what Smuggling refers to in each of these cases, and why you'd want to do it!
SHATTERED SOULS
Soul Fragments serve as the most important resource for Devourer, powering you to access your key cooldown in  Void Metamorphosis while in normal form, or allowing you to cast the powerful  Collapsing Star when already inside of it. Soul Fragments for Devourer exist in two separate states, those collected outside of Void Metamorphosis, and those collected while already inside of it. Importantly, these stacks do not carry over when you change forms, and are wiped each time you enter or leave Void Metamorphosis.

Thankfully, Soul Fragments do not automatically pick up, and we can have 10 lingering on the ground at any given time without collecting them. Because of these, we are able to leave Soul Fragments on the ground when we enter or leave Void Metamorphosis, and then collect them after. This allows us to enter Void Meta with more progress made towards Collapsing Star, or to leave Void Meta and have some progress made to get into our next one faster!
HUNGERING SLASH
This is predominantly done in the M+  Void-Scarred build, but is still quite strong! Simply put Blizzard fixed a bug during the Pre-Patch which allowed us to carry any  Hungering Slash proc we had outside of  Void Metamorphosis into it, which would then cause it to upgrade into  Reaper's Toll, gaining 20% additional damage. On top of that, our damage further increases in Void Metamorphosis due to the second half of our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within, further increasing our damage. This allows us to get 3 supercharged  Reaper's Toll per Void Metamorphosis, up from 3.
ERADICATE AND MOMENT OF CRAVING
Similar to smuggling  Hungering Slash, by smuggling  Eradicate and  Moment of Craving into  Void Metamorphosis, we're able to take advantage of the 30% more damage that  Cull gains, as well as the additional damage we gain from our mastery  Mastery: Monster Within. By smuggling  Moment of Craving into Void Meta, we're able to spend that on a Cull rather than a  Reap. Likewise, since  Void Ray is reset upon entering Void Meta, we're able to use 2 Moment of Craving buffed Culls at the start of our burst, rather than 1.  Eradicate works similarly, upgrading into the "Cull" version when entering Meta, and doing 30% more damage (+damage from our mastery) as a result.

Unique for a caster specialization, Devourer Demon Hunter can choose a package of Melee Talents. These Melee talents form a package in the bottom left of your talent tree, and have special synergy with the  Void-Scarred Hero Tree. The Melee Talents are as follows:

DEVOURER'S BITE
 Devourer's Bite is a very powerful buff window for Devourer Single Target builds, and is very important to optimize to unlock as much of your damage as possible. Simply put, whenever you use a melee ability, you leave a 10s debuff on your target, which increases their damage taken from you by 12%. These stacks can overlap, which means that for  Void-Scarred specifically, you're able to stack large damage increases around your  Void Metamorphosis windows.

Because of this, it's recommended to always hold your  Voidblade and  The Hunt untill right before you enter Void Metamorphosis, to get the most out of the upfront damage you gain from entering this form. In this window, you want to try and fit in as much of your damage as possible, such as  Collapsing Star,  Void Ray,  Voidsurge,  Voidfall, and  Cull buffed with  Moment of Craving.

Since the best builds play  Emptiness this season, you will often find yourself in Void Metamorphosis long enough to get a second Devourer's Bite window. For these additional windows, try to fit in either a Collapsing Star, or a Moment of Craving buffed Cull, ideally both!

REAP AND CULL OPTIMIZATION
 Reap and  Cull serve very important roles in Devourer Demon Hunters rotation, working as both fury generators (for  Void-Scarred) and as large damage sources. Due to this, I feel its important to break down how they work.

Mechanically, Reap and Cull are identical, and anything that buffs Reap will also buff Cull. The only actual difference between the two is that Cull does 30% more damage baseline, and you also get more of them as  Devour reduces its cooldown by 1s each time it is cast, and you get more resets from more casts of  Void Ray.

At a baseline, Reap and Cull are on an 8s recharge, and will deal damage and consume up to 4 Soul Fragments you have on the ground. However, they can be augmented in a variety of ways, and this is where the optimizations come in.  Soulshaper is the first notable buff, and causes Reap and Cull to do significantly more damage when consuming Soul Fragments. Due to this, its important to ensure you're always consuming the maximum number of Soul Fragments (4 normally, 10 with  Moment of Craving). This means you can wait a few seconds to cast it, as long as you aren't overcapping on charges.

This is especially important for  Eradicate, as this is a large portion of your damage in AoE and cleave. In these situations it is VERY important you are always casting it at 10 Souls, and never less. Cull in AoE is also a very important source of priority damage, and can be used freely since Void Ray will always provide a charge of Eradicate. In AoE situations where priority is important and you aren't fighting a large group of enemies (generally 8-10+, but the exact number will change on a pull to pull basis and on how important the prio mob is to kill), you should look to use Cull whenever you have 4 or more Soul Fragments available. While this will be slightly less casts of  Collapsing Star overall, it will be a noticeable increase to priority damage, and will help you time your keys faster!

Finally,  Annihilator has some small optimizations which can be made with Reap and Cull. The usage of these abilities are much more important as they are used to proc  Voidfall. Because of this, they are not used on cooldown, and are instead used to proc Voidfall once you reach three stacks!
