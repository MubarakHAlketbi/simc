 TIER SET BONUSES FOR DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Devourer Demon Hunter gear page!

Devourer Demon Hunter Gear Guide
Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Devourer Demon Hunter
Devourer's first ever tier bonus is a fairly passive bonus, providing damage increases to your two damaging "cooldowns" in  Void Ray and  Collapsing Star. It also provides a minor fury gain on each cast of Collapsing Star, allowing you to stay in  Void Metamorphosis slightly longer! This tier set synergizes quite well with that  Annihilator is trying to do, but unfortunately does not provide much of a benefit to the melee variants of  Void-Scarred.
Devouring Reaver's Sheathe
 Demon Hunter Devourer 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Void Ray damage increased by 10%
 Demon Hunter Devourer 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Collapsing Star damage increased by 10% and it now generates 10 Fury.

Best Tier Set Pieces for Devourer Demon Hunter
Our tier set bonus, Devouring Reaver's Sheathe, is comprised of the following items from Midnight Season 1:
 Devouring Reaver's Intake (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)
 Devouring Reaver's Exhaustplates (Shoulders from Fallen-King Salhadaar - The Voidspire)
 Devouring Reaver's Engine (Chest from Chimaerus - The Dreamrift)

 Devouring Reaver's Pistons (Legs from Vaelgor & Ezzorak - The Voidspire)
Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to their itemization, Devourer Demon Hunter prefer equipping tier pieces on these slots:

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR DEVOURER DEMON HUNTER TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
