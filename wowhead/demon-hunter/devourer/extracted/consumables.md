Devourer Demon Hunter Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond, and then only  Flawless Quick Amethyst  in the other sockets! As always, sim yourself for the most accurate results!.
Devourer Demon Hunter Weapon Enchants
For Season One, Devourer looks to use a mixture of the secondary stat weapon enchantments, one  Enchant Weapon - Arcane Mastery  and  Enchant Weapon - Berserker's Rage , due to how powerful those secondary stats are for us. Early in the patch, you might find higher gains from using the primary stat enchantment,  Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei , but should swap to the secondary stat ones as you gain item level.

Devourer Demon Hunter Flask
The best Flask for Devourer Demon Hunters is  Flask of the Magisters , though  Flask of the Blood Knights  can still be helpful if you feel you want the extra haste to smooth out your gameplay.
Devourer Demon Hunter Combat Potions
While there are many options for potions, Devourer Demon Hunters will always use  Potion of Recklessness .
Devourer Demon Hunter Health Potions
The best healing potion for Devourer Demon Hunters is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Devourer Demon Hunter Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Devourer Demon Hunters will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Devourer Demon Hunter Augment Rune
The best Augment run for Devourer Demon Hunter is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Devourer Demon Hunter Food
In Midnight, Devourer Demon Hunters are looking to use primary stat buffs when possible, as the stamina + primary stat is quite useful, and they give more primary stat relative to the secondary stat feasts. If you are using personal food, instead pivot to a strong secondary stat provider.
Feast:  Silvermoon Parade
Personal Food:  Champion's Bento
