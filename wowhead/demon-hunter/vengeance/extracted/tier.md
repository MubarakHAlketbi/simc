 TIER SET BONUSES FOR VENGEANCE DEMON HUNTER
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Vengeance Demon Hunter
The Midnight Season 1 Tier set bonus simply improves our primary generator,  Fracture. The two piece makes it deal more damage, while the four piece gives it a chance to deal AoE damage on top of the baseline. Due to it simply giving Fracture extra damage, it has no effect whatsoever on our talents or rotation.
Devouring Reaver's Sheathe
 Demon Hunter Vengeance 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Fracture damage increased by 35%.
 Demon Hunter Vengeance 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Fracture has a 30% chance to spark a violent detonation, causing (180% of Attack Power) Fire damage onto nearby enemies. Damage reduced beyond 5 targets. (500ms cooldown)

Best Tier Set Pieces for Vengeance Demon Hunter
Our tier set bonus, Devouring Reaver's Sheathe, is comprised of the following items from Midnight Season 1:
 Devouring Reaver's Intake (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)
 Devouring Reaver's Exhaustplates (Shoulders from Fallen-King Salhadaar - The Voidspire)
 Devouring Reaver's Engine (Chest from Chimaerus - The Dreamrift)
 Devouring Reaver's Essence Grips (Gloves from Vorasius - The Voidspire)
 Devouring Reaver's Pistons (Legs from Vaelgor & Ezzorak - The Voidspire)
Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to their itemization, Vengeance Demon Hunter prefer equipping tier pieces on these slots:

Ideally, you'll have Chest as your off-piece as the tier option is poorly itemized for Vengeance Demon Hunter. While the shoulders are also heavy mastery at a bad ratio, there are more options for chests. This is only a minor optimization; wearing five pieces of the tier is fine if you don't have a high item-level alternative. Fully Best-In-Slot setups will often choose to replace the Chest piece instead. Many options are possible for gearing on Vengeance, and we urge players to simulate themselves for more accurate answers.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR VENGEANCE DEMON HUNTER TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
