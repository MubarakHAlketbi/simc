## ~~~~Vengeance Demon Hunter Overview

Vengeance Demon Hunter is a dual-wielding, leather-wearing Tank specialization with a mix of damage reduction, self-healing, high mobility, and enemy control. While we are not fully based around preventing damage like Protection Warriors, our toolkit does allow us to reduce significant amounts of damage. In addition, we are one of the two specs that can heal based on damage taken, the other being Blood Death Knights.

New Demon Hunters will start at Level 8 rather than Level 1 due to being a Hero Class and are restricted to being either Blood Elf if Horde or Night Elf if Alliance. Vengeance Demon Hunter is a spec that is good for players who enjoy high mobility Tanks with self-sustain.

## ~~~~ Vengeance Demon Hunter Changes in Midnight

With Midnight, WoW's core combat systems have received substantial changes. Similar to most other specs, Vengeance Demon Hunter has received many changes that impact how the class plays as a whole. Some of these changes include the removal of certain abilities or talents, with new ones also being introduced to replace them.

### Removed Abilities

Vengeance lost the following abilities in Midnight:

- **[Demonic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213410)** \- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_feldevastation.gif)Fel Devastation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=212084/fel-devastation) is much more niche now.

- **[The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=370965)** \- We have a bit less mobility for tagging packs.

- **[Illuminated Sigils](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=428557)** \- Significantly reduced power on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_sigilofinquisition.gif)Sigil of Flame](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204596/sigil-of-flame).

- **Fel-Scarred Hero Tree** \- replaced with Annihilator.

## Vengeance Demon Hunter Hero Talents: Annihilator and Aldrachi Reaver

Unlike many other specs, if you are familiar with how baseline Vengeance Demon Hunter plays, then you will find it quite simple to transition into playing with the Hero Talent trees, regardless of which one you choose.

Aldrachi Reaver is the first of the two Hero Talent trees for Vengeance Demon Hunters. Players choosing this tree will be primarily focused on maintaining uptime on short duration buffs and focusing a priority target down. This tree has stronger self-sustain through healing buffs and has a minor effect on your rotation. Whenever you have consumed 20 Souls, your next [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_throwglaive.gif)Throw Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204157/throw-glaive) transforms into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive), which you must use to activate a series of buffs. As a baseline, the first cast of each [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_felsunder.gif)Fracture](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263642/fracture) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave.gif)Soul Cleave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228477/soul-cleave) will do bonus damage, as well as proc a separate bonus effect. For [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_felsunder.gif)Fracture](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263642/fracture), you will apply a debuff onto your target, making it take more damage from all your melee attacks and abilities. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave.gif)Soul Cleave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228477/soul-cleave) will proc bonus AoE damage instead. Depending on the order in which you cast them, these bonus effects and their damage will be doubled. The most important part of the rotation in AoE is to ensure you apply [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_harass.gif)Reaver's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442624/reavers-mark) to the target that will live longest. Maintaining your uptime will be critical with this tree, since there is a feedback loop of spending Souls to generate more, which increases your overall buff uptime.

Annihilator is the other option for Vengeance Demon Hunters. This tree is largely passive, but rewards proper rotational gameplay. The major difference is that upon every cast of Metamorphosis, Spirit Bomb will reset, so with Apex procs you will want to consistently be pressing it immediately after transforming. Due to some bugs in implementation, the optimal rotation currently requires holding some abilities at times to prevent overlaps of meteors.

## ~~~~ Gearing a Vengeance Demon Hunter in Midnight Season 1

Vengeance Demon Hunter gearing is much simpler than one might expect. You can follow a general rule of taking the highest item level item and then only comparing Secondary stats when two pieces of gear have the same item level. ## ~~~~ Best Stats for Vengeance Demon Hunter

You'll want to prioritize pieces with higher Agility (item level is almost always king, with few exceptions). You still want to get pieces that are Haste and Crit-heavy. After that, Versatility is generally preferred. Remember to consider external or temporary sources of stats, such as soulbinds and trinket procs, when trying to understand why a stat might be good or bad for you on your gear. Having a large amount of Critical Strike from an on-use trinket, for example, may reduce the value of the stat for your character.

Vengeance has similar stat priorities in both Raid and Mythic+. Realistically, all stats are roughly equal in strength with Mastery very slightly behind, so aiming for maximum item level while keeping stats relatively balanced is a good idea. Hero talents also affect this slightly, as since Aldrachi Reaver benefits from consuming more Souls, Haste becomes much more important.

In Raid, Haste is our most important defensive stat due to reducing our cooldowns, providing more Active Mitigation uptime.

Critical Strike provides parry defensively, which works well with our naturally high avoidance, and it also provides some healing and offensive power.

Versatility is equally as good as Critical Strike since it applies to all damage and is consistent.

Mastery is marginally last because it makes us more survivable only against damage reduced by armor.

Remember that item level is always the most important!

In Mythic+, Haste is best, followed by Critical Strike, Versatility, and Mastery but they should generally be roughly equal anyway.

We've added a chart below for stat prioritization, but **it must be emphasized that all secondary stats are roughly equal, closer to the difference between .95 and 1 in value rather than .1 and 1**.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

- **Agility**: This is your primary stat, found on all gear except trinkets and rings, and scales extremely well with increased item level. Agility increases your Attack Power and damage done with abilities. It also increases your armor while [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes) is up, and dodge chance.

- **Stamina**: This stat is found on every piece of gear except trinkets, and adds to your health. It is good as a baseline, but generally does not factor into consideration outside of item level.

- **Armor**: This is also found on every piece of gear aside from rings and trinkets. It gives you physical mitigation up to a cap of 85% reduction. However, you cannot currently reach the armor cap. Every point of armor increases your effective health by the same amount, but gives less damage reduction.


### Secondary Stats

For secondary stats, the Vengeance Demon Hunter has the following:

- **Critical Strike**: This increases your chance to critically hit, doubling both damage and healing when that occurs. Critical Strike rating is also converted to Parry rating, increasing your chance to avoid melee hits. There is a hard cap at 100%, but it is currently not achievable.

- **Haste**: This stat increases the proc rate of nearly everything in the game, aside from traits and trinkets that provide stats on proc. It also reduces the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_immolation.gif)Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920/immolation-aura), as well as the recharge rate of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_felsunder.gif)Fracture](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263642/fracture) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes), and reduces your global cooldown by up to a maximum of 100%, from 1.5 seconds down to 0.75 seconds. However, it continues to reduce other cooldowns beyond that, as well as increasing proc rates with no limit, so it does not get worse at any point.

- **Versatility**: This stat provides a general increase to damage and healing done, as well as reducing damage taken at half the rate. It works against all forms of damage, so it is the most effective general purpose stat for damage reduction.

- **Mastery**: This stat increases your Attack Power, as well as the amount of Agility converted to armor during [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes). Since half of the value applies at all times, and half is applied during [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes), it is a fairly mediocre stat at this time, with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes) having such a low uptime, unless running [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicempowerment.gif)Feed the Demon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218612/feed-the-demon), in which case it gains some value, but still not enough to outweigh the value of other stats.


All Secondaries are hard capped at 126% from rating, with diminishing returns of 10% every 10% starting at 30%. This means that at 30-39% you get 10% less, 39-47% you get 20% less, 47-54% you get 30% less, 54-66% you get 40% less, 66-126% you get 50% less, etc.

### Tertiary Stats

These are bonus stats that can sometimes appear on gear. They do not factor into your decisions unless you have two of the same item with different tertiaries. They are nice to have.

- **Leech**: This grants you a portion of your damage and healing, excluding the healing from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment), as healing. It cannot critically hit, and is not affected by Versatility.

- **Speed**: This makes you run a bit faster.

- **Avoidance**: This allows you to take reduced damage from AoE attacks. AoE attacks must be those which specifically deal damage to multiple players immediately, and not through a ticking debuff.

- **Indestructible**: This causes your armor to have no durability, so it doesn't need to be repaired.


All Tertiaries are hard capped at 49% from rating. This follows a similar diminishing returns curve as Secondaries, but at a higher rate. At 0-20% you gain full value, from 20-28% you lose 20%, from 28-34% you lose 40%, from 34-38% you lose 60%, and then from 38-49% you lose 80% of the rating.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

Has 2 charges on a short cooldown.Details ~~~~

This causes your spell to deal more damage and shatter 2 [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment) and generate 25 Fury, which is increased to 3 [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment) and 45 Fury during [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasistank.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187827/metamorphosis). This has 2 charges with a 6 second recharge time reduced by Haste and Talents.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_immolation.gif)Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920/immolation-aura) ( **Immo/IA**) \- Causes damage to nearby targets every second for 6 seconds and generates Fury.Details ~~~~

This is your other primary generator. It deals a burst of damage when cast, generating 8 Fury immediately and having a chance to shatter a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment) with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_volatilefiregreen.gif)Fallout](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=227174/fallout). It then pulses damage every second for 6 seconds, as well as generating 2 Fury on each pulse.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave.gif)Soul Cleave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228477/soul-cleave) ( **Cleave/SC**) \- Consumes 30 Fury to deal damage to 5 targets in front of the caster and heals the Demon Hunter while also consuming 2 nearby Soul Fragments.Details ~~~~

This is your primary spender when not playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_icon_shadowcouncilorb_purple.gif)Spirit Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=247454/spirit-bomb). It deals damage to up to 5 targets (hard-capped) in a melee ranged 180° arc in front of your character, and is the primary method of holding aggro on multiple enemies. When playing with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_icon_shadowcouncilorb_purple.gif)Spirit Bomb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=247454/spirit-bomb), this should never be used if you have souls out, but only afterwards to spend Fury.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_sigilofinquisition.gif)Sigil of Flame](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204596/sigil-of-flame) ( **SoF**) \- A reticle-based ability that detonates after a short time. Causes the enemy to take Fire damage over time. Also grants a stack of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_icon_shadowcouncilorb_purple.gif)Frailty](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=389958/frailty) when talented.Details ~~~~

There is a 2 second delay on the spell after being placed before it activates. It has a 25 second cooldown and is not reduced by Haste. Generates 30 Fury baseline. It pulses every 2 seconds for a total of 8 seconds baseline. Can be upgraded with some talents for shorter cooldown, shorter activation time, longer duration, and to generate a Soul.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_throwglaive.gif)Throw Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204157/throw-glaive) ( **TG**) \- This is a ranged aggro generator with a short cooldown.Details ~~~~

This bounces to 1 additional target for a total of 2 with a 9 second cooldown, and has a bonus threat multiplier causing it to generate 4 times more aggro than other spells. Because of this, despite the low damage, it will generate about 25% more threat on single-target than [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_hatefulstrike.gif)Shear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203783/shear), at the cost of not generating any [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment) or Fury. On AoE it is about equal to a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave.gif)Soul Cleave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228477/soul-cleave) on the targets that it hits. You will primarily use this to pull when there is no risk of accidentally pulling more than intended because of the bounce range, which can be deceptively large, or to help maintain AoE threat when other abilities are on cooldown. Talents can improve this to bounce to 3 targets, have a 3 second cooldown, and have an extra charge with a slow.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_infernalstrike1.gif)Infernal Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=189110/infernal-strike) ( **IS/Leap**) \- Leaps to a location and deals damage to all enemies at the location.Details ~~~~

This is your primary mobility spell. It allows you to leap 30 yards and has 2 charges with a 20 second cooldown. This is reduced to 10 seconds with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_demonhunter_felfirefists.gif)Felfire Fist](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=389724/felfire-fist), or down to 8 seconds with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_archaeology_70_crystallineeyeofundravius.gif)Erratic Felheart](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391397/erratic-felheart?rank=2), and is not reduced by Haste. This ability is not on the GCD, so you should be weaving it while using other spells for extra damage and threat, maintaining one charge for mobility. However, in some cases, you may want to save both charges for mobility to do mechanics.



Make sure you never leap through a pack that you are fighting, as you cannot dodge or parry from behind. The way it is coded, you travel through the pack causing enemies to hit your back. Occasionally, if you leap at the correct angle, you can get past without getting a pack into combat, so watch behind in case your group runs into it.

### Defensive Cooldowns

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720/demon-spikes) ( **DS/Spikes**) \- This ability can hold 2 charges and reduces Physical damage taken for 12 seconds. After level 33, it also grants 8% parry while active.Details ~~~~

This is your primary form of active mitigation. It increases armor by a percentage of your Agility, increased by Mastery. It has no requirements and has a 20 second cooldown, reduced by Haste. By default it has low uptime, but is extremely powerful while active. With [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicempowerment.gif)Feed the Demon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218612/feed-the-demon), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_demonspikes.gif)Extended Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=389721/extended-spikes) and high Haste, the uptime is significantly increased. Avoid overlapping this with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasistank.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187827/metamorphosis) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demonform.gif)Demonic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=321453/demonic) procs when possible, unless playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicempowerment.gif)Feed the Demon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=218612/feed-the-demon) in which case just avoid capping at 2 charges. Talents improve this by reducing cooldown, granting passive damage reduction, and can reflect some damage.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasistank.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187827/metamorphosis) ( **Meta**) \- This is your biggest cooldown.Details ~~~~

## Vengeance Demon Hunter Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Vengeance Demon Hunters operate on a priority list builder-spender system. Depending on the situation, you will prioritize either building or spending one of our two resources while trying to avoid overcapping either of them. You generally want to abide by the following rules:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Generators are key:** While in most cases there is a fairly smooth flow between building and spending, whenever there is a priority conflict, it is more important to keep your generators on cooldown due to procs associated with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_felsunder.gif)Fracture](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=263642/fracture) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219741/soul-fragment) generation for the Hero trees. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Prioritize Spenders by Soul cost:** You should always try to use Soul spenders with as many Souls as possible. This gives the maximum efficiency per global used. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid Overcapping Resources when Possible:** While it is sometimes inevitable due to multiple generators coming off cooldown and priority conflict, try to build and spend in waves to manage resources. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Press Cooldowns as they become Available:** Maximize cooldown efficiency by constantly using abilities as they become available. Procs can get wasted if your abilities are sitting off cooldown for long periods. | |

That means you want to prioritize it more heavily when you are about to proc the third stack.
