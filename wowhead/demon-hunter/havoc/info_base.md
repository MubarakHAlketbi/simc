## ~~~~Havoc Demon Hunter Overview

Havoc Demon Hunter is a fast paced dual-wielding, leather-wearing DPS specialization with a mix of short AoE cooldowns, consistent DPS, and high mobility. Our damage isn't tied to long cooldowns, it is more consistent in relying on shorter, rolling cooldowns with something almost always being available to hit.

As a Hero Class, new Demon Hunters start at Level 8 rather than Level 1. The class is also restricted to only 3 race options, Blood Elf if Horde and your choice of Night Elf or Void Elf if Alliance. Havoc Demon Hunter is a spec that is good for players who enjoy high mobility DPS with great self-sustain.

## Havoc Demon Hunter Hero Talents: Aldrachi Reaver and Fel-Scarred

For Hero Talents, Havoc Demon Hunters have to pick between Aldrachi Reaver and Fel-Scarred.

Aldrachi Reaver Havoc Demon Hunters focus on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive), which replaces [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_throwglaive.gif)Throw Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185123/throw-glaive) for your next cast every time you consume 6 [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_soullink.gif)Shattered Souls](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=178940/shattered-souls). After Reaver's Glaive is cast, your usual rotation ordering of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_chaosstrike.gif)Chaos Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=162794/chaos-strike) is changed, based on prioritizing single-target or AoE damage due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_harass.gif)Reaver's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442679/reavers-mark) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felflamestrike.gif)Fury of the Aldrachi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442718/fury-of-the-aldrachi).

Fel-Scarred, on the other hand, empowers [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis) to deal high AoE damage and causes it to buff additional spells focused on increasing AoE DPS. With [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demoniccirclesummon.gif)Violent Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452409/violent-transformation), there is a small rotational change to get [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_immolation.gif)Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920/immolation-aura) on cooldown before casting [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis).

Between the two options, in Midnight currently **Fel-Scarred** beats out **Aldrachi Reaver** in most situations due to the changes to each hero spec. For some quick side-by-side comparisons in a few key areas:

- **Throughput & Damage Profiles:** **Fel-Scarred** wins out in raw throughput due to positive changes making [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_felscarreddemonhunter_demonsurge.gif)Demonsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452402/demonsurge) scale with our mastery again, while **Aldrachi Reaver** nerfs and changes does less damage it is still incredibly powerful funnel through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_venomouswounds.gif)Wounded Quarry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442806/wounded-quarry) despite [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_glaivetoss.gif)Fury of the Aldrachi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444806/fury-of-the-aldrachi) being soft capped.

- **Defensives:** Both our Hero trees have a strong defensive option available. **Fel-Scarred** has [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_empowerwards.gif)Set Fire to the Pain](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452406/set-fire-to-the-pain) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave2.gif)Improved Soul Rending](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=452407/improved-soul-rending), which provide a small amount of damage stagger to help against one-shots or more leech. **Aldrachi Reaver** has [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_avoidance.gif)Army Unto Oneself](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442714/army-unto-oneself) for active damage reduction, or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nzinsanity_chasedbyshadows.gif)Incorruptible Spirit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442736/incorruptible-spirit) for a decent increase in self-sustain.

## ~~~~ Playing a Havoc Demon Hunter in Midnight Season 1

Havoc Demon Hunter follows a priority list due to high-value cooldowns while juggling Fury as a resource to spend as available. There are some general rules to help, as listed below.

| ~~~~ | **Avoid Fury Waste:** [![Fury](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/fury-17) is our main resource. It is typically spent on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_chaosstrike.gif)Chaos Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=162794/chaos-strike) for single target, while also trying to maintain enough to cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance) on cooldown. The exception to this can be pooling some Fury before [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) comes off cooldown in order to have a large amount for your burst window. |

## ~~~~ Gearing a Havoc Demon Hunter in Midnight Season 1

As a rough guideline, for both **Fel-Scarred** and **Aldrachi Reaver** follow the stat priority of: **Critical Strike > Mastery >> Haste > Versatility**. Don't neglect the value of **Agility**, as a substantial increase in item level is generally better than optimized stats. It is always recommended that you use [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear) to sim your character, since every character has different circumstances.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Havoc Demon Hunter?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Havoc Demon Hunter players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Leatherworking](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/leatherworking-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/demon-hunter/havoc/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ What Race is Best for Havoc Demon Hunter?

Unlike most classes, Demon Hunters are limited to only 3 options. All of these options have utility racials with a passive damage amp.

Horde Demon Hunters can only be Blood Elves while Alliance can pick between Night Elves or Void Elves.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_teleport.gif)Arcane Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202719/arcane-torrent) is typically the weakest option as we already have access to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_misc_zandalari_council_soulswap.gif)Consume Magic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=278326/consume-magic), though there have been niche scenarios for it in the past. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ambush.gif)Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984/shadowmeld) is a strong racial ability in Mythic+, PvP, and open-world content. It can be used to drop aggro or cancel spell casts. Lastly, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_spatialrift.gif)Spatial Rift](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=256948/spatial-rift) for Void Elves can be good for mobility, however this is almost entirely unnecessary for Havoc Demon Hunter due to our high mobility. Still, it is better than nothing.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

**Agility** is your Primary stat, found on leather gear. It increases your Attack/Spell Power and damage/healing done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Havoc Demon Hunter gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike**: Chance for attacks and heals to deal double damage. Advanced Interactions

Critical Strike is very strong for Havoc Demon Hunter due to direct scaling interaction with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyeofleotheras.gif)Know Your Enemy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388118/know-your-enemy?def=117962&rank=2) as a talent, as well as some other options that can be taken like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireballgreen.gif)Ragefire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388107/ragefire). This pushes it to be much better than it appears at a glance due to it increasing it's own value via Critical Strike Damage.

- **Haste**: Increases attack speed, reduces your Global Cooldown, and reduces the cooldown of most rotational abilities.

- **Mastery**: Increases the effectiveness of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_demonicpower.gif)Mastery: Demonic Presence](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185164/mastery-demonic-presence), increasing your Chaos damage dealt and when talented into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_infernalstrike2.gif)Pursuit](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=320654/pursuit) movement speed.

- **Versatility**: Increases damage and healing, while also reducing damage taken.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

### **Aldrachi Reaver** Notes

- **Aldrachi Reaver** in AoE Scenarios (2+ targets) will always use your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive) empowerments on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_chaosstrike.gif)Chaos Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=162794/chaos-strike) first and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance) second. This gives you 12 [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felflamestrike.gif)Fury of the Aldrachi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442718/fury-of-the-aldrachi) slashes, significantly increasing your AoE damage while also doing powerful Priority Target damage through funneling via [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_venomouswounds.gif)Wounded Quarry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442806/wounded-quarry).

* * *

- **Aldrachi Reaver** plays heavily around [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive), however with the changes to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcanetactics.gif)Thrill of the Fight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442686/thrill-of-the-fight) in pre-patch the rotational changes are minimal, as you will have 100% uptime on the buff.

* * *

- At times in the rotation you will overcap [![Fury](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/fury-17), this is **NOT** an issue as long as you are following the priority list. Getting your cooldowns back on cooldown is simply more important than efficient Fury usage.

* * *

- When playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_foundryraid_demolition.gif)Exergy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206476/exergy), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_vengefulretreat2.gif)Vengeful Retreat](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198793/vengeful-retreat) can be held for a few seconds to line up your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_surpriseattack.gif)Initiative](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388108/initiative) buff to a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felflamestrike.gif)Fury of the Aldrachi](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442718/fury-of-the-aldrachi) as long as you will not drop your Exergy buff. Exergy can stack up to 30 seconds of duration, so every [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_demonhunter_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=370965/the-hunt) cast will give you leeway to maintain Exergy.

* * *

- When [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_surpriseattack.gif)Initiative](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388108/initiative) is your only 'movement' talent, you will use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_vengefulretreat2.gif)Vengeful Retreat](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198793/vengeful-retreat) to get [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_surpriseattack.gif)Initiative](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388108/initiative) for your empowered [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance). You want to cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_vengefulretreat2.gif)Vengeful Retreat](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198793/vengeful-retreat) a GCD or two **BEFORE** [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance) is ready to be pressed, to better position and have the [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_surpriseattack.gif)Initiative](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388108/initiative) buff in advance.

* * *

- If there are add spawns, you should hold [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive) to empower [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance). This will funnel significant damage into your priority target through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_venomouswounds.gif)Wounded Quarry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442806/wounded-quarry). The more targets you can hit, the more efficient your glaives will be.

* * *

- Your goal should be to maintain your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_harass.gif)Reaver's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442679/reavers-mark) debuff on your primary target at all times, the stack count is only a 6% damage buff per stack it will not multiply the damage of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_venomouswounds.gif)Wounded Quarry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442806/wounded-quarry). You cannot have [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_harass.gif)Reaver's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442679/reavers-mark) on multiple targets, it will replace any other mark when you swap targets.

### **Fel-Scarred** Notes

- The **Fel-Scarred** opener focuses on quickly entering [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis) as quickly as possible, getting all your important cooldowns on cooldown.

* * *

- As a significant portion of your **Fel-Scarred** burst happens prior to casting [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis), **NEVER** macro On-Use Trinkets and Combat Potions directly into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis). They must have their own dedicated keybind to be pressed earlier in the opener to not miss half of your damage window.

* * *

- The AoE Opener for **Fel-Scarred** uses a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_bladedance.gif)Blade Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=188499/blade-dance) to apply [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_felhellfire.gif)Burning Wound](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391189/burning-wound) from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_magtheridonshead.gif)Screaming Brutality](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1220506/screaming-brutality). This is due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_immolation.gif)Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920/immolation-aura) being such a significant amount of your AoE damage, and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_throwglaive.gif)Throw Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185123/throw-glaive) is not used rotationally.

## ~~~~ Havoc Demon Hunter Major Cooldowns

For Havoc Demon Hunter, their cooldowns all interact with one another and have some specific utilization and ordering to use correctly as talked about below.

Metamorphosis

Eye Beam

Essence Break

The Hunt

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis) is not simply a press and go button due to the talent [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_glide.gif)Chaotic Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388112/chaotic-transformation), which resets the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_glaive_1h_artifactaldrochi_d_02dual.gif)Death Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210152/death-sweep). This means that a large portion of your burst damage will happen before [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis) is cast, making it **extremely bad** to macro trinkets directly into Meta. This also gives you a specific rotation to setup before casting, as the openers show.

Due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demonform.gif)Demonic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213410/demonic), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) provides a burst window where you are temporarily in Demon Form to make use of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_glaive_1h_npc_d_02.gif)Annihilation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=201427/annihilation) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_glaive_1h_artifactaldrochi_d_02dual.gif)Death Sweep](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=210152/death-sweep). This typically means preparing before the window to get [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_10_inscription3_pigments_black.gif)Inertia](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=427640/inertia) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_surpriseattack.gif)Initiative](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=388108/initiative), up as well as using [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_ritualofsacrifice.gif)Essence Break](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258860/essence-break) (if talented) after, to stack all the damage amplification effects possible.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_ritualofsacrifice.gif)Essence Break](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258860/essence-break) is cast when already in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_metamorphasisdps.gif)Metamorphosis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191427/metamorphosis) form, whether from the cooldown cast or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demonform.gif)Demonic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213410/demonic) proccing from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) as the extra haste will allow for an extra GCD in the window. As Essence Break does a flat damage amount and works on AoE through Death Sweep, you will always use it after [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_eyebeam.gif)Eye Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=198013/eye-beam) to double Death Sweep in AoE.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_demonhunter_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=370965/the-hunt) usage depends on your Hero Talent, for **Fel-Scarred** there is no talent interaction with your kit and it is simply pressed on cooldown for the damage it deals. **Aldrachi Reaver** has an important interaction with The Hunt, after the cast succeeds it will generate a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_aldrachireaverdemonhunter_reaversglaive.gif)Reaver's Glaive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=442294/reavers-glaive) if you do not already have one. This means you must ALWAYS use a generated Reaver's Glaive in hand before casting The Hunt when it comes up, however you still will be looking to cast The Hunt on cooldown. The one caveat is due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_demonhunter_eternalhunt.gif)Eternal Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1270898/eternal-hunt) only lasting 12 seconds, you should wait for Eye Beam to be up within 10 seconds before casting.

### Havoc Demon Hunter Flask

The best Flask for Havoc Demon Hunter is [![Flask of the Shattered Sun](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_flask_sindoreipotion_red--.gif)Flask of the Shattered Sun![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241326/flask-of-the-shattered-sun), though this depends on your precise gear and [![Flask of the Magisters](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_flask_sindoreipotion_black.gif)Flask of the Magisters![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241322/flask-of-the-magisters) can be used if you need Mastery more than Critical Strike.

### Havoc Demon Hunter Combat Potions

Currently for Havoc Demon Hunter your best combat potion is [![Potion of Recklessness](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_voidpotion_red.gif)Potion of Recklessness![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241288/potion-of-recklessness) in best gear. This provides an INCREDIBLE amount of stats and is strongest if put towards Critical Strike. [![Light's Potential](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_lightpotion_yellow.gif)Light's Potential![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241308/lights-potential) is generally good, and will be better earlier in the tier on lower item level.

### Havoc Demon Hunter Health Potions

The best healing potion for Havoc Demon Hunter is [![Silvermoon Health Potion](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_lightpotion_orange.gif)Silvermoon Health Potion![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241304/silvermoon-health-potion).

### Havoc Demon Hunter Weapon Buff

For their weapons, Havoc Demon Hunters will always use [![Thalassian Phoenix Oil](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_enchanting_manaoil_red.gif)Thalassian Phoenix Oil![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=243734/thalassian-phoenix-oil).

### Havoc Demon Hunter Augment Rune

With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is [![Void-Touched Augment Rune](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_10_enchanting_crystal_color2.gif)Void-Touched Augment Rune](https://www.wowhead.com/item=259085/void-touched-augment-rune). While these provide a DPS increase, they are often expensive and considered optional.

The Feast [![Hearty Harandar Celebration](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_1h_soup_b_01_misc_1h_soup_b_01.gif)Hearty Harandar Celebration](https://www.wowhead.com/item=266996/hearty-harandar-celebration) is preferred as it also gives Stamina.

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Havoc Demon Hunter, these tools are typically of very low value. Many core rotational buttons such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_felblade.gif)Felblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213241/felblade) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_demonhunter_thehunt.gif)The Hunt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=370965/the-hunt) are never pressed or recommended. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_immolation.gif)Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920/immolation-aura) is ALSO not used in Single Target, this means both active generators for Havoc go entirely unused.
