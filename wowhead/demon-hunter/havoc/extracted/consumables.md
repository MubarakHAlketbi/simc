Havoc Demon Hunter Gems
Havoc Demon Hunter will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as their single Unique Eversong Diamond, and fill all remaining sockets with  Flawless Masterful Garnet .

Havoc Demon Hunter Flask
The best Flask for Havoc Demon Hunter is  Flask of the Shattered Sun , though this depends on your precise gear and  Flask of the Magisters  can be used if you need Mastery more than Critical Strike.
Havoc Demon Hunter Combat Potions
Currently for Havoc Demon Hunter your best combat potion is  Potion of Recklessness  in best gear. This provides an INCREDIBLE amount of stats and is strongest if put towards Critical Strike.  Light's Potential  is generally good, and will be better earlier in the tier on lower item level.
Havoc Demon Hunter Health Potions
The best healing potion for Havoc Demon Hunter is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Havoc Demon Hunter Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Havoc Demon Hunters will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Havoc Demon Hunter Augment Rune
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune. While these provide a DPS increase, they are often expensive and considered optional.
Havoc Demon Hunter Food
In Midnight, Havoc Demon Hunters are going to start out with Primary Stat food as it will provide more damage in the first tier before Secondary Stats have scaled. The Feast  Hearty Harandar Celebration is preferred as it also gives Stamina.
Feast:  Hearty Harandar Celebration
Personal Food:  Royal Roast /  Hearty Royal Roast
