Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Havoc Demon Hunter
The Season 1 tier set for Havoc Demon Hunter is unfortunately quite weak with very little interaction to our kit. It buffs  Blade Dance and  Death Sweep by a paltry 15% giving a minor DPS increase in single target and almost zero DPS in AoE, while the 4pc gives Haste in  Metamorphosis which has never been needed on Havoc also resulting in a minor increase.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR HAVOC DEMON HUNTER TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
