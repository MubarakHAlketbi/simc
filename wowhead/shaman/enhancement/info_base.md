## ~~~~Enhancement Shaman Overview

Enhancement is a fast-paced melee spec that involves juggling many different abilities and effects and abilities. Their main focus involves using elemental-infused melee strikes like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_shaman_stormstrike.gif)Stormstrike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=17364/stormstrike), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_shaman_lavalash.gif)Lava Lash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=60103/lava-lash), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shaman_crashlightning.gif)Crash Lightning](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187874/crash-lightning) to generate [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shaman_maelstromweapon.gif)Maelstrom Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=187880/maelstrom-weapon) stacks, which are consumed to cast powerful spells. Enhancement is suited to players who enjoy a battlemage playstyle, emphasizing moment-to-moment decisions managing procs, resources, and cooldowns.
