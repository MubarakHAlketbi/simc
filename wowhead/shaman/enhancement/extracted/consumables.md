Enhancement Shaman Gems
It's generally best to sim your character to find the absolute best gems to put in gear. Everyone has access to a minimum of 3, as each piece of jewelry always comes with one socket each. In Midnight, Agility is valued so highly that you should always be using an Eversong Diamond in your first socket. After that, you should mix gems based on gear needs:
Eversong Diamond:  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond .
Other Gems:  Flawless Quick Amethyst  and  Flawless Masterful Peridot 
Enhancement Shaman Weapon Enchant
In Midnight, due to the significantly higher value of Agility,  Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei  is the best choice in the majority of situations. At extremely high gear levels, secondary boosting enchants begin to compete, but aren't best to use early in the season.

Enhancement Shaman Flask
In Midnight, the best general flask to use is  Flask of the Magisters . That said, Flasks are also a great way to re-balance your stats if you're lacking one in your current gearset (especially if switching Hero Trees). Due to that, both  Flask of the Blood Knights  and  Flask of the Shattered Sun  are also useful. You can also obtain these from a  Cauldron of Sin'dorei Flasks  if your group is using one.
Enhancement Shaman Combat Potions
There's a few potions available in Midnight, but currently in Season 1 the strongest is  Light's Potential . These can also be obtained from an  Voidlight Potion Cauldron  if your group is using one.
Enhancement Shaman Health Potions
The best healing potion for Enhancement Shaman is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Enhancement Shaman Weapon Buff
Unfortunately, due to the Weapon Imbues ( Flametongue Weapon and  Windfury Weapon) that Enhancement uses, it CANNOT use weapon enhancements such as oils or whetstones at the same time. Due to the way that  Elemental Weapons works, opting out of these is not an option, so we do not use runes.
Enhancement Shaman Augment Rune
The Augment Rune available in Midnight is  Void-Touched Augment Rune. These can be acquired from the weekly housing quest, but bear in mind they're lost on death and are very expensive!
Enhancement Shaman Food
In Midnight, Agility is back to being extremely valuable for Enhancement Shaman, so feasts such as  Silvermoon Parade are back on the menu. If not, then default to  Royal Roast as personal food.
Feast:  Silvermoon Parade
