ASCENDANCE
 Ascendance and  Deeply Rooted Elements are a choice node capstone, which replace  Doom Winds if selected. When triggered, this transforms you into an air ascendant, converting your  Stormstrike into  Windstrike and auto-attacks into Windlash. Both of these attacks have a 30-yard range, are considered special attacks, and ignore your target's armor. Additionally,  Windstrike gains the benefit of any talent that benefits  Stormstrike, and its cooldown is reduced by 60% while active. Depending on which talent is taken, there's a few different things to know:
 Ascendance - Has a 3-minute cooldown, reduced to 2 minutes with  Thorim's Invocation taken, and lasts for 15 seconds. When activated,  Doom Winds is triggered at the same time for 10 seconds. Finally, whenever you spend  Maelstrom Weapon outside of this form, there's a 0.5% chance per stack to trigger  Doom Winds.
 Deeply Rooted Elements - Provides a 0.6% chance per stack of  Maelstrom Weapon spent to trigger  Ascendance for 8 seconds and  Doom Winds for 10 seconds.
Both these also gain the effects of both  Static Accumulation and  Thorim's Invocation while active, if taken. The former generates 1/2 stacks of  Maelstrom Weapon every second, while the latter gives a way to spend resources through both  Windstrike and  Crash Lightning. We go into further detail on how this works in its respective tab. These talents are always taken in conjunction with this node, and turns the window into a race to cast  Windstrike as much as possible when active.
 STORMBRINGER ASCENDANCE
Due to the heavy  Maelstrom Weapon generation while active,  Stormbringer can often find it struggles to drain it off with  Thorim's Invocation triggers alone. Especially with  Thunder Capacitor and  Supercharge refunds considered, it will often lead to situations where you overcap, but there's no way of draining it faster. In Midnight,  Thorim's Invocation always spends up to 10 stacks with  Overflowing Maelstrom taken, so just cast it on cooldown. There's a couple of quick things to remember:
Single Target - No matter what,  Windstrike is cast on cooldown. Even if  Tempest procs, there's no downside.
AoE - Cast  Crash Lightning if available, and  Windstrike otherwise. Similarly, even if  Tempest procs, don't switch priority.

#### Thorim's Invocation

THORIM'S INVOCATION
In Midnight, we have multiple ways of triggering  Thorim's Invocation - both through  Ascendance like before, and through  Doom Winds. This is an exceptionally powerful effect, allowing us to siphon our  Maelstrom Weapon through other GCDs that generate it, condensing two casts into one. When paired with  Static Accumulation granting generation every second during these windows, it leads to explosive cooldown power, high burst, and an overflow of resources. The basic function is:
Mechanics - Whenever you cast specific abilities, up to 5  Maelstrom Weapon (or up to 10 with  Overflowing Maelstrom) will be consumed to discharge either  Lightning Bolt,  Tempest if active, or  Chain Lightning. This is activated by:
 Doom Winds - Casting  Stormstrike or  Crash Lightning discharges a spender.
 Ascendance - Casting  Windstrike or  Crash Lightning discharges a spender.
The  Maelstrom Weapon spender cast is dictated by a few specific conditions, and setting which ability is used is known as " Thorim's Invocation Priming". This works as follows under these conditions:
 Lightning Bolt /  Chain Lightning Casts - primes  Thorim's Invocation to each respective spell.
 Tempest Casts - Dynamic: If it hits one target, switches to  Lightning Bolt, two or more switches to  Chain Lightning.
 Primordial Storm Casts - Dynamic: primes to  Lightning Bolt or  Chain Lightning depending on targets hit with the preceding strikes.
 Ride the Lightning Procs - Do not influence priming in any way.
 Stormbringer - with  Tempest active, it will always be consumed, regardless of the current priming state.
In Midnight, it's good to know what you're currently primed to, but with the removal of addons it's quite difficult to know for sure. You already want to be in the correct state before you cast these, as fixing it while they're active forces you to use up a valuable GCD on the appropriate spell. #### Sundering
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, make sure as an Enhancement Shaman to have the following done. Forgetting any of these will hurt your performance significnatly, and this applies to Raids, Dungeons, Delves and even Open-World content.
SHAMAN BUFFS
Apply Weapon Imbues -  Lightning Shield applies weapon imbues with  Instinctive Imbuements.
Apply Your Shield -  Lightning Shield will apply  Earth Shield as well with  Elemental Orbit.
Cast Group Buffs - Cast  Skyfury to buff your party with Mastery and extra weapon strikes.
CONSUMABLES CHECK
Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of the Magisters  and Agility food.
No Weapon Oils - never use either Weapon Oils or a Whetstone, they overwrite your weapon imbues!
Have Some Healing - Always have some a  Healthstone and a  Silvermoon Health Potion !
With all of those applied, you're ready to jump straight into combat. Make sure to watch out for your weapon imbues and buffs falling off - without these we lose a significant amount of damage.  Instinctive Imbuements should get everything set up with a single  Lightning Shield cast if you forget!

SUNDERING
In Midnight,  Sundering takes the place on the tree as your mid cooldown burst tool, and inherits the same follow-up talents and bonuses that  Primordial Wave did previously. It comes with a 30-second cooldown (and no longer incapacitates), and deals a large amount of direct Flamestrike damage to targets in a line in front of you. These hits can trigger weapon imbues, including  Windfury Weapon. This is a staple talent in all  Totemic builds due to  Whirling Elements, but also flexes as a high damage burst AoE button.

 Surging Elements causes  Sundering casts to grant 15% Haste for 12 seconds whenever cast, and also generates 5  Maelstrom Weapon instantly. This is often layered over other cooldowns, and the extra resources it grants makes it an excellent setup tool as well. Note - this does not trigger from Splitstream as  Totemic.

Casting  Sundering with  Primordial Storm taken grants a 15-second buff window, replacing the keybind with a powerful 4-step AoE attack. When cast, this consumes (and scales) with all current Maelstrom Stacks, and deals a series of attacks in a fixed order Fire ➜ Frost ➜ Nature ➜  Lightning Bolt /  Chain Lightning. Each of the first three elemental strikes are considered AoE Main-Hand hits (so can trigger  Windfury Weapon separately), and it then selects  Lightning Bolt or  Chain Lightning based on the number of targets hit by the initial strikes. Note - this is not activated by Splitstream as  Totemic.
Depending on the Hero Talent tree taken, the ability serves a variety of functions, but we always aim to use it on cooldown every 30 seconds:
 Stormbringer - makes use of this as a strong front-loaded burst cooldown in AoE situations, using the Haste provided by  Surging Elements to empower  Ascendance. Unfortunately, it struggles to fit it into single target builds.
 Totemic - always takes this due to interactions with  Whirling Elements. The additional Haste it can layer over both  Surging Totem and  Doom Winds consistently makes an already strong combo of cooldowns even more powerful.

When playing with  Primordial Storm, this should always be synced with  Doom Winds casts - it provides 3 individual AoE  Windfury Weapon triggers,
New in Midnight, this also gains the effects of both  Static Accumulation and  Thorim's Invocation while active, if taken. The former generates 1/2 stacks of  Maelstrom Weapon every second, while the latter gives a way to spend resources through both  Stormstrike and  Crash Lightning.  TOTEMIC DOOM WINDS

This, in turn, extends the buff for a significant duration with  Totemic Momentum, and is a key part of the new Midnight rotation. While active, it has a few simple rules:

Additionally,  Windstrike gains the benefit of any talent that benefits  Stormstrike, and its cooldown is reduced by 60% while active. Depending on which talent is taken, there's a few different things to know:
 Ascendance - Has a 3-minute cooldown, reduced to 2 minutes with  Thorim's Invocation taken, and lasts for 15 seconds. When activated,  Doom Winds is triggered at the same time for 10 seconds. Finally, whenever you spend  Maelstrom Weapon outside of this form, there's a 0.5% chance per stack to trigger  Doom Winds.
 Deeply Rooted Elements - Provides a 0.6% chance per stack of  Maelstrom Weapon spent to trigger  Ascendance for 8 seconds and  Doom Winds for 10 seconds.
Both these also gain the effects of both  Static Accumulation and  Thorim's Invocation while active, if taken. The former generates 1/2 stacks of  Maelstrom Weapon every second, while the latter gives a way to spend resources through both  Windstrike and  Crash Lightning. We go into further detail on how this works in its respective tab. These talents are always taken in conjunction with this node, and turns the window into a race to cast  Windstrike as much as possible when active.
 STORMBRINGER ASCENDANCE
Due to the heavy  Maelstrom Weapon generation while active,  Stormbringer can often find it struggles to drain it off with  Thorim's Invocation triggers alone. Especially with  Thunder Capacitor and  Supercharge refunds considered, it will often lead to situations where you overcap, but there's no way of draining it faster. In Midnight,  Thorim's Invocation always spends up to 10 stacks with  Overflowing Maelstrom taken, so just cast it on cooldown. There's a couple of quick things to remember:
Single Target - No matter what,  Windstrike is cast on cooldown. Even if  Tempest procs, there's no downside.
AoE - Cast  Crash Lightning if available, and  Windstrike otherwise. Similarly, even if  Tempest procs, don't switch priority.

#### Thorim's Invocation
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, make sure as an Enhancement Shaman to have the following done. Forgetting any of these will hurt your performance significnatly, and this applies to Raids, Dungeons, Delves and even Open-World content.
SHAMAN BUFFS
Apply Weapon Imbues -  Lightning Shield applies weapon imbues with  Instinctive Imbuements.
Apply Your Shield -  Lightning Shield will apply  Earth Shield as well with  Elemental Orbit.
Cast Group Buffs - Cast  Skyfury to buff your party with Mastery and extra weapon strikes.
CONSUMABLES CHECK
Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of the Magisters  and Agility food.
No Weapon Oils - never use either Weapon Oils or a Whetstone, they overwrite your weapon imbues!
Have Some Healing - Always have some a  Healthstone and a  Silvermoon Health Potion !
With all of those applied, you're ready to jump straight into combat. Make sure to watch out for your weapon imbues and buffs falling off - without these we lose a significant amount of damage.  Instinctive Imbuements should get everything set up with a single  Lightning Shield cast if you forget!

THORIM'S INVOCATION
In Midnight, we have multiple ways of triggering  Thorim's Invocation - both through  Ascendance like before, and through  Doom Winds. This is an exceptionally powerful effect, allowing us to siphon our  Maelstrom Weapon through other GCDs that generate it, condensing two casts into one. When paired with  Static Accumulation granting generation every second during these windows, it leads to explosive cooldown power, high burst, and an overflow of resources. The basic function is:
Mechanics - Whenever you cast specific abilities, up to 5  Maelstrom Weapon (or up to 10 with  Overflowing Maelstrom) will be consumed to discharge either  Lightning Bolt,  Tempest if active, or  Chain Lightning. This is activated by:
 Doom Winds - Casting  Stormstrike or  Crash Lightning discharges a spender.
 Ascendance - Casting  Windstrike or  Crash Lightning discharges a spender.
The  Maelstrom Weapon spender cast is dictated by a few specific conditions, and setting which ability is used is known as " Thorim's Invocation Priming". This works as follows under these conditions:
 Lightning Bolt /  Chain Lightning Casts - primes  Thorim's Invocation to each respective spell.
 Tempest Casts - Dynamic: If it hits one target, switches to  Lightning Bolt, two or more switches to  Chain Lightning.
 Primordial Storm Casts - Dynamic: primes to  Lightning Bolt or  Chain Lightning depending on targets hit with the preceding strikes.
 Ride the Lightning Procs - Do not influence priming in any way.
 Stormbringer - with  Tempest active, it will always be consumed, regardless of the current priming state.
In Midnight, it's good to know what you're currently primed to, but with the removal of addons it's quite difficult to know for sure. You already want to be in the correct state before you cast these, as fixing it while they're active forces you to use up a valuable GCD on the appropriate spell. In most cases, you should naturally be in the correct state through your rotation, and especially in the case of  Stormbringer your opening  Tempest via  Descending Skies will adjust it to the current situation anyway.

#### Sundering

Apply Weapon Imbues -  Lightning Shield applies weapon imbues with  Instinctive Imbuements.
Apply Your Shield -  Lightning Shield will apply  Earth Shield as well with  Elemental Orbit.
Cast Group Buffs - Cast  Skyfury to buff your party with Mastery and extra weapon strikes.

Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of the Magisters  and Agility food.
No Weapon Oils - never use either Weapon Oils or a Whetstone, they overwrite your weapon imbues!
Have Some Healing - Always have some a  Healthstone and a  Silvermoon Health Potion !
With all of those applied, you're ready to jump straight into combat. Make sure to watch out for your weapon imbues and buffs falling off - without these we lose a significant amount of damage.  Instinctive Imbuements should get everything set up with a single  Lightning Shield cast if you forget!

SUNDERING
In Midnight,  Sundering takes the place on the tree as your mid cooldown burst tool, and inherits the same follow-up talents and bonuses that  Primordial Wave did previously. It comes with a 30-second cooldown (and no longer incapacitates), and deals a large amount of direct Flamestrike damage to targets in a line in front of you. These hits can trigger weapon imbues, including  Windfury Weapon. This is a staple talent in all  Totemic builds due to  Whirling Elements, but also flexes as a high damage burst AoE button.

 Surging Elements causes  Sundering casts to grant 15% Haste for 12 seconds whenever cast, and also generates 5  Maelstrom Weapon instantly. This is often layered over other cooldowns, and the extra resources it grants makes it an excellent setup tool as well. Note - this does not trigger from Splitstream as  Totemic.

Casting  Sundering with  Primordial Storm taken grants a 15-second buff window, replacing the keybind with a powerful 4-step AoE attack. When cast, this consumes (and scales) with all current Maelstrom Stacks, and deals a series of attacks in a fixed order Fire ➜ Frost ➜ Nature ➜  Lightning Bolt /  Chain Lightning. Each of the first three elemental strikes are considered AoE Main-Hand hits (so can trigger  Windfury Weapon separately), and it then selects  Lightning Bolt or  Chain Lightning based on the number of targets hit by the initial strikes. Note - this is not activated by Splitstream as  Totemic.
Depending on the Hero Talent tree taken, the ability serves a variety of functions, but we always aim to use it on cooldown every 30 seconds:
 Stormbringer - makes use of this as a strong front-loaded burst cooldown in AoE situations, using the Haste provided by  Surging Elements to empower  Ascendance. Unfortunately, it struggles to fit it into single target builds.
 Totemic - always takes this due to interactions with  Whirling Elements. The additional Haste it can layer over both  Surging Totem and  Doom Winds consistently makes an already strong combo of cooldowns even more powerful.

Now that it's broke free from the shackles of being a maintenance buff, it adds an explosive second layer to your burst windows when playing it. When cast, you'll gain 4 buffs - 3 of which come from  Whirling Elements:
 Whirling Earth - Increases your next  Sundering cast by 100%, and spawns a Searing Totem. This is consumed first.

 Whirling Air - Spending  Maelstrom Weapon triggers  Totemic Rebound 3 times. Consumed last with  Doom Winds +  Crash Lightning.

Executing these sequences to set up for a long  Hot Hand through  Totemic Momentum is key, and gives a lot of breathing room after it ends. This often leads to a buff so long it lasts until your next  Sundering when played correctly, so aim for that.
