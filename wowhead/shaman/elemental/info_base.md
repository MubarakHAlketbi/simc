## ~~~~Elemental Shaman Overview

Elemental Shaman is a mail-wearing, ranged DPS caster with many great spell effects and different flavors of abilities, wielding Nature, Fire, and Frost spells to damage its opponents.

We can use Staves or One-Handed Maces with Shields and, depending on our builds, switch between being very sturdy or very mobile depending on the encounter.

As a class with a healing specialization in Restoration, we excel at off-healing our teammates and, in general, providing various forms of utility with our totems.

Elemental Shaman is a spec to play for people who enjoy playing a caster that can specialize in helping their teammates or just like to look at impressive spell effects.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Elemental Shaman

Stat priorities follow a very simple guideline: Get your Mastery to the first breakpoint at 72%, then split the rest of your secondary stat budget between Critical Strike and Haste. Try to avoid Versatility where possible. Some amount of Versatility is unavoidable because you will find it on tier pieces, so don't sweat if you have it on some random pieces.

The other important guideline is that Mastery becomes pretty much useless after reaching 100%. Utilize all your utility spells, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_ravenform.gif)Nature's Swiftness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=378081/natures-swiftness) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shaman_spiritwalkersgrace.gif)Spiritwalker's Grace](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=79206/spiritwalkers-grace) as much as possible and try to get your group to set up external mobility spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_evoker_timespiral.gif)Time Spiral](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=374968/time-spiral). |
