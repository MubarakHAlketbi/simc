STORMKEEPER
 Stormkeeper is our main short cooldown spell. For  Lightning Bolt it increases the damage of the next 2 casts by 150% and force it to trigger one additional overload. For  Chain Lightning it does not directly increase the damage, but instead triggers an additional overload on every target hit.

The tier set improves this cooldown to instead buff the next 3 casts by an extra 25% up to 175% for Lightning Bold and 25% for Chain Lightning and also giving some Haste whenever you activate the cooldown.

Try to sync this with  Ascendance, which buffs the resulting overloads quite substantially.
In addition to the aformentioned points, for Farseer this cooldown also spawns an Ancestor, significantly increasing the effective damage done by this cooldown.

FARSEER AOE
Farseer relies on the damage down by the Ancestors spawned whenever you press either  Stormkeeper or  Ancestral Swiftness. In general, the Ancestors are duplicating your spell casts in that they cast  Lava Burst whenever you cast a "Single-Target" spell, and  Chain Lightning whenever you cast an "AoE" spell. The classification for "Single Target" and "AoE" is pretty intuitive and would exceed this little tip box, but it is important to note that fundamentally only "actual spell casts" trigger the Ancestors. If you cast a spell such  Lava Burst, your Ancestors will also send a Single Target  Lava Burst, but any overloads on that spell will be ignored by them. The exception to this rule are all spells that trigger  Flame Shock to be applied to enemies. For some reason, these are still reacted to by the Ancestors, triggering a Lava Burst on each target.

So what does this mean rotationally?

Always try to line up your  Ascendance and  Voltaic Blaze right after you cast your Stormkeeper and Ancestral Swiftness, but try to cast the Voltaic Blaze outside of the Ascendance window. In other words your burst window should look like this:

Stormkeeper>>Ancestral Swiftness>>Voltaic Blaze>>Ascendance.

HOW DOES RANDOMNESS WORK FOR ELEMENTAL?
Most, if not all, random procs in the spec rely on a system commonly known as "Deck of Cards". In summary, random procs like  Tempest or  Routine Communication use very strong bad luck protection, forcing a certain amount of successes within a certain amount of "attempts", normalizing proc rates and limiting variance.

Playing around these mechanics and effectively "predicting" random procs during normal gameplay can lead to notable DPS increases but usually requires external tools to simplify tracking of the actual proc chances at any given moment.

With the changes to addons, it has becomes significantly hard to do so, but by no means impossible. While currently there are no addons available that implement this functionality for Elemental, it is only a matter of time.

On a similar note, while to our knowledge  Lava Surge doesn't use this explicit deck system, its procs are also strongly normalized and can effectively be predicted during normal gameplay. This knowledge can be very helpful to achieve very minor DPS gains via  Master of the Elements allowing players that are very comfortable and used to the spec to hold  Maelstrom when they can "feel" a proc coming, and on the other hand, hold a Lava Surge proc for a while, because for the first 3 or so seconds, it is effectively impossible to immediately get another proc and overwrite the existing one.

HOW DO I MINMAX MY DAMAGE WITH PURGING FLAMES?
For a very unintuitive "trick" and tip we can take a look at the functionality of  Purging Flames. This spell makes it so that your next  Lava Burst is "echoed" at up to 5 other targets that are not your main target with an active  Flame Shock. Because the echoes are limited to a number lower than the active Flame Shocks and cant hit your main target, a weird little quirk appears where if you fight a number of enemies and you have less than 6 Flame Shock active, but you have at least one extra target without a Flame Shock, then casting Lava Burst against this target becomes optimal.

As an example, if you are fighting 6 targets and you only hit 5 of those with your  Voltaic Blaze, if you then cast your Lava Burst against a target with Flame Shock, you get your main cast and 4 "echoes", one per other target with a Flame Shock. If you instead cast the Lava Burst against the target without a Flame Shock, you get your main cast which loses its guaranteed crit, and then you get 5 "echoes" instead.

All in all, this is very situational, and should not be played around by most people, but if you are desperate for ways to minmax, this is one of the possible ways to do so.

#### What is RNG?

Before any combat happens, it is important for Elemental Shaman to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

HOW DOES RANDOMNESS WORK FOR ELEMENTAL?
Most, if not all, random procs in the spec rely on a system commonly known as "Deck of Cards". In summary, random procs like  Tempest or  Routine Communication use very strong bad luck protection, forcing a certain amount of successes within a certain amount of "attempts", normalizing proc rates and limiting variance.

Playing around these mechanics and effectively "predicting" random procs during normal gameplay can lead to notable DPS increases but usually requires external tools to simplify tracking of the actual proc chances at any given moment.

With the changes to addons, it has becomes significantly hard to do so, but by no means impossible. While currently there are no addons available that implement this functionality for Elemental, it is only a matter of time.

On a similar note, while to our knowledge  Lava Surge doesn't use this explicit deck system, its procs are also strongly normalized and can effectively be predicted during normal gameplay. This knowledge can be very helpful to achieve very minor DPS gains via  Master of the Elements allowing players that are very comfortable and used to the spec to hold  Maelstrom when they can "feel" a proc coming, and on the other hand, hold a Lava Surge proc for a while, because for the first 3 or so seconds, it is effectively impossible to immediately get another proc and overwrite the existing one.

HOW DO I MINMAX MY DAMAGE WITH PURGING FLAMES?
For a very unintuitive "trick" and tip we can take a look at the functionality of  Purging Flames. This spell makes it so that your next  Lava Burst is "echoed" at up to 5 other targets that are not your main target with an active  Flame Shock. Because the echoes are limited to a number lower than the active Flame Shocks and cant hit your main target, a weird little quirk appears where if you fight a number of enemies and you have less than 6 Flame Shock active, but you have at least one extra target without a Flame Shock, then casting Lava Burst against this target becomes optimal.

As an example, if you are fighting 6 targets and you only hit 5 of those with your  Voltaic Blaze, if you then cast your Lava Burst against a target with Flame Shock, you get your main cast and 4 "echoes", one per other target with a Flame Shock. If you instead cast the Lava Burst against the target without a Flame Shock, you get your main cast which loses its guaranteed crit, and then you get 5 "echoes" instead.

All in all, this is very situational, and should not be played around by most people, but if you are desperate for ways to minmax, this is one of the possible ways to do so.

#### What is RNG?
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Elemental Shaman to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

HOW DOES RANDOMNESS WORK FOR ELEMENTAL?
Most, if not all, random procs in the spec rely on a system commonly known as "Deck of Cards". In summary, random procs like  Tempest or  Routine Communication use very strong bad luck protection, forcing a certain amount of successes within a certain amount of "attempts", normalizing proc rates and limiting variance.

Playing around these mechanics and effectively "predicting" random procs during normal gameplay can lead to notable DPS increases but usually requires external tools to simplify tracking of the actual proc chances at any given moment.

With the changes to addons, it has becomes significantly hard to do so, but by no means impossible. While currently there are no addons available that implement this functionality for Elemental, it is only a matter of time.

On a similar note, while to our knowledge  Lava Surge doesn't use this explicit deck system, its procs are also strongly normalized and can effectively be predicted during normal gameplay. This knowledge can be very helpful to achieve very minor DPS gains via  Master of the Elements allowing players that are very comfortable and used to the spec to hold  Maelstrom when they can "feel" a proc coming, and on the other hand, hold a Lava Surge proc for a while, because for the first 3 or so seconds, it is effectively impossible to immediately get another proc and overwrite the existing one.

HOW DO I MINMAX MY DAMAGE WITH PURGING FLAMES?
For a very unintuitive "trick" and tip we can take a look at the functionality of  Purging Flames. This spell makes it so that your next  Lava Burst is "echoed" at up to 5 other targets that are not your main target with an active  Flame Shock. Because the echoes are limited to a number lower than the active Flame Shocks and cant hit your main target, a weird little quirk appears where if you fight a number of enemies and you have less than 6 Flame Shock active, but you have at least one extra target without a Flame Shock, then casting Lava Burst against this target becomes optimal.

As an example, if you are fighting 6 targets and you only hit 5 of those with your  Voltaic Blaze, if you then cast your Lava Burst against a target with Flame Shock, you get your main cast and 4 "echoes", one per other target with a Flame Shock. If you instead cast the Lava Burst against the target without a Flame Shock, you get your main cast which loses its guaranteed crit, and then you get 5 "echoes" instead.

All in all, this is very situational, and should not be played around by most people, but if you are desperate for ways to minmax, this is one of the possible ways to do so.
