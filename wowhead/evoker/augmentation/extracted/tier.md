 TIER SET BONUSES FOR AUGMENTATION EVOKER
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Augmentation Evoker
Augmentation Evoker's tier set bonuses in Midnight Season 1 feature substantial upgrades to the spec's core damaging spells, while improving uptime on  Ebon Might and Augmentation's new Apex talent,  Duplicate. This tier set does not force any major talent swaps or gameplay changes, instead serving to reinforce the spec's existing priorities.

The 2pc set bonus increases damage dealt by  Eruption, while also increasing the amount of Ebon Might extension provided. Not only does this effect directly improve ebon Might uptime, but this additional extension also applies to  Duplicate, making this 2pc bonus particularly impactful.

The 4pc set bonus increases damage dealt by Empower spells  Fire Breath and  Upheaval while also reducing their cooldowns. This achieves a similar overall result to the 2pc bonus, increasing both personal damage dealt while making Ebon Might and Duplicate extension available more frequently, improving uptime of these two effects. The condition on Ebon Might being active reinforces the goals of the spec, as Empower spells should already only be cast while Ebon Might is active, and maximizing uptime of Ebon Might is essential.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR AUGMENTATION EVOKER TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
