 BEST DEVASTATION EVOKER GEMS AND ENCHANTS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below is a list of the recommended gems and enchants to use on your gear during Midnight Season 1.
Slot	Best Enchant
Weapon	 Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei

Gems	One  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  with a mix of  Flawless Quick Garnet  and  Flawless Masterful Garnet .
Devastation Evoker Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond, and a mix of  Flawless Quick Garnet  and  Flawless Masterful Garnet  in all other sockets.

 Powerful Eversong Diamond  is also very close, but requires a combination of at least 1 Peridot, 1 Lapis, 1 Garnet, and 1 Amethyst.  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  does not require as many gem slots. As always, sim yourself.
Peridot:  Flawless Deadly Peridot 
Amethyst:  Flawless Deadly Amethyst 
Garnet:  Flawless Quick Garnet 
Lapis:  Flawless Deadly Lapis 
Devastation Evoker Weapon Enchant
Early on,  Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei  is going to be your weapon enchant. As the expansion goes on, secondary stat-based weapon enchants will likely pull ahead. Use Raidbots to sim your own character.

Devastation Evoker Flask
The best Flask for Devastation Evokers is  Flask of the Shattered Sun .  Flask of the Magisters  and  Flask of the Blood Knights  can also be good to push your Haste and Mastery above Versatility for  Potion of Recklessness .
Devastation Evoker Combat Potions
When it comes to combat potions, they are both close.  Potion of Recklessness  is best if your stats are good.  Light's Potential  is slightly worse, but does not have any special requirements.
Devastation Evoker Health Potions
The best healing potion for Devastation Evokers is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Devastation Evoker Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Devastation Evokers will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Devastation Evoker Augment Rune
In Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune. These provide a small gain, but are likely to be expensive. Augment runes are not necessary for most players!
Devastation Evoker Food
In Midnight, personal stat food is no better than feast buffs. Intellect food is equivalent to Secondary stats. As always, sim your character to check which food is the best for your configuration of Secondary stats.
Feast:  Harandar Celebration and  Quel'dorei Medley are equal
Personal Food:  Champion's Bento
In addition to food, you can also consume any tea (ex:  Sanguithorn Tea) for a movement speed increase that stacks with your food buff.
