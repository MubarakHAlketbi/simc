For crafted Dev weapons  Vagabond's Bounding Baton created by Inscription is a good alternative as well.

It turned out for me that you can save a lot of gold by crafting the 606 base item first with rank 2 mats and no concentration (the quality does not matter). Then add the enchanted crest and recraft with rank 3 mats and concentration. The difference between rank 2 and rank 3 mats is so big that it is worth here doing two crafts.

Also you might get the base staff from the first craft already with your favorite stats and then you can avoid using a stats scroll for the recraft. All base items having no versa are desirable to save the stats scroll.

The easiest way to get an enchanted crest for 619 early on is to pick up the quest  Our Chance to Strike from Webster and kill Queen Ansurek in Story mode.

As trinket  Shadow-Binding Ritual Knife works out as well. This trinket drops in epic quality from the chest in bountiful delves.

Update for patch 11.1.0:

For Evokers that have the crafted  Vagabond's Bounding Baton from 11.0.x it is the best to wait 2 weeks for the second spark and then upgrade the staff. The staff upgrade costs 7  Codified Greenwood  and 1  Boundless Cipher  + 20 Acuity for the recraft.
