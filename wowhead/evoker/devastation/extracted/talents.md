#### Scalecommander
Scalecommander is built around improved versions of  Deep Breath and  Disintegrate. We take any talents that improve these spells.  Onyx Legacy reduces the cooldown of Deep Breath,  Lay Waste increases the damage, and  Strafing Run allows a second use. Most of the other talents are core to Devastation, regardless of the Hero talent.
