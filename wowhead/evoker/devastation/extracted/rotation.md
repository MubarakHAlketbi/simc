DRAGONRAGE
 Dragonrage is your main cooldown as Devastation. This buff guarantees  Essence Burst procs from  Living Flame and  Azure Strike for the duration. Try to use it every 2 minutes.

Other key takeaways are:
Activating this spell fires 3  Pyres at random targets.
Gain the full effect of  Mastery: Giantkiller with  Tyranny.
 Fire Breath and  Eternity Surge extend the buff with  Animosity.
 Dragonrage can be held for AoE, but it is a fairly long buff. The average duration should be around 32 seconds with proper play.
Apex talents buff your damage during this cooldown ( Rising Fury /  Rising Fury)
Gain a damage buff after  Dragonrage expires with  Rising Fury.

HOVER AND MOVEMENT
 Hover is your most important tool for minimizing damage losses due to movement.  Hover can be cast while casting, and allows you to cast on the move. Subsequent  Hover casts add to the duration, meaning you can use it again while the buff is up without wasting duration. Any time you cancel a cast because you needed to move, it created a small period of downtime. These add up and greatly reduce your damage.

 Glide is also very useful for movement, since using it is much faster than running. Simply tap your spacebar for a boost. Keep in mind, this will not work during normal casts. The trick with  Glide is to triple tap, since canceling it removes the slowfall effect. This makes you travel faster, and hit the ground quicker. Bonus tip: Some ground effects can be avoided if you are at the height of your  Glide.

Another interesting quirk of  Hover is that it allows you to  Glide while channeling  Disintegrate. This is very powerful, since it allows you to quickly reposition by tapping your spacebar. Combine  Hover with  Glide canceling to quickly hop around the battlefield while channeling.

Not all downtime is created equal. During  Dragonrage, stakes are higher. As Scalecommander, getting full value from  Mass Disintegrate is also very important. For these reasons, I recommend always having a  Hover in hand for cooldowns. Scalecommander has a much easier time with  Hover, since each  Deep Breath refunds a charge via  Slipstream. Make sure you make room for this ahead of time, or the value is wasted.

Aside from  Eternity Surge and  Fire Breath, all spells can be cast on the move with  Hover.

#### Disintegrate Chaining and Clipping
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Devastation Evoker to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Blessing of the Bronze to buff you and your allies.
Heal up - Use  Living Flame /  Emerald Blossom /  Verdant Embrace to heal yourself and allies. This will fill your  Scarlet Adaptation buff, if it does not overheal.
Consider Threat (Optional) - Think twice about going full blast if Tanks are inexperienced. You have no way to drop aggro if you pull before Tanks establish threat.

FURY OF THE ASPECTS
 Fury of the Aspects is a group cooldown, which means that when to use it is often a group decision. This spell grants you and your allies the  Bloodlust effect, increasing your Haste. This spell is off the global cooldown.

 Fury of the Aspects applies the  Exhaustion debuff, locking players out of receiving similar effects for 10 minutes.

DISINTEGRATE CHAINING AND CLIPPING
 Disintegrate has 5 ticks with  Azure Celerity and 4 ticks without. If channeling  Disintegrate, you can cast  Disintegrate again after the 4th tick without losing damage. This causes the new  Disintegrate to chain in a similar "Pandemic" fashion and moves remaining duration and final tick of the previous channel to the new one. This is chaining, and you don't lose any value doing this. As a general rule, you always want to avoid downtime by chaining  Disintegrate after the penultimate tick.

As Scalecommander, avoid chaining a  Mass Disintegrate into a normal  Disintegrate as this will cause you to lose a percentage of the buff equal to how many ticks you clipped.

Clipping is when you cut  Disintegrate short by either interrupting it with another spell or Chaining too early. Clipping is best avoided, but can be a small gain under certain circumstances. Currently, clipping non- Mass Disintegrate  Disintegrate one tick early, by interrupting it for Empowers or  Deep Breath, is a very small (0.4%) gain for Scalecommander.

If you mess clipping up the loss will outweigh the gain, so decide for yourself if the juice is worth the squeeze.

#### Deep Breath
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Devastation Evoker to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Blessing of the Bronze to buff you and your allies.
Heal up - Use  Living Flame /  Emerald Blossom /  Verdant Embrace to heal yourself and allies. This will fill your  Scarlet Adaptation buff, if it does not overheal.
Consider Threat (Optional) - Think twice about going full blast if Tanks are inexperienced. You have no way to drop aggro if you pull before Tanks establish threat.

DEEP BREATH
 Deep Breath is another notable cooldown. This spell always gets full  Mastery: Giantkiller value with  Tyranny. Optimal usage depends on your Hero Talents, but it can also be used for movement or to avoid/break crowd control (stuns and roots). Always use  Deep Breath at minimum range unless you need it for movement. It is important to remember that while flying, effects on the ground will still hit you, and you will be locked into the animation. Some class talents modify this ability:
 Imminent Destruction gives you a short buff that decreases Essence costs and increases damage.
 Strafing Run increases damage and allows for a second use.
 Onyx Legacy reduces the cooldown.
 Stretch Time takes any damage taken during the skill and stretches it out, adding some safety.
 Scalecommander uses this spell on cooldown, due to talents like  Maneuverability and  Command Squadron. This version of  Deep Breath can be steered, and you can eject from it.
Cooldown is further reduced by  Bombardments procs via  Wingleader.
Your Z-axis is preserved during this spell, so long as you don't fly uphill. Use height to your advantage if you plan to fly over rocky or dangerous terrain.
 Flameshaper does not typically use  Deep Breath for damage, unless talents like  Imminent Destruction are taken.

HOVER AND MOVEMENT
 Hover is your most important tool for minimizing damage losses due to movement.  Hover can be cast while casting, and allows you to cast on the move. Subsequent  Hover casts add to the duration, meaning you can use it again while the buff is up without wasting duration. Any time you cancel a cast because you needed to move, it created a small period of downtime. These add up and greatly reduce your damage.

 Glide is also very useful for movement, since using it is much faster than running. Simply tap your spacebar for a boost. Keep in mind, this will not work during normal casts. The trick with  Glide is to triple tap, since canceling it removes the slowfall effect. This makes you travel faster, and hit the ground quicker. Bonus tip: Some ground effects can be avoided if you are at the height of your  Glide.

Another interesting quirk of  Hover is that it allows you to  Glide while channeling  Disintegrate. This is very powerful, since it allows you to quickly reposition by tapping your spacebar. Combine  Hover with  Glide canceling to quickly hop around the battlefield while channeling.

Not all downtime is created equal. During  Dragonrage, stakes are higher. As Scalecommander, getting full value from  Mass Disintegrate is also very important. For these reasons, I recommend always having a  Hover in hand for cooldowns. Scalecommander has a much easier time with  Hover, since each  Deep Breath refunds a charge via  Slipstream. Make sure you make room for this ahead of time, or the value is wasted.

Aside from  Eternity Surge and  Fire Breath, all spells can be cast on the move with  Hover.

FURY OF THE ASPECTS

 Fury of the Aspects applies the  Exhaustion debuff, locking players out of receiving similar effects for 10 minutes.

DEEP BREATH Z-AXIS
As Scalecommander,  Deep Breath works a bit differently due to  Maneuverability. One interesting property is that it preserves your Z-axis while you are flying. This can be good or bad, depending on the situation. A common situation you want to avoid is using  Deep Breath uphill. Once you start scraping the ground, even a pebble can stop you in your tracks. Instead, try to gain the high ground before using  Deep Breath.

Another trick is to  Glide to gain altitude before  Deep Breath. This helps greatly on level surfaces, since even a little added height often allows you to fly over obstacles that would normally stop you in your tracks. In some cases, added height also allows you to avoid damage ground mechanics.

 Hover and  Deep Breath can also be used in tandem to cross great distances in the air. You can try this yourself: Start by jump  Glideing from a high place. Use both  Hover charges to dash forward twice, then use  Deep Breath until it expires.  Deep Breath again, then  Hover forward twice using the refunds from  Slipstream.  Time Spiral for one last forward  Hover, then finish by reactivating  Glide. In total, this is around 200 yards of movement with minimal loss of height.

PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Devastation Evoker to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Blessing of the Bronze to buff you and your allies.
Heal up - Use  Living Flame /  Emerald Blossom /  Verdant Embrace to heal yourself and allies. This will fill your  Scarlet Adaptation buff, if it does not overheal.
Consider Threat (Optional) - Think twice about going full blast if Tanks are inexperienced. You have no way to drop aggro if you pull before Tanks establish threat.

FURY OF THE ASPECTS
 Fury of the Aspects is a group cooldown, which means that when to use it is often a group decision. This spell grants you and your allies the  Bloodlust effect, increasing your Haste. This spell is off the global cooldown.

 Fury of the Aspects applies the  Exhaustion debuff, locking players out of receiving similar effects for 10 minutes.

DISINTEGRATE CHAINING AND CLIPPING
 Disintegrate has 5 ticks with  Azure Celerity and 4 ticks without. If channeling  Disintegrate, you can cast  Disintegrate again after the 4th tick without losing damage. This causes the new  Disintegrate to chain in a similar "Pandemic" fashion and moves remaining duration and final tick of the previous channel to the new one. This is chaining, and you don't lose any value doing this. As a general rule, you always want to avoid downtime by chaining  Disintegrate after the penultimate tick.

As Scalecommander, avoid chaining a  Mass Disintegrate into a normal  Disintegrate as this will cause you to lose a percentage of the buff equal to how many ticks you clipped.

Clipping is when you cut  Disintegrate short by either interrupting it with another spell or Chaining too early. Clipping is best avoided, but can be a small gain under certain circumstances. Currently, clipping non- Mass Disintegrate  Disintegrate one tick early, by interrupting it for Empowers or  Deep Breath, is a very small (0.4%) gain for Scalecommander.

If you mess clipping up the loss will outweigh the gain, so decide for yourself if the juice is worth the squeeze.
