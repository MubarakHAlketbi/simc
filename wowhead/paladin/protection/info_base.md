## ~~~~Protection Paladin Overview

As a Protection Paladin, you're the frontline guardian that channels Holy Light to tank Raids and Mythic+. As a warrior of the Light, you have access to an arsenal of blessed hammers, shields and Auras for massive mitigation, self-healing, and group utility.

Protection Paladin is a spec that is good for players who enjoy a classic Tank feel, combined with the ability to self-heal for a considerable amount of damage you take and support your allies through situational spells.

### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_paladin_shieldofvengeance.gif)Shield of the Righteous](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=53600/shield-of-the-righteous) **(SotR)**\- Slams the enemies in front of you with your shield, causing moderate Holy damage and increases your armor based on your Strength stat. Costs 3 Holy power. This should be your primary Holy Power spender.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_righteousfury.gif)Judgment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20271/judgment) **(Judge)**\- Deals moderate Holy damage to your current target. Generates 1 Holy Power.

You can only have one down at a time and recasting it will replace the old one.


### Defensive Cooldowns

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_divineshield.gif)Divine Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=642/divine-shield) **(DS)**\- Renders you immune to all damage, spells, and forced movement effects for 8 seconds. Causes [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_removecurse.gif)Forbearance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=25771/forbearance) and can't be used if you already have [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_removecurse.gif)Forbearance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=25771/forbearance). Use this to avoid damage that might kill you, to remove specific debuffs or in a pre-planned way. Be sure to have cancel macro if you use this while actively tanking.


### Healing Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_helmet_96.gif)Word of Glory](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85673/word-of-glory) **(WoG)** \- Calls down the light to heal you such that the heal amount is increased the lower your health is. Costs 3 Holy Power.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_holy_flashheal.gif)Flash of Light](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=19750/flash-of-light) **(FoL)**\- Quick-casting healing spell. Due to not being able to Block/Parry/Dodge while casting, avoid using this spell while actively tanking. Use this when you or your teammates need a heal during combat downtime.
