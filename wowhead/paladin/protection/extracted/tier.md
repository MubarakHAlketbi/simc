Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Protection Paladin
The Season 1 tier set for Protection Paladin is completely passive and provides a
slight increase to our regular rotational damage by buffing Shield of the Righteous
and Avengers Shield damage.
Luminant Verdicts Vestments
 Paladin Protection 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Shield of the Righteous deals 20% increased damage.
 Paladin Protection 12.0 Class Set 4pc - After casting Shield of the Righteous, your next Avengers Shield deals 5% increased damage. Stacks up to 5.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR PROTECTION PALADIN TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
