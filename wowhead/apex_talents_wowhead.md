# Apex Talents — Wowhead Ground Truth

Source: https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/apex-talents-overview
Extracted: 2026-03-22
Author: iMX3, updated 2026/02/25

Note: All specs use max rank (Rank 4 = all 3 nodes talented). The 3 nodes are:
- 1st Talent (Rank 1) — unlocks at Level 81
- 2nd Talent (Rank 2-3, 2-point node) — unlocks at Level 84
- 3rd Talent (Rank 4, capstone) — unlocks at Level 90

---

## Death Knight

### Blood — Dance of Midnight
- R1: While Dancing Rune Weapon is active, Parrying an attack has a chance to make your next Heart Strike cost no Runes and deal 150% increased damage.
- R2-3: When you consume a Rune you have a chance to call a Dancing Rune Weapon to your aid for 6 sec.
- R4: For each Dancing Rune Weapon active your damage is increased by 0% and your damage taken is reduced by 0%.

### Frost — Chosen of Frostbrood
- R1: Frostwyrm's Fury deals 100% increased damage to the first enemy it hits and grants you 15% Haste for 12 sec.
- R2-3: Frostwyrm's Fury extends the duration of an active Pillar of Frost by 2.0 sec. Enduring Strength grants an additional 4% Strength.
- R4: All Frost damage increased by 10%. Frostwyrm's Fury deals 100% increased damage. After the Frostwyrm flies away, you may cast Frostwyrm's Fury again to recall it at 50% effectiveness.

### Unholy — Forbidden Knowledge
- R1: Army of the Dead siphons the power of the fallen, transforming Death Coil and Epidemic into Necrotic Coil and Graveyard for 15 sec.
- R2-3: Putrefy causes your next 1 Scourge Strike to summon a Lesser Ghoul, and grants you 3% Mastery for 12 sec. Multiple applications may overlap.
- R4: Each Magus of the Dead under your command increases the damage of Necrotic Coil and Graveyard by 8%. Dread Plague has a 20% chance to rouse the dead, causing a Lesser Ghoul to unearth and leap at the target to Putrefy at 60% effectiveness.

## Demon Hunter

### Devourer — Midnight
- R1: Collapsing Star always critically strikes.
- R2-3: All Cosmic damage dealt increased by 3%. Collapsing Star's critical strike damage is increased by 50% of your critical strike chance.
- R4: Entering Void Metamorphosis causes your soul to erupt, generating 5 Soul Fragments and granting immediate access to Collapsing Star.

### Havoc — Eternal Hunt
- R1: The Hunt empowers your next Eye Beam, increasing damage by 100% and affecting enemies in a wider area.
- R2-3: The Hunt has 15 sec reduced cooldown, deals 15% more damage, and its damage over time effect applies to 2 additional enemies.
- R4: Blade Dance damage increased by 20%. Fully channeling Eye Beam causes your next Blade Dance to refresh its own cooldown.

### Vengeance — Untethered Rage
- R1: Soul Cleave and Spirit Bomb have a chance per soul fragment consumed to grant Untethered Rage, allowing Metamorphosis to be cast without incurring its cooldown and lasting 10 sec.
- R2-3: Soul Cleave and Spirit Bomb can consume an additional soul fragment and deal an additional 5% damage for each consumed.
- R4: Each cast of Soul Cleave and Spirit Bomb that does not grant Untethered Rage instead applies a stack of Seething Anger. Seething Anger grants 1% Agility and increases your chance to gain Untethered Rage. Lasts 12 sec and cannot be refreshed after gaining Untethered Rage.

## Druid

### Balance — Ascendant Eclipses
- R1: Activating an Eclipse makes your next Wrath or Starfire instant. The first 3 Starsurges or Starfalls you cast during each Eclipse deal 20% increased damage.
- R2-3: Your critical strikes dealt during an Eclipse cause their victims to languish for an additional 12% of damage dealt for 6 sec.
- R4: Activating an Eclipse also launches Solar or Lunar Bolts at enemies within 40 yds that always critically strike.

### Feral — Unseen Predator
- R1: When you Ferocious Bite, you have a 15% chance per combo point spent to flicker to an enemy within 30 yds and deliver an Unseen Attack.
- R2-3: Unseen Attacks increase your damage done by 0% for 5 sec. Additional applications extend this effect.
- R4: Tiger's Fury causes you to perform an Unseen Attack after your next 2 combo point generating attacks. Rip and Unseen Attack damage increased by 30%.

### Guardian — Wild Guardian
- R1: After Berserk or Incarnation ends, you gain access to Wild Guardian: Spirits of the wild come to your aid, causing your next 2 casts of Ironfur, Maul, and Frenzied Regeneration to be echoed at 50% effectiveness.
- R2-3: Mastery increased by 3%. Maul is always empowered and deals 20% additional Nature damage over 12 sec.
- R4: When you cast Wild Guardian, you gain 2 charges of Dream Guide. Wild Guardian's echoes are now cast at 150% effectiveness, and each echo 2 additional times over 8 sec.

## Evoker

### Augmentation — Duplicate
- R1: Breath of Eons summons a version of you from the future to assist you in battle, lasting 20 sec. Your duplicate casts Eruption, Fire Breath, and Upheaval.
- R2-3: Any time you extend Ebon Might, your duplicate is also extended, equal to 50% of the amount you extended Ebon Might by.
- R4: While your duplicate is active, your Ebon Might grants 100% additional stats and Upheaval and Eruption deal 25% increased damage.

### Devastation — Rising Fury
- R1: While Dragonrage is active you gain Rising Fury every 6 sec, increasing your haste by 4%, stacking up to 5 times.
- R2-3: When Dragonrage ends, gain Risen Fury for 4 sec for each stack of Rising Fury. Risen Fury grants the damage and haste bonuses accumulated from Rising Fury and generates Essence Burst every 4 sec.
- R4: At 5 stacks of Rising Fury, all damage dealt is increased by 8%.

## Hunter

### Beast Mastery — Nature's Ally
- R1: Bestial Wrath summons an Animal Companion from your Stable to fight alongside you for 15 sec. The summoned Animal Companion obeys your Kill Command, Wild Thrash, and Stomps alongside your other pets.
- R2-3: Damage dealt by your pets increased by 5%. Bestial Wrath strikes 2 additional targets.
- R4: Barbed Shot and Cobra Shot damage increased by 15%. Barbed Shot, Cobra Shot, and Black Arrow increases the damage of your next Kill Command by 30%.

### Marksmanship — Take Aim
- R1: Rapid Fire damage increased by 25%. Each shot fired from Rapid Fire reduces the cooldown of Aimed Shot by 0.50 sec.
- R2-3: Aimed Shot's critical strike damage is increased by 25% of your critical strike chance. The damage of your other ranged abilities is increased by 3%.
- R4: Aimed Shot always critically strikes.

### Survival — Raptor Swipe
- R1: (Note: Wowhead page shows "Aimed Shot always critically strikes" which appears to be a copy error from Marksmanship. Actual R1 likely relates to Raptor Swipe mechanics.)
- R2-3: Raptor Strike, Wildfire Bomb, and Raptor Swipe damage increased by 10%. Raptor Swipe deals 25% increased damage to its primary target.
- R4: Raptor Strike now has a 100% chance to upgrade itself to a Raptor Swipe. Raptor Swipes that benefit from Tip of the Spear provoke an additional Strike as One at 300% effectiveness.

## Mage

### Arcane — Touch of the Archmage
- R1: Touch of the Magi increases the damage your target receives from you by 15%.
- R2-3: Arcane Charges further increase the damage of Arcane Blast, Arcane Pulse, and Arcane Barrage by 15%. Arcane Missiles damage increased by 10%.
- R4: When Touch of the Magi explodes, it leaves behind a pulsing rune on its target, dealing 75% of its damage over 6 sec to nearby enemies. Damage is increased by 50% on your initial Touch of the Magi target.

### Fire — Fired Up
- R1: Consuming Hot Streak has a 20% chance to grant you a stack of Fired Up, increasing your Fire damage by 4% for 12 sec. Multiple applications may overlap.
- R2-3: Combustion significantly increases your chance of gaining Fired Up, reducing each time you gain Fired Up. Additionally Fired Up now extends Combustion's duration by 1.0 sec.
- R4: Gaining Fired Up reduces the cooldown of Fire Blast by 2.5 sec. All Fire damage dealt is increased by 3%.

### Frost — Hand of Frost
- R1: Shattering an enemy has a 10% chance to summon a Hand of Frost to pursue your target, dealing Frost damage and applying 1 stack of Freezing on contact.
- R2-3: Each stack of Freezing you Shatter increases your chance to summon a Hand of Frost by an additional 1.0%. Damage from Hand of Frost increases your spell damage by 1% for 8 sec. Multiple applications may overlap.
- R4: Ray of Frost summons 4 Hands of Frost over its duration. Ray of Frost gains an additional charge and its damage is increased by 25%.

## Monk

### Brewmaster — Bring Me Another
- R1: Drinking a Brew has a 20% chance to leave you with an Empty Barrel, which is tossed along with your next Keg Smash to deal Physical damage to your target. Ricochets up to 4 times at 80% effectiveness.
- R2-3: Gaining an Empty Barrel resets the cooldown of Keg Smash and makes its next cast cost 0% less energy. Physical damage dealt increased by 10%.
- R4: When you cast Fortifying Brew or Celestial Brew you tap a fresh keg, granting yourself and 2 nearby allies a refreshing drink, and leaving you with an Empty Barrel.

### Windwalker — Tigereye Brew
- R1: While in combat, you gain Tigereye Brew every 8 sec, causing your critical strike chance to be increased by 2% during your next Zenith, stacking up to 20 times. Generates quickly up to 10 stacks while out of combat.
- R2-3: Your critical strike damage is increased by 5%.
- R4: You have a chance equal to your critical strike to increase the damage of your Fists of Fury by 15% each time it deals damage.

## Paladin

### Protection — Glory of the Vanguard
- R1: Judgment has a chance to grant you Vanguard, causing your next Avenger's Shield to leave an echoing bolt of light in a line between you and the initial target, piercing any enemies for Holy damage.
- R2-3: Judgment deals 10% increased damage. When you consume Vanguard you gain 1 Holy Power.
- R4: Your next cast of Shield of the Righteous after Vanguard deals 50% additional damage to your primary target and causes light to refract onto 4 other targets for Holy damage. During Avenging Wrath your Avenger's Shield always benefits from Vanguard.

### Retribution — Light Within
- R1: Righteous Cause / Art of War increases the damage of your next Blade of Justice by 150%.
- R2-3: Avenging Wrath further increases the damage of Final Verdict / Templar's Verdict and Divine Storm by 10%.
- R4: Righteous Cause / Art of War further empowers Blade of Justice to unleash a wave of Holy energy, dealing Holy damage to its primary target and all other enemies in its path.

## Priest

### Shadow — Void Apparitions
- R1: When an effect from an Idol is activated, conjure Shadowy Apparitions.
- R2-3: Shadowy Apparitions have a 15%/30% chance to spawn as Void Apparitions that release a Void Bolt at their target and deal 50% increased damage.
- R4: Tentacle Slam has a 100% chance to activate an effect from one of your Idols. Idol of Y'Shaarj summons a Shadowfiend. Idol of N'Zoth: target witnesses a nightmare or vision. Idol of Yogg-Saron: summons a Thing From Beyond. Idol of C'Thun: summons a Void Tendril or Void Lasher.

## Rogue

### Assassination — Implacable
- R1: Envenom damage increased by 10%. When your Envenom bonus expires, gain 20% increased Energy generation for 5 sec. Gain an additional 15% per Envenom used while bonus was active, up to 80% total.
- R2-3: Your Nature and Bleed ability damage is increased by 10%.
- R4: After striking with Kingsbane, rapidly strike that target 5 times over 1 sec for Physical and Nature damage. Each strike applies Lethal Poisons and generates a combo point.

### Outlaw — Gravedigger
- R1: Between the Eyes has a 45% chance to apply 2 stacks of its bonus to your damage dealt.
- R2-3: When you spend 5 or more combo points on Dispatch, immediately strike your target with an underhanded Scoundrel Strike for Physical damage.
- R4: When you Dispatch you have a 12% chance per combo point spent to hide a bullet up your sleeve. When you've hidden 6 bullets, your next Between the Eyes consumes them and costs no Energy, generates 6 combo points, and immediately resets its own cooldown.

### Subtlety — Ancient Arts
- R1: Damaging attacks that expend Shadow Techniques to generate combo points have a 15% chance per stack expended to create a Shadow Clone that repeats the attack for 50% of normal damage as Shadow.
- R2-3: Shadow damage increased by 5%. Shadow Clones have a 50% chance to trigger Shadow Techniques' effect.
- R4: After attacks that generate combo points, if 5 or more stacks of Shadow Techniques remain, your next damaging finishing move will expend them to generate combo points up to your max.

## Shaman

### Elemental — Feedback Loop
- R1: Elemental Overload damage increased by 35%.
- R2-3: Spell critical strike chance increased by 5%. Elemental Fury increases spell critical strike damage by an additional 25%.
- R4: Elemental Overloads have a 25% chance to cause an additional Elemental Overload. This effect cannot chain.

### Enhancement — Storm Unleashed
- R1: Each Maelstrom Weapon spent has a 2% chance to cause your next Crash Lightning to ignore its cooldown. The weapon enhancement effect of Crash Lightning may now overlap.
- R2-3: Maelstrom Weapon spending abilities deal 8% increased damage. Weapon Imbuements deal 10% increased damage.
- R4: Crash Lightning electrocutes the area struck, dealing 50% of its damage 2 additional times over 2 sec, and its weapon enhancement effect now increases auto-attack speed by 15%.

## Warlock

### Affliction — Shadow of Nathreza
- R1: Haunt now calls upon a demonic soul from within the Twisting Nether to haunt your target. The soul deals Shadow damage to its host and 3 enemies within 10 yards suffering from Corruption over its duration.
- R2-3: Increases the initial damage of Haunt by 50% and Haunt increases damage dealt to its host by an additional 2%.
- R4: Damage dealt by Haunt has a chance to release the demonic soul haunting its target. When released, it calls down a meteor (Wrath of Nathreza) dealing Shadow damage to all enemies within 10 yds. Damage reduced beyond 8 targets.

### Demonology — (Spell #1264137, name not displayed on Wowhead)
- R1: (Not displayed on Wowhead)
- R2-3: (Not displayed on Wowhead)
- R4: A Soulkeeper stalks you, collecting Demonic Cores as you use them. It has an increasing chance to unleash Willbreaker at your target whenever it collects a Demonic Core. Willbreaker assaults the target's soul with chaos magic, inflicting Chaos damage to it and all enemies within 10 yds every 0.5 sec for 8.5 sec. Damage is reduced beyond 8 targets.

### Destruction — Embers of Nihilam
- R1: Casting Incinerate has a chance to evoke an echo of the Dark Titan's power (Echo of Sargeras). Hurl an ember torn from Nihilam dealing Shadowflame damage and additional Shadowflame damage to all enemies within 10 yds. Damage reduced beyond 8 targets.
- R2-3: Memories of the Titans' destruction flash in your mind when you evoke the Dark Titan's power, increasing critical strike by 4% and haste by 4% for 6 sec.
- R4: Casting Chaos Bolt, Shadowburn, or Rain of Fire evokes an echo of the Dark Titan's power at 50%, 50%, or 60% effectiveness respectively. If no target is found, a random target suffering from Immolate will be selected.

## Warrior

### Arms — Master of Warfare
- R1: Overpower has a chance to upgrade Slam to Heroic Strike. Heroic Strike deals (600% AP) Physical damage and grants a stack of Master of Warfare, allowing attacks to penetrate 3% armor for 30 sec. Multiple stacks may overlap.
- R2-3: Each stack of Master of Warfare increases Mortal Strike, Cleave, and Overpower damage by 2.5% and critical strike damage by 2.5%.
- R4: Heroic Strike reduces the cooldown of Colossus Smash by 2.0 sec.

### Fury — Rampaging Berserker
- R1: Rampage damage increased by 10% and Rampage makes you go Berserk, increasing your Strength by 3% for 8 sec. Multiple applications of Berserk may overlap.
- R2-3: Rampage's Rage cost is reduced by 15 and damage is increased by a further 5% during Recklessness.
- R4: Recklessness grants 3 stacks of Berserk and duration of Recklessness increased by 50%.

### Protection — Phalanx
- R1: Thunder Clap causes your next Shield Slam to unleash a wave of force, dealing Physical damage to all enemies in front of you, and reducing the damage they deal to you by 5% for 8 sec.
- R2-3: Damage dealt by Thunder Clap and Phalanx's wave of force is increased by 10%.
- R4: While Shield Block is active, Shield Slam deals 10% additional damage and has a 20% increased chance to critically strike.
