## ~~~~ Baseline Destruction Warlock Spells

### ~~~~ Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_immolation.gif)Immolate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=348/immolate) **(Immo)** \- A casted dot that deals fire damage and generates 1 soul shard fragment per tick, and has a 50% chance to generate an additional soul shard fragment on critical strikes.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_burnout.gif)Incinerate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=29722/incinerate) \- Our primary filler, and single target soul shard generator that generates 2 soul shard fragments, and an additional 1 on critical strikes.

- (talent) [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_chaosbolt.gif)Chaos Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116858/chaos-bolt) **(CB)**\- Longer cast soul shard spender, that costs 2 soul shards to cast. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_chaosbolt.gif)Chaos Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116858/chaos-bolt) always crits, and has its damage further increased based on your critical strike chance.

- (talent) [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireball.gif)Conflagrate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=17962/conflagrate) \- **(Conflag)** \- Instant cast ability that deals damage and generates 5 soul shard fragments, as well as grants a stack of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_backdraft.gif)Backdraft](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196406/backdraft).


### ~~~~ Offensive Cooldowns

While all of our offensive cooldowns are found in the talent tree, I still thought it relevant to cover them briefly here:

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_summoninfernal.gif)Summon Infernal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1122/summon-infernal) \- Summons an infernal for 30 seconds that deals damage and generates extra soul shard fragments while active.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_baneofhavoc.gif)Havoc](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=80240/havoc) \- Apples a curse to a target that copies all of your single-target abilities onto another target for 60% efficiency. This is also found in the passive proc version [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonbolt.gif)Mayhem](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=387506/mayhem).

## Destruction Warlock Rotation in The War Within

Destruction excels at dealing large hits through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_chaosbolt.gif)Chaos Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116858/chaos-bolt) and very strong cleave damage through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_baneofhavoc.gif)Havoc](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=80240/havoc). Destruction is by nature a relatively simple spec to play, but with a different [![Soul Shards](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_gem_amethyst_02.gif)Soul Shards](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/soul-shards-7) system than Affliction and Demonology, the playstyle is quite different.

When playing Destruction, your rotation includes:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Soul Shard management** Do not overcap Soul Shards. Destruction's Soul Shards resource is different than Affliction and Demonology, as all our shards are split into 10 soul shard fragments. We have great control over generating and spending this resource, so make sure you're aware of having Soul Shards when you need them, while not wasting any Soul Shards by overcapping. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Cooldown management:** Being able to properly manage your cooldowns, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_baneofhavoc.gif)Havoc](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=80240/havoc) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_summoninfernal.gif)Summon Infernal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1122/summon-infernal) will make a big difference in how much damage you're doing. Making sure you have the correct amount of Soul Shards before using these, and making sure you don't lose out on any uses, is a big difference maker between high DPS and low DPS. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Movement:** When you're moving around, you only have very few ways to deal damage. You can play around with always casting abilities such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireball.gif)Conflagrate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=17962/conflagrate) while running, but odds are you're going to lose out on DPS. That means, playing movement segments correctly will be a big difference maker. Utilizing abilities like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demoniccirclesummon.gif)Demonic Circle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48018/demonic-circle) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicportal_green.gif)Demonic Gateway](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=111771/demonic-gateway) properly will help you with that. | |
