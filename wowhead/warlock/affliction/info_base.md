## ~~~~ Affliction Warlock Overview

Affliction Warlock is a cloth-wearing DPS caster that focuses on defeating its enemies with a large selection of Damage over Time abilities (or DoTs), along with nukes that benefit greatly from those DoTs. Affliction has one of the best defensive toolkits in the game and some of the best consistent damage for spread targets among all class specializations.

## ~~~~ Affliction Warlock Strengths and Weaknesses

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strengths** |  | **Weaknesses** |
| - Durable spec with a large degree of survivability.<br>  <br>- High variety of talent options that can suit different group compositions.<br>  <br>- Rich talent tree that can bring strong single target, AoE, or a mix of the two.<br>  <br>- Provides the option for instance skips with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicportal_green.gif)Demonic Gateway](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=111771/demonic-gateway) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soulstone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=231811/soulstone). |  | - No damage immunity.<br>  <br>- Ramp time for DPS can be considered higher than other specs.<br>  <br>- Lack of fast target swapping potential. |
