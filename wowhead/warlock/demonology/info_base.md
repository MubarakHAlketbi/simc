## ~~~~ Demonology Warlock Overview

Demonology Warlocks are known to be masters of demons, constantly summoning new Dreadstalkers and Wild Imps, while also controlling their Felguard to do their bidding.

Our primary resource is [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_gem_amethyst_02.gif)Soul Shards](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=246985/soul-shards), which we both generate and spend with several abilities from our overall toolkit.

## ~~~~ Demonology Warlock Strengths and Weaknesses

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strengths** |  | **Weaknesses** |
| - Durable spec with a large degree of survivability.<br>  <br>- High variety of talent options that can suit different group compositions.<br>  <br>- Solo ability is quite strong due to pet Tanking and overall self-healing.<br>  <br>- Provides the option for instance skips with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_demonicportal_green.gif)Demonic Gateway](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=111771/demonic-gateway) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soulstone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=231811/soulstone). |  | - No damage immunity.<br>  <br>- Ramp time for DPS can be considered higher than other specs.<br>  <br>- Low burst DPS in most situations outside of cooldowns. |

## ~~~~ Baseline Demonology Warlock Spells

### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_shadowbolt.gif)Shadow Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=686/shadow-bolt) **(SB)** \- Single target filler spell that generates one Soul Shard.


### Defensive Cooldowns

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demonictactics.gif)Unending Resolve](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=104773/unending-resolve) **(UR)** \- Shield wall effect, granting you damage reduction and immunity to spell cast interrupts.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_lifedrain02.gif)Drain Life](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=234153/drain-life) \- Drains life from your target and restores it to you.


### Utility Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_possession.gif)Fear](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5782/fear) \- Fears a target in a random direction.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_curseofmannoroth.gif)Curse of Weakness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=702/curse-of-weakness) \- Debuff applied to an enemy mob, increasing the time between their attacks.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/warlock_-healthstone.gif)Create Healthstone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6201/create-healthstone) \- Usable item that restores 25% of your life on use.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_soulgem.gif)Soulstone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20707/soulstone) **(SS)** \- Allows the target to resurrect after dying in combat. Usable before or during combat.


### Passive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_gem_amethyst_02.gif)Soul Shards](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=246985/soul-shards) \- Secondary power, passively regenerates up to three Soul Shards when out of combat.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/warlock_spelldrain.gif)Demonic Core](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=267102/demonic-core) \- When Wild Imps expire or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_warlock_calldreadstalkers.gif)Call Dreadstalkers](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=104316/call-dreadstalkers) expire you have a chance to gain a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/warlock_spelldrain.gif)Demonic Core](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=267102/demonic-core) which reduces the cast time of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv__demonbolt.gif)Demonbolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=264178/demonbolt) by 100%.

## Demonology Warlock Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Demonology Warlock is a fairly simple generator ➜ spender rotation at its core. With a few short cooldowns, there are just a small handful of rules to follow in general:
