#### Master of Harmony (Standard)
This build sacrifices a bit of offensive power for an overall simpler and safer experience, making it an ideal build for players learning to play Brewmaster Monk in a raid setting. It's also a great setup for players in progression raiding environments looking to maximize survivability. In particular, it opts into  Niuzao's Resolve,  Awakening Spirit, and  Tranquil Spirit for a fair bit of passive defense. If you need even more defense, consider dropping the point of  Walk with the Ox for  High Tolerance or  Ox Stance.

#### Master of Harmony (Advanced)
If maximum damage is what you are after, this build is catered more toward that goal while sacrificing a bit of defensive power in the process. However, this will require you to effectively play around  Expel Harm for self-healing due to no longer using  Niuzao's Resolve. It similarly will ask you to master the  Black Ox Brew ability for resource management, and also includes the rotational pressure of more frequent  Blackout Kicks due to  Fluidity of Motion. Consider a talent approach that's between this build and the standard variant if you do not wish to go "all in" on the shift in playstyle.

#### Raid Talent Choices Explained
Legend
Mandatory Talents: You will always take these nodes, regardless of build variant. Unspecified choice nodes are always selected, but their choice may vary between build variants or personal preference.
Flex Talents: These nodes will be frequently moved in and out of, acting as locations to place your remaining spare couple of points based on content or preference.
Niche/Utility Talents: You will rarely slot in and out of these nodes, but there can be moments of them being useful.
Unusable Talents: These options will never be selected in these builds.

Flex Talents and When to Consider Them
Class Tree
 Bounding Agility - If an encounter features frequent movement, the extra distance travelled by  Roll and  Chi Torpedo will quickly add up.
 Jade Walk - Although it will not be active during an encounter, the additional speed can prove useful any time you are traveling between encounters or need to run back after a wipe. As it currently stands, you already have a spare talent point to spend in the top of the tree for most encounters.
 Quick Footed - Many dangerous debuffs come with a snare component. Shortening their duration will usually be beneficial.
 Hasty Provocation - Use on the odd encounter where a boss needs to quickly move out of an area, even at the cost of players potentially losing uptime.
 Improved Touch of Death - Think of this talent as an optional quality of life improvement if you wish to use  Touch of Death as more of a defensive ability during the last 15% of a boss's HP for its  Stagger removal effect.
 Peace and Prosperity - There may be encounters where you are barely able to consistently use  Ring of Peace at its normal cooldown length; the extra 5 seconds of cooldown reduction could make the difference.
 Summon Black Ox Statue - The AoE  Provoke effect of this talent is exceptional when a cluster of enemies spawns far away during an encounter, or even the statue itself can be used when adds need to be kept away from a boss but still tanked. Be careful not to taunt the boss when you shouldn't, though.
 Windwalking - Select this talent in the event that none of your optional utilities are necessary and you can spare the point.
 Fatal Touch - Select for AoE encounters, particularly those with many important adds to defeat.
Spec Tree
 Niuzao's Resolve - If you are in a situation where you need to be in more direct control of your health, particularly if needing to react to consecutive spikes of damage, then it may make sense to not use this talent. It is otherwise a strong default choice, particularly when learning the specialization.
 Tranquil Spirit - While it will not help you survive massive hits of damage, this talent considerably reduces your healing burden over time. However, its effect is best paired with  Niuzao's Resolve from a survival point of view.
 High Tolerance - This talent stands to become one of your biggest optional defensive gains under most circumstances when you feel in need of it.
 Counterstrike - While its damage potential is decent, this talent is unable to trigger if you are not always tanking or are fighting an enemy whose attacks cannot be dodged; consider it something nice to have in raid, but not needed if a point's demanded elsewhere.
 Awakening Spirit - This talent sort of emulates the  Mantra of Purity node available to Master of Harmony Brewmasters, rewarding you with extra mitigation when casting  Purifying Brew. However, it is less useful against larger, infrequent hits than multiple smaller ones for the sake of reducing your healing burden.
 Empty the Cellar - A noteworthy improvement to your  Exploding Keg ability, this follow-up attack nearly generates an extra cast of  Purifying Brew from its cooldown reduction. Unfortunately, your points at the bottom of the tree are limited, making this more useful as an extra bit of damage when you can afford it.
 Fuel on the Fire - For exceptionally add-heavy encounters, this talent offers an extra bit of burst threat to your  Exploding Keg, but is often difficult to pick up without compromising your defenses.
Niche/Utility Talents and When to Consider Them
Class Tree
 Detox - Consider taking this when an encounter features more Poison and Disease dispels than your Healers can reliably cover on their own.
 Vivacious Vivification - Though rare, there may be the odd moment where an instant-cast can prove useful for saving either yourself or another player in a key moment of an encounter. This choice is more noteworthy for Shado-Pan players due to their  Protect and Serve passive.
 Pressure Points - While Enrage effects are generally rare in raid environments, you have the ability to deal with them through this talent.
 Swift Art - By being able to remove snare effects with  Roll on occasion, you may have the opportunity to then cast  Tiger's Lust on an ally and remove a similar effect from them at the same time. Just remember that this talent will not remove root effects!
 Diffuse Magic - Some encounters may feature a dangerous debuff on a tank that can be dispelled, but these usually revolve around a healer doing that task at a set moment. Consider this alternative in rare circumstances when your healers are taxed and you were already planning to use  Fortifying Brew anyway.
 Chi Transfer - It is nice to be able to make  Touch of Death an even more defensive ability (especially if combined with  Improved Touch of Death), but this talent is hindered by being in a section of the talent tree that is extremely tight on points while its heal is unlikely to be lifesaving. Still, it is more than nothing.
 Transcendence: Linked Spirits - Although there are usually better uses for regular  Transcendence, including the ability to reset raid encounters, there may be the rare moment where you can take advantage of teleporting directly to another player rather than a set location. Remember to apply the link to someone first!
Spec Tree
 Elixir of Determination - While its effect is still a potent defensive option for a Brewmaster, this node's lack of meaningful connections at the top of the tree makes it tough to select in all but the most defensive builds.
 Swift as a Coursing River - The effectively permanent speed boost offered by this talent is a delight, but its real power lies in the small damage boost to a Shado-Pan's Brewmaster's  Flurry Strikes. However, it too is unlikely to justify spending the point unless playing a very offensive build.
 Celestial Flames - This talent offers a relatively modest and random damage boost, and is tough to budget into most builds. Still, it can be done if you are willing to sacrifice most defensive choices as an alternative path to  Walk with the Ox.
 One With the Wind - If an encounter features a series of important attacks to dodge, having a chance of not resetting your  Mastery: Elusive Brawler may make all the difference in survival; it similarly is a lower-priority defensive talent.
 Ox Stance - If an encounter features regular Tankbuster hits for large portions of your health, this talent is fantastic for boosting your  Stagger specifically in those dangerous moments. Its stacks will otherwise frequently go unused across an entire encounter.

With the massive amount of different scenarios you'll encounter in a mythic+ dungeon, there is a lot more flexibility to consider. For example, this build defaults to  Niuzao's Resolve and  Bob and Weave for a smoother healing experience, but like the advanced raid build above you will eventually be expected to handle regular use of  Expel Harm and  Black Ox Brew in higher keystone levels. Likewise,  Detox and  Pressure Points are not always required in a dungeon, but be warned that not having them when you do need them can be a serious mistake. Further decision making can be found on the "Talent Choices Explained" tab.

Mandatory Talents: You will always take these nodes, regardless of build variant. Unspecified choice nodes are always selected, but their choice may vary between build variants or personal preference.
Flex Talents: These nodes will be frequently moved in and out of, acting as locations to place your remaining spare couple of points based on content or preference.
Niche/Utility Talents: You will rarely slot in and out of these nodes, but there can be moments of them being useful.
Unusable Talents: These options will never be selected in these builds.

Class Tree
 Bounding Agility - If an encounter features frequent movement, the extra distance travelled by  Roll and  Chi Torpedo will quickly add up.
 Jade Walk - Although it will not be active during an encounter, the additional speed can prove useful any time you are traveling between encounters or need to run back after a wipe. As it currently stands, you already have a spare talent point to spend in the top of the tree for most encounters.
 Quick Footed - Many dangerous debuffs come with a snare component. Shortening their duration will usually be beneficial.
 Hasty Provocation - Use on the odd encounter where a boss needs to quickly move out of an area, even at the cost of players potentially losing uptime.
 Improved Touch of Death - Think of this talent as an optional quality of life improvement if you wish to use  Touch of Death as more of a defensive ability during the last 15% of a boss's HP for its  Stagger removal effect.
 Peace and Prosperity - There may be encounters where you are barely able to consistently use  Ring of Peace at its normal cooldown length; the extra 5 seconds of cooldown reduction could make the difference.
 Summon Black Ox Statue - The AoE  Provoke effect of this talent is exceptional when a cluster of enemies spawns far away during an encounter, or even the statue itself can be used when adds need to be kept away from a boss but still tanked. Be careful not to taunt the boss when you shouldn't, though.
 Windwalking - Select this talent in the event that none of your optional utilities are necessary and you can spare the point.
 Fatal Touch - Select for AoE encounters, particularly those with many important adds to defeat.
Spec Tree
 Niuzao's Resolve - If you are in a situation where you need to be in more direct control of your health, particularly if needing to react to consecutive spikes of damage, then it may make sense to not use this talent. It is otherwise a strong default choice, particularly when learning the specialization.
 Tranquil Spirit - While it will not help you survive massive hits of damage, this talent considerably reduces your healing burden over time. However, its effect is best paired with  Niuzao's Resolve from a survival point of view.
 High Tolerance - This talent stands to become one of your biggest optional defensive gains under most circumstances when you feel in need of it.
 Counterstrike - While its damage potential is decent, this talent is unable to trigger if you are not always tanking or are fighting an enemy whose attacks cannot be dodged; consider it something nice to have in raid, but not needed if a point's demanded elsewhere.
 Awakening Spirit - This talent sort of emulates the  Mantra of Purity node available to Master of Harmony Brewmasters, rewarding you with extra mitigation when casting  Purifying Brew. However, it is less useful against larger, infrequent hits than multiple smaller ones for the sake of reducing your healing burden.
 Empty the Cellar - A noteworthy improvement to your  Exploding Keg ability, this follow-up attack nearly generates an extra cast of  Purifying Brew from its cooldown reduction. Unfortunately, your points at the bottom of the tree are limited, making this more useful as an extra bit of damage when you can afford it.
 Fuel on the Fire - For exceptionally add-heavy encounters, this talent offers an extra bit of burst threat to your  Exploding Keg, but is often difficult to pick up without compromising your defenses.
Niche/Utility Talents and When to Consider Them
Class Tree
 Detox - Consider taking this when an encounter features more Poison and Disease dispels than your Healers can reliably cover on their own.
 Vivacious Vivification - Though rare, there may be the odd moment where an instant-cast can prove useful for saving either yourself or another player in a key moment of an encounter. This choice is more noteworthy for Shado-Pan players due to their  Protect and Serve passive.
 Pressure Points - While Enrage effects are generally rare in raid environments, you have the ability to deal with them through this talent.
 Swift Art - By being able to remove snare effects with  Roll on occasion, you may have the opportunity to then cast  Tiger's Lust on an ally and remove a similar effect from them at the same time. Just remember that this talent will not remove root effects!
 Diffuse Magic - Some encounters may feature a dangerous debuff on a tank that can be dispelled, but these usually revolve around a healer doing that task at a set moment. Consider this alternative in rare circumstances when your healers are taxed and you were already planning to use  Fortifying Brew anyway.
 Chi Transfer - It is nice to be able to make  Touch of Death an even more defensive ability (especially if combined with  Improved Touch of Death), but this talent is hindered by being in a section of the talent tree that is extremely tight on points while its heal is unlikely to be lifesaving. Still, it is more than nothing.
 Transcendence: Linked Spirits - Although there are usually better uses for regular  Transcendence, including the ability to reset raid encounters, there may be the rare moment where you can take advantage of teleporting directly to another player rather than a set location. Remember to apply the link to someone first!
Spec Tree
 Elixir of Determination - While its effect is still a potent defensive option for a Brewmaster, this node's lack of meaningful connections at the top of the tree makes it tough to select in all but the most defensive builds.
 Swift as a Coursing River - The effectively permanent speed boost offered by this talent is a delight, but its real power lies in the small damage boost to a Shado-Pan's Brewmaster's  Flurry Strikes. However, it too is unlikely to justify spending the point unless playing a very offensive build.
 Celestial Flames - This talent offers a relatively modest and random damage boost, and is tough to budget into most builds. Still, it can be done if you are willing to sacrifice most defensive choices as an alternative path to  Walk with the Ox.
 One With the Wind - If an encounter features a series of important attacks to dodge, having a chance of not resetting your  Mastery: Elusive Brawler may make all the difference in survival; it similarly is a lower-priority defensive talent.
 Ox Stance - If an encounter features regular Tankbuster hits for large portions of your health, this talent is fantastic for boosting your  Stagger specifically in those dangerous moments. Its stacks will otherwise frequently go unused across an entire encounter.

 Jade Walk - Although it will not be active during an encounter, the additional speed can prove useful any time you are traveling between encounters or need to run back after a wipe. As it currently stands, you already have a spare talent point to spend in the top of the tree for most encounters.
 Quick Footed - Many dangerous debuffs come with a snare component. Shortening their duration will usually be beneficial.
 Hasty Provocation - Use on the odd encounter where a boss needs to quickly move out of an area, even at the cost of players potentially losing uptime.
 Improved Touch of Death - Think of this talent as an optional quality of life improvement if you wish to use  Touch of Death as more of a defensive ability during the last 15% of a boss's HP for its  Stagger removal effect.
 Peace and Prosperity - There may be encounters where you are barely able to consistently use  Ring of Peace at its normal cooldown length; the extra 5 seconds of cooldown reduction could make the difference.
 Summon Black Ox Statue - The AoE  Provoke effect of this talent is exceptional when a cluster of enemies spawns far away during an encounter, or even the statue itself can be used when adds need to be kept away from a boss but still tanked. Be careful not to taunt the boss when you shouldn't, though.
 Windwalking - Select this talent in the event that none of your optional utilities are necessary and you can spare the point.
 Fatal Touch - Select for AoE encounters, particularly those with many important adds to defeat.
Spec Tree
 Niuzao's Resolve - If you are in a situation where you need to be in more direct control of your health, particularly if needing to react to consecutive spikes of damage, then it may make sense to not use this talent. It is otherwise a strong default choice, particularly when learning the specialization.
 Tranquil Spirit - While it will not help you survive massive hits of damage, this talent considerably reduces your healing burden over time. However, its effect is best paired with  Niuzao's Resolve from a survival point of view.
 High Tolerance - This talent stands to become one of your biggest optional defensive gains under most circumstances when you feel in need of it.
 Counterstrike - While its damage potential is decent, this talent is unable to trigger if you are not always tanking or are fighting an enemy whose attacks cannot be dodged; consider it something nice to have in raid, but not needed if a point's demanded elsewhere.
 Awakening Spirit - This talent sort of emulates the  Mantra of Purity node available to Master of Harmony Brewmasters, rewarding you with extra mitigation when casting  Purifying Brew. However, it is less useful against larger, infrequent hits than multiple smaller ones for the sake of reducing your healing burden.
 Empty the Cellar - A noteworthy improvement to your  Exploding Keg ability, this follow-up attack nearly generates an extra cast of  Purifying Brew from its cooldown reduction. Unfortunately, your points at the bottom of the tree are limited, making this more useful as an extra bit of damage when you can afford it.
 Fuel on the Fire - For exceptionally add-heavy encounters, this talent offers an extra bit of burst threat to your  Exploding Keg, but is often difficult to pick up without compromising your defenses.

Class Tree
 Detox - Consider taking this when an encounter features more Poison and Disease dispels than your Healers can reliably cover on their own.
 Vivacious Vivification - Though rare, there may be the odd moment where an instant-cast can prove useful for saving either yourself or another player in a key moment of an encounter. This choice is more noteworthy for Shado-Pan players due to their  Protect and Serve passive.
 Pressure Points - While Enrage effects are generally rare in raid environments, you have the ability to deal with them through this talent.
 Swift Art - By being able to remove snare effects with  Roll on occasion, you may have the opportunity to then cast  Tiger's Lust on an ally and remove a similar effect from them at the same time. Just remember that this talent will not remove root effects!
 Diffuse Magic - Some encounters may feature a dangerous debuff on a tank that can be dispelled, but these usually revolve around a healer doing that task at a set moment. Consider this alternative in rare circumstances when your healers are taxed and you were already planning to use  Fortifying Brew anyway.
 Chi Transfer - It is nice to be able to make  Touch of Death an even more defensive ability (especially if combined with  Improved Touch of Death), but this talent is hindered by being in a section of the talent tree that is extremely tight on points while its heal is unlikely to be lifesaving. Still, it is more than nothing.
 Transcendence: Linked Spirits - Although there are usually better uses for regular  Transcendence, including the ability to reset raid encounters, there may be the rare moment where you can take advantage of teleporting directly to another player rather than a set location. Spec Tree
 Elixir of Determination - While its effect is still a potent defensive option for a Brewmaster, this node's lack of meaningful connections at the top of the tree makes it tough to select in all but the most defensive builds.
 Swift as a Coursing River - The effectively permanent speed boost offered by this talent is a delight, but its real power lies in the small damage boost to a Shado-Pan's Brewmaster's  Flurry Strikes. However, it too is unlikely to justify spending the point unless playing a very offensive build.
 Celestial Flames - This talent offers a relatively modest and random damage boost, and is tough to budget into most builds. Still, it can be done if you are willing to sacrifice most defensive choices as an alternative path to  Walk with the Ox.
 One With the Wind - If an encounter features a series of important attacks to dodge, having a chance of not resetting your  Mastery: Elusive Brawler may make all the difference in survival; it similarly is a lower-priority defensive talent.
 Ox Stance - If an encounter features regular Tankbuster hits for large portions of your health, this talent is fantastic for boosting your  Stagger specifically in those dangerous moments. Its stacks will otherwise frequently go unused across an entire encounter.

Legend
Mandatory Talents: You will always take these nodes, regardless of build variant. Unspecified choice nodes are always selected, but their choice may vary between build variants or personal preference.
Flex Talents: These nodes will be frequently moved in and out of, acting as locations to place your remaining spare couple of points based on content or preference.
Niche/Utility Talents: You will rarely slot in and out of these nodes, but there can be moments of them being useful.
Unusable Talents: These options will never be selected in these builds.

 Bounding Agility - When a dungeon may not allow you to mount, the extra distance travelled by  Roll and  Chi Torpedo will quickly add up over time.
 Detox - If the dungeon you are running features dangerous Poison or Disease effects, this talent represents a powerful piece of added utility. See our dedicated Mythic+ page for a list of which dungeons this applies to in the current season!
 Pressure Points - Any dungeon featuring Enrage effects can benefit from you having the ability to deal with them through this talent. See our dedicated Mythic+ page for a list of which dungeons this applies to in the current season!
 Improved Touch of Death - While it is less difficult to cast  Touch of Death in a dungeon where enemies are taking less damage, this talent can still be useful against bosses as an extra bit of  Stagger removal.
 Peace and Prosperity -  Ring of Peace is often heavily featured in dungeons, and a small amount of cooldown reduction will naturally result in more uses over time.
 Summon Black Ox Statue - There may be times when adds need to be drawn away but still Tanked. Your use of this statue can do this effectively as a Tank, or you can even utilize its AoE  Provoke effect when threat is especially an issue. Finally, there can even be the odd bit of unexpected utility involving the manipulation of enemy AI now and then.
 Windwalking - Due to an abundance of spare talent points, you may be able to regularly make use of this talent's additional movement speed for your entire party.
Spec Tree
 Improved Blackout Kick/ Swift as a Coursing River - You will always select one of these two talents, with each offering a negligible difference in your overall performance. However, Swift as a Coursing River does provide Shado-Pan Brewmasters with a small boost to their  Flurry Strikes generation alongside an always-helpful movement speed bonus.
 Niuzao's Resolve - Although this talent is a strong default choice for most key levels, at a certain point you may need to be in more direct control of your health as a Brewmaster by taking advantage of  Expel Harm. Once you have reached key levels where this is the case, switch out of this talent and select a different defensive choice.
 Zen State/ Tranquil Spirit/ High Tolerance/ Anvil and Stave - These talents represent your biggest optional defensive gains under most circumstances when you feel in need of them. They are fairly interchangeable, though Zen State is perhaps the most potent for its investment.
 Vital Flame - Your potential with this talent is based on how much you have invested into dealing additional Fire or Nature damage elsewhere. When combined with  Exploding Keg in particular on larger groups of enemies, it becomes a noteworthy bit of burst healing beyond its regular trickle.
 Empty the Cellar - Though this talent's damage and cooldown reduction is significant, each of its thrown kegs will only deal single-target damage rather than AoE. Consider opting into something more defensive unless you want more DPS.
Niche/Utility Talents and When to Consider Them
Class Tree
 Vivacious Vivification - While this talent does enable frequent usage of  Vivify for added healing, it will rarely be an effective use of an ability over something more productive. Still, it has moments of strength when you are truly alone without a healer.
 Energy Transfer - Dungeons tend to feature a much larger number of effects to interrupt, giving you ample opportunity to accrue cooldown reduction on  Paralysis in particular. However, it can be hard to fit this point in over something more generally useful.
 Hasty Provocation - Use this in the odd dungeon where a boss or other enemy needs to quickly move out of an area, even at the cost of players potentially losing uptime.
 Spirit's Essence - As you usually have enough mobility on your own to kite with ease, a pre-placed  Transcendence for kiting becomes even more powerful with this talent's potent snare.
 Swift Art - By being able to remove snare effects with  Roll on occasion, you may have the opportunity to then cast  Tiger's Lust on an ally and remove a similar effect from them at the same time. Just remember that this talent will not remove root effects!
 Diffuse Magic - Having access to an extra dispel in a group where usually only the healer can remove Magic debuffs is an excellent prospect, but being tied to your strongest defensive cooldown of  Fortifying Brew greatly limits this talent's potential; the redirection effect is also usually irrelevant.
 Chi Transfer - If you would like to turn your frequent  Touch of Death casts into a more powerful defensive tool, this talent is great for the job. Its greatest problem is being in the bottom of the class tree where the point investment can be tough to justify.
Spec Tree
 Elixir of Determination - With only one healer present, Mythic+ dungeons tend to ask for a bit more self-reliance from tanks; this talent gives you an extra cushion in dangerous moments to help with that task, but its lack of noteworthy pathing to other talents makes it difficult to recommend if you don't truly need its defense.
 Swift as a Coursing River - Though the increase in attack speed is nice for Shado-Pan Brewmasters especially, the passive movement speed increase is more noteworthy for moments when you may need to kite your enemies. Otherwise, you are likely outrunning your party members already with your mobility and cannot spare the talent point.
 Celestial Flames - The increased range on  Rushing Jade Wind while this talent's buff is active is its greatest benefit in a dungeon. Unfortunately, its random nature and poor tree placement renders it difficult to spend a point on unless this slight increase in AoE damage is what you desire.
 One With the Wind - In higher key levels where dodging boss autoattacks can be useful, this talent gains (limited) use. Otherwise, you are likely exceeding 100% dodge on a regular basis when fighting groups of enemies, negating much of its value.
 Ox Stance - If your key level features regular Tankbuster hits for large portions of your health, this talent is fantastic for boosting your  Stagger during those dangerous moments. That said, it's also important to consider the value of this talent versus another point of  High Tolerance.
 Mighty Stomp - The additional knock-up effect this talent offers to each cast of  Invoke Niuzao, the Black Ox is helpful as a source of enemy disruption. However your groups will frequently have access to abilities that do not move the enemies to stop their attacks, which is more favorable for AoE damage. Beyond this, your talent point budget makes this difficult to acquire.

This build is aimed at content that is trivial enough for you to likely to defeat any enemies in your path with a single hit. As such, it focuses on selecting as many talents as possible for mobility, passive damage, and enemy gathering. You can expect to see some talents used here that rarely, if ever, see play in maximum-level content. Don't forget to bring a  Charged Phial of Alacrity ,  Exquisitely Eviscerated Muscle, and all of your speed-increasing gear!
