 TIER SET BONUSES FOR BREWMASTER MONK
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Brewmaster Monk gear page!

Brewmaster Monk Gear Guide
Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Brewmaster Monk
In Midnight Season 1, Brewmaster Monk's set bonuses revolve around the core rotational abilities of  Keg Smash and  Breath of Fire. This is largely just a matter of buffing their damage dealt, though they also include an additional damage effect and an Energy cost reduction to help offset the Energy deficit naturally found in the rotation.
Way of Ra-den's Chosen
 Monk Brewmaster 12.0 Class Set 2pc -  Keg Smash damage increased by 10%.  Breath of Fire damage increased by 10%.
 Monk Brewmaster 12.0 Class Set 4pc -  Keg Smash's Energy cost is reduced by 5 and when it strikes a target afflicted by  Breath of Fire it deals additional Fire damage.

Tier Set Bonus Rotation Changes for Brewmaster Monk
While tier set bonuses normally create potentially significant impacts to a specialization's rotation when equipped, this season's bonuses for Brewmaster Monk are instead quite minimal. The 2-piece bonus's buffs to  Keg Smash and  Breath of Fire are both small enough to not alter their fairly low priority in the rotation, though the natural synergy with the  Dragonfire Brew talent makes it more competitive with the alternate choice of  Charred Passions in single-target.

Although they may seem more significant, the effects of the 4-piece bonus are even more inconsequential. While reducing the Energy cost of  Keg Smash by 1/8 at all times seems relevant, and would be before Midnight, the Brewmaster Apex Talents completely negate this effect. Due to  Bring Me Another, every bonus charge of  Keg Smash generated by using Brew abilities instead costs 0 Energy. If anything, the small reduction outside of Apex activations will benefit Brewmasters making use of  Bob and Weave instead of  Black Ox Brew, due to the rotation slowly depleting Energy reserves when unlucky. Meanwhile, the additional Fire damage (shown on a damage breakdown as  Extra Kick) offers no real benefits beyond a small bit of additional healing when talented into  Vital Flame.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR BREWMASTER MONK TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
