## ~~~~ Brewmaster Monk Overview

Brewmaster Monk is a leather-wearing Tank specialization that combines damage smoothing, a fluid base rotation, tons of mobility, and a powerful collection of offensive and defensive cooldowns. In the years since they were introduced in Mists of Pandaria, Brewmaster Monks have consistently been one of the top Tank specs in the game, and they are usually well-represented at the highest levels of raiding. Although they see infrequent play at the very highest levels of keystone dungeons, Brewmasters are still more than capable of tackling small-group content of all ranges.

### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_tigerpalm.gif)Tiger Palm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100780/tiger-palm) ( **TP**) \- Basic damaging "filler" skill that reduces the cooldown on your Brew abilities by 1 second, costing 25 Energy.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_roundhousekick.gif)Blackout Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=100784/blackout-kick) ( **BoK**) \- Single-target damage skill that grants [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_shuffle.gif)Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322120/shuffle) and a stack of your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_guard.gif)Mastery: Elusive Brawler](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=117906/mastery-elusive-brawler) buff, with no cost and a short cooldown.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_cranekick_new.gif)Spinning Crane Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322729/spinning-crane-kick) ( **SCK**) \- AoE "filler" skill, costing 25 Energy and hitting all nearby enemies 4 times over 1.5 seconds, with the duration reduced by your Haste. Hitting an enemy with this ability will grant you [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_shuffle.gif)Shuffle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=322120/shuffle), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_healthsphere.gif)Healing Sphere](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=224863/healing-sphere) s from other talents will be slowly drawn to you during the channel. Note that you can still dodge while channeling this ability, and casting it multiple times in a row will combine the durations.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_cracklingjadelightning.gif)Crackling Jade Lightning](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=117952/crackling-jade-lightning) ( **CJL**) \- Channeled single-target damage spell, costing 20 Energy per tick and occasionally knocking back the target if in melee range. Exclusively used for occasionally tagging a distant enemy and immediately cancelling the cast.

## Brewmaster Monk Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Brewmaster Monk is often considered a difficult specialization to learn, with three main resources to watch and a "weird" way of taking damage due to the [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/monk_stance_drunkenox.gif)Stagger](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=115069/stagger) passive. While there are more buttons to press than a DPS would, most of them fall under a few specific goals that are easy to follow. Working toward these goals should help you understand what you're doing and why as a drunken brawler. For all combat in general, consider these as your four main priorities by importance:
