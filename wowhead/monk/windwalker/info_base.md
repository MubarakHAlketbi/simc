## ~~~~Windwalker Monk Overview

Windwalker Monk is a dual-wielding or Two-Handed weapon using, leather-wearing damage specialization with a mix of damage reduction, self-healing, high mobility, and enemy control. The main focus is using on turning Energy into Chi to use with hard hitting or fast melee strikes like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/monk_ability_fistoffury.gif)Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_risingsunkick.gif)Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick). Windwalker is a good fit for players who enjoy close up and fast hitting playstyle that emphasizes resource management and high mobility.

## Windwalker Monk Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Although Windwalker Monks have one of the most unique Masteries in the game, the spec's playstyle can be simplified into a few important things to remember. The core concepts to keep in mind are these:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Don't Repeat Abilities:** Don't hit the same ability twice in a row. Midnight added a new buff that tracks what button you pressed last, track that buff using the in-game options or another tool. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid Overcapping Resources:** Windwalker has to manage both Energy and Chi, try to avoid wasting them. There are times where wasting resources cannot be avoided, but as a general rule, try to do this as little as possible. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Use Abilities with Cooldowns:** Abilities with short cooldowns like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_monk_risingsunkick.gif)Rising Sun Kick](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107428/rising-sun-kick) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/monk_ability_fistoffury.gif)Fists of Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=113656/fists-of-fury) are some of the most important damage abilities you have. Avoid abilities with cooldowns sitting unused as this often leaves to lost damage potential. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Plan your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_monk_weaponsoforder.gif)Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) Usage:** [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_monk_weaponsoforder.gif)Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) has a very flexible cooldown depending on your choice of talents, plan when to use it to maximize its strength. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_monk_weaponsoforder.gif)Zenith](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249625/zenith) can be a 60, 70, 80, or a 90-second cooldown, you'll likely want to go with a 60-second or 90-second cooldown and adjust for using it every 60, 90, or 120 seconds depending on the fight. | |
