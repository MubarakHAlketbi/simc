Windwalker Monk Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond, and fill the rest of the slots with gems that increase whatever stats you need the most. This decision is the easiest, and one of the more important, decisions to use Raidbots to sim the different options.

Windwalker Monk Flasks in Midnight Season 1
Overall the best Flask for Windwalker Monks is  Flask of the Blood Knights . Any of the Flasks that give Haste, Critical Strike, or Mastery will be viable options, just be sure that your sims are using the one you want and it will adjust your gems and enchants if needed.
Windwalker Monk Combat Potions in Midnight Season 1
While there are many options for potions, Windwalker Monks will always use  Potion of Recklessness .
Windwalker Monk Health Potions in Midnight Season 1
The best healing potion for Windwalker Monks is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Windwalker Monk Weapon Buff in Midnight Season 1
For their weapons, Windwalker Monks will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Windwalker Monk Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
