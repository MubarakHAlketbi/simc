### tab_group_0 — Tabs: ['Shado-Pan']

#### Shado-Pan
Shado-pan is a relatively simple and streamlined version of Windwalker to play in raids. It should excel at providing consistent pressure, lose minimal damage to mechanics, and also provide some flexible use of  Zenith to adapt to different fight timings. The main difference between this build and one looking to deal more AOE damage is that it travels fully down the right side of the talent tree to grab  Jadefire Stomp and the connected choice node.
