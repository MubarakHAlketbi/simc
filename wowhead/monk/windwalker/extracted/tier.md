 TIER SET BONUSES FOR WINDWALKER MONK
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Windwalker Monk gear page!

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Windwalker Monk
Thematically, the Season 1 set takes it's name from Ra-Den, the twice-Raid boss that first appeared in the second tier of Mists of Pandaria, Throne of Thunder.

Like most Season 1 tier bonuses, the Midnight Season 1 bonuses are relatively benign and focus on simply improving the baseline functions rather than changing anything. Currently, these tier bonuses are very small gains overall, roughly 3-5% and do not change your talent or priority recommendations.
Way of Ra-den's Chosen
 Monk Windwalker12.0 Class Set 2pc - Strike of the Windlord and Whirling Dragon Punch's damage is increased by 30%.
 Monk Windwalker12.0 Class Set 4pc - The cooldowns of Strike of the Windlord and Whirling Dragon Punch are reduced by 5 sec.






Best Tier Set Pieces for Windwalker Monk
Our tier set bonus, Way of Ra-den's Chosen, is comprised of the following items from Midnight Season 1:
 Fearsome Visage of Ra-den's Chosen (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)
 Aurastones of Ra-den's Chosen (Shoulders from Fallen-King Salhadaar - The Voidspire)
 Battle Garb of Ra-den's Chosen (Chest from Chimaerus - The Dreamrift)
 Thunderfists of Ra-den's Chosen (Gloves from Vorasius - The Voidspire)
 Swiftsweepers of Ra-den's Chosen (Legs from Vaelgor & Ezzorak - The Voidspire)
Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to their itemization, Windwalker Monk prefer equipping tier pieces on these slots:
Helm
Legs
Shoulder
Chest
Ideally, you'll have Gloves as your off-piece as the tier option is poorly itemized for Windwalker Monk, since they are the only piece with Versatility.

This is only a minor optimization; wearing five pieces of the tier is fine if you don't have a high item-level alternative. Fully Best-In-Slot setups will often choose to replace the Chest piece instead. Many options are possible for gearing on Assassination, and we urge players to simulate themselves for more accurate answers.
Tier Set Bonus Rotation Changes for Windwalker Monk
The Midnight Season 1 bonuses are not very strong and simply add damage to the existing rotation. This means there is no changes to the priority with or without the bonuses.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR WINDWALKER MONK TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
