## ~~~~Frost Death Knight Overview

Frost Death Knight is a Two-Handed **and** Dual-Wield plate DPS specialization focusing on dealing both Physical and magical damage. Frost can guarantee critical hits on its primary damage-dealing ability, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_classicon.gif)Obliterate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020/obliterate), with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine); breathe ice on its enemies with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_breathofsindragosa.gif)Breath of Sindragosa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249658/breath-of-sindragosa) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_sindragosa.gif)Frostwyrm's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=279302/frostwyrms-fury); and engulf its enemies in freezing blizzards with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_remorselesswinters2.gif)Remorseless Winter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196770/remorseless-winter).

As a Hero Class, new Frost Death Knights start at Level 8 instead of Level 1. All races can play Frost Death Knights, except for Dracthyr and Earthen. Players who enjoy resource management, reacting to procs, and setting up cooldown windows will enjoy Frost.

## ~~~~ Frost Death Knight Changes in Midnight

With Midnight, WoW's core combat systems have received substantial changes. Frost Death Knight, however, got its changes early, in patch 11.2. For Midnight, Frost sees hardly any changes outside of some minor tuning adjustments. If it has been a while since you last played Frost and are curious what's new, details on the 11.2 rework are included here.

**Previous Frost Changes**

Patch 11.2 brings Frost's biggest shakeup since the Dragonflight talent tree update, and even that update was more focused on providing familiar Shadowlands-era talents and Legendaries in a cohesive tree. Ghosts of K'aresh has brought the kind of rework to Frost that we were not expecting to see until Midnight. Nearly every node in the spec talent tree has changed, along with new pathways throughout the tree, creating multiple build possibilities. The gameplay will still feel familiar to veterans of the spec while leaving it in a flexible state for further changes down the road.

Conceptually, Frost's core abilities have stayed the same. There is a primary Rune spender, a primary Runic Power spender, and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_arcticwinds.gif)Howling Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49184/howling-blast) with Rime to provide resource-free GCDs. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) is still the most critical proc in the rotation, while [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost) is still the spec's primary cooldown. The bulk of changes are directed at clunky talents or game balance concerns that made Frost difficult to tweak. As a result, the new Frost has a more streamlined feel to it. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_cursed_05.gif)Killing Streak](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1230153/killing-streak) relieves concerns over wasting Killing Machine, making it easier to pick up the spec and understand how to play it without getting bogged down in a counterintuitive rotation. Here are the most significant changes that will be immediately noticeable:

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_classicon.gif)Obliterate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020/obliterate) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_empowerruneblade2.gif)Frost Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49143/frost-strike) are now single-target only buttons, with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_frostscythe.gif)Frostscythe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207230/frostscythe) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_glacialtrap.gif)Glacial Advance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194913/glacial-advance) taking their place as the primary AoE buttons.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_1115_warrior_fastermeleeattacks.gif)Cleaving Strikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=316916/cleaving-strikes) no longer causes Obliterate to cleave. Instead, it now buffs Frostscythe when [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_remorselesswinters2.gif)Remorseless Winter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196770/remorseless-winter) is active. Unholy Ground is also gone, so we have no reason to use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathanddecay.gif)Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265/death-and-decay) rotationally.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_62.gif)Empower Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47568/empower-rune-weapon) has been entirely reworked into a medium cooldown off-GCD ability that we weave into the rotation. It now provides a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) proc and Runic Power to give us an on-demand tool to control our rotation.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_freezingbreath.gif)Rime](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59057/rime) triggers from Frost Strike and Glacial Advance now. This change breaks up a toxic loop where Obliterate would proc Rime, but it was more important to Obliterate again to spend [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) than to ensure that the Rime proc was not wasted.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_breathofsindragosa.gif)Breath of Sindragosa](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1249658/breath-of-sindragosa) no longer continuously drains Runic Power and is on a 90-second cooldown. It starts with an 8-second base duration, which extends every time we consume [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_freezingbreath.gif)Rime](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59057/rime). The gameplay feels like the previous version of the spell, but it is now decoupled from our Runic Power economy.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_sindragosa.gif)Frostwyrm's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=279302/frostwyrms-fury) now has a 90-second cooldown and stuns enemies it hits. Rider of the Apocalypse has less of a talent tax to pay now; however, for Deathbringer Frostwyrm's Fury is taken more for an AoE stun than for its raw damage, which is now relatively low.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_titanargus_d_01.gif)Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441378/exterminate) no longer has a chance to reset [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark) and instead grants [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine).


## Frost Death Knight Hero Talents: Deathbringer and Rider of the Apocalypse

Deathbringer encompasses unleashing bursts of direct damage through stacking [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark) and sweeping scythes through foes with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_titanargus_d_01.gif)Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441378/exterminate). [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark) adds a short cooldown to Frost's kit that syncs with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost), making every cooldown phase strong. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_argus_soulbombdebuffsmall.gif)Bind in Darkness](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=440031/bind-in-darkness) adds a hefty buff to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_arcticwinds.gif)Howling Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49184/howling-blast), leading Deathbringer builds to invest heavily in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_arcticwinds.gif)Howling Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49184/howling-blast) talents to make the most of the modifier.

Alternatively, for those who want to take on the title of Deathlord, Rider of the Apocalypse is available to summon the Four Horsemen to swarm their foes. This tree adds no buttons but requires taking [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_sindragosa.gif)Frostwyrm's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=279302/frostwyrms-fury) to activate [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_rideroftheapocalypsedeathknight_apocalypsenow.gif)Apocalypse Now](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444040/apocalypse-now). [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mount_steelwarhorse.gif)Death Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444010/death-charge) provides fantastic mobility, letting us continue to attack from horseback! Rider of the Apocalypse has little impact on the rotation and functions more as a series of passives that buff the player.

The best Hero Talent for a situation will depend on which of those two talents is stronger, given that they are opposed in their design. Deathbringer excels when the encounter suits frequent cooldown phases, particularly when adds are timed to spawn close to 45 seconds apart. Rider of the Apocalypse excels when high mobility is absolutely necessary or when big spikes of damage are needed every 90 seconds. It also rules open-world content and leveling, where the mobility from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mount_steelwarhorse.gif)Death Charge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444010/death-charge) is more important than raw throughput.

## ~~~~ Playing a Frost Death Knight in Midnight Season 1

Frost Death Knight operates on a strict priority list rather than a traditional rotation. You can check how it works in detail in our [rotation guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/frost/rotation-cooldowns-pve-dps). Having a priority list means that all actions should be used in an ordered list of highest value from top to bottom, with you needing to judge your current state throughout an encounter with some predictions. It follows 3 key rules:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Spend procs:** Every talent build relies on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) empowered [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_classicon.gif)Obliterate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020/obliterate)/ [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_frostscythe.gif)Frostscythe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207230/frostscythe) as a primary damage dealer. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_freezingbreath.gif)Rime](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=59057/rime) gives Frost a free filler in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_arcticwinds.gif)Howling Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49184/howling-blast), and it always stays important due to the cooldown reduction granted through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_frostjaw.gif)Frostbound Will](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1238680/frostbound-will). |

|     |     |

| ~~~~ | **Cycle resources effectively:** Frost has two resources, Runes and Runic Power, which co-exist within a positive feedback loop of one another. Spending Runes builds Runic Power, and spending Runic Power restores Runes through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_rune_10.gif)Runic Empowerment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=81229/runic-empowerment). By spending both without waste, we can keep them in balance and ensure we can always cast something. Neglecting a resource can result in downtime due to insufficient resources. |

The Rune spenders are weak when used without Killing Machine, so Empower Rune Weapon is key to avoid "wasting" a GCD on a weak button. | |

## ~~~~ Gearing a Frost Death Knight in Midnight Season 1

As a Frost Death Knight, a general stat priority of **Critical Strike >= Mastery > Haste > Versatility** is best. This stat priority does outweigh the value of **Strength** currently, though the value of item level upgrades still tends to pull ahead of perfectly itemized pieces. We also always recommended using [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear) to sim your own character, since every character has different circumstances. Check our [Great Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/guide=17592) to learn how to increase the chances of obtaining the bis gear for your Frost Death Knight.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Frost Death Knight?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Frost Death Knight players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Blacksmithing](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/blacksmithing-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/frost/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ What Race is Best for Frost Death Knight?

While there are multiple racial options available to Death Knights, it's usually the case that the difference between the performance benefits granted by different racial attributes is minor. As such, it's generally recommended to pick your race based on aesthetic preferences or to go for a specific form of utility that you desire. If you're still intent on min-maxing, there are a few options depending on your goal:

- **Horde**: [![Pandaren](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/race_pandaren_male.gif)Pandaren](https://www.wowhead.com/race=25/pandaren) currently performs the best because of the value from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/pandarenracial_epicurean.gif)Epicurean](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=107072/epicurean). It should be noted that Races providing Primary stat buffs typically fall off as expansions go on, so factor that into your decision-making.

- **Alliance**: [![Dark Iron Dwarf](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/race_darkirondwarf_male.gif)Dark Iron Dwarf](https://www.wowhead.com/race=34/dark-iron-dwarf) wins hand down for offensive and defensive value gained from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_fireblood.gif)Fireblood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=265221/fireblood).

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

**Strength** is your Primary stat, found on mail gear. It increases your Attack Power and damage/healing done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Frost Death Knight gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike** increases the chance of dealing critical damage with your spells. It also interacts with numerous abilities for Frost. Critical Strike directly increases how often our most crucial proc, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine), occurs. It also boosts the proc rate for [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_argus_soulburst.gif)Icy Death Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=435010/icy-death-torrent) and extends [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost) through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_10_dungeonjewelry_primalist_necklace_1_frost.gif)The Long Winter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=456240/the-long-winter).

- **Haste** increases our Rune regeneration rate, decreases the Global Cooldown, and speeds up our auto-attacks. Because the GCD decreases at the same rate as Rune regeneration increases, Haste does not reduce downtime significantly. Instead, it changes longer gaps into multiple short gaps. Haste also works alongside Critical Strike to boost [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_argus_soulburst.gif)Icy Death Torrent](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=435010/icy-death-torrent), with more chances to proc through increased attack speed.

- **Mastery** increases the frost damage done by our abilities ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_gem_sapphire_01.gif)Mastery: Frozen Heart](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77514/mastery-frozen-heart)) and does so at a favorable conversion rate of 2% Frost damage per 1% of Mastery rating gained. This is most useful for AoE, as all of our multi-target abilities deal Frost damage. Mastery does not increase Frost damage from sources outside the spec, such as trinkets. The initial Mastery Diminishing Return kicks in at **76%**.

- **Versatility** increases damage and healing done and decreases damage taken.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

## Pre-Combat Check

Before any combat happens, it is important for Frost Death Knight to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

- **Buff Up** Make sure you have a food buff, an active flask, and weapon oils applied.


## ~~~~ Frost Death Knight Major Cooldowns

Frost only has a few major cooldowns. They should be used alongside each other as much as possible. You never want to use one on its own. Frost's cooldowns require little setup and planning, but there are a few interactions between them to consider.

Pillar of Frost

Frostwyrm's Fury

Reaper's Mark

### Pillar of Frost

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost) is extremely simple by itself, but it serves as an activator for some important effects.

The most important is [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_axe_114.gif)Obliteration](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=281238/obliteration), which alters the feel of the rotation while Pillar of Frost is active. The overall priority does not change, but you will notice that during Pillar of Frost you are pressing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_classicon.gif)Obliterate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020/obliterate) more often to keep spending [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51124/killing-machine). [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_bluehellfire.gif)Frozen Dominion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=377226/frozen-dominion) does not impact your rotation, but it is important to be aware that it makes [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_remorselesswinters2.gif)Remorseless Winter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=196770/remorseless-winter) an automatic passive, so we still take talents that buff it in AoE builds.

### Frostwyrm's Fury

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_sindragosa.gif)Frostwyrm's Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=279302/frostwyrms-fury) is integral to Frost in Midnight, thanks for the Apex talents. The most important thing to know is to never cast Frostwyrm's Fury without [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost), otherwise you will waste [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_reputation_wyrmresttemple.gif)Chosen of Frostbrood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265633/chosen-of-frostbrood). Other concerns are specific to each Hero Talent.

Deathbringer needs put some delay between using Frostwyrm's Fury and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark). [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_titanargus_d_01.gif)Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441378/exterminate) can only stack two times, so [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_blueflamebreath.gif)Echoing Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265855/echoing-fury) will lose effectiveness if you cast Frostwyrm's Fury right after Reaper's Mark (Reaper's Mark can be expected to detonate in only a few seconds). Casting the recall Frostwyrm's Fury grants a single stack of Exterminate through Echoing Fury.

Rider of the Apocalypse wants to use Frostwyrm's Fury as soon as possible after Pillar of Frost. Frostwyrm's Fury summons all the Horsemen through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_rideroftheapocalypsedeathknight_apocalypsenow.gif)Apocalypse Now](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444040/apocalypse-now), meaning [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_axe_1h_draenorcrafted_d_02_b_horde.gif)Apocalyptic Conquest](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444763/apocalyptic-conquest) is active. We want to overlap it with Pillar of Frost's strength buff to keep our Strength as high as possible as long as possible.

### Reaper's Mark

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark) is used on cooldown, on the same GCD as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_pillaroffrost.gif)Pillar of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51271/pillar-of-frost) if it is available. Pairing with Pillar of Frost is especially important in AoE to overlap the Strength buff with the hits from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_animamaw_wave.gif)Wave of Souls](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439851/wave-of-souls). Ensure your target does not already have Reaper's Mark when carrying an [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_titanargus_d_01.gif)Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441378/exterminate) into a new pack. Applying Reaper's Mark to a target that already has it will prematurely end the existing mark.

Killing Streak

### Understanding Killing Streak

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_cursed_05.gif)Killing Streak](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1230153/killing-streak) poses an important question: should I intentionally wait for two stacks of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine)? The short answer is no. Killing Machine stacks to two to give us some rotational breathing room, but do not try to intentionally go to two stacks.

Frost wants to spend as many GCDs as possible on spending Killing Machine, and as few GCDs as possible on casting [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_classicon.gif)Obliterate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49020/obliterate)/ [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_frostscythe.gif)Frostscythe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207230/frostscythe) without Killing Machine. Obliterate deals over double its normal damage with Killing Machine, and Frostscythe deals four times its normal damage. You deal more damage overall by spending two single Killing Machine abilities than by consuming double Killing Machine. The bonus crit damage from Killing Streak is not nearly as much as the difference between an unbuffed hit and a hit buffed by Killing Machine.

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Frost Death Knight, these tools are solid when using the beginner build described above. The built-in logic will not leave any abilities out, and will prompt them in roughly the right order.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Frost Death Knight Beginners

Frost primarily cares about Mastery and Critical Strike. Mastery increases our Frost damage, which covers all our main damage sources except auto-attacks. Critical Strike is key for getting additional procs of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_122.gif)Killing Machine](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51128/killing-machine).
