Frost Death Knight Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond; the increased Strength means it outweighs  Powerful Eversong Diamond .

If you are using one of the embellishments that deals with gem colors, double-check that your gems are the colors you need.
Amethyst:  Flawless Deadly Amethyst 
Garnet:  Flawless Masterful Garnet 
Peridot:  Flawless Deadly Peridot 
Lapid:  Flawless Deadly Lapis 
Frost Death Knight Weapon Enchant
 Rune of the Fallen Crusader is used at all times as it is our most powerful Runeforge option. Builds that play  Shattering Blade will want to use  Rune of Razorice on their main-hand weapon so abilities that only strike once like  Frostscythe apply Razorice. All other dual-wielding builds can rely on  Glacial Advance to apply  Razorice, and so they can take  Rune of the Stoneskin Gargoyle instead.

Frost Death Knight Flask
The best Flask for Frost Death Knight is  Flask of the Shattered Sun .
Frost Death Knight Combat Potions
The best combat potion is  Light's Potential . Another option is  Draught of Rampant Abandon . The void zone dropped is fairly easy to play around as a melee DPS, but in hectic situations, you may not notice fast enough to start moving out and will lose a GCD, making it no longer worth it.
Frost Death Knight Health Potions
The best healing potion for Frost Death Knight is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Frost Death Knight Weapon Buff
Frost Death Knights should always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Frost Death Knight Augment Rune
With The War Within, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Frost Death Knight Food
The differences between the food buffs are very slight; do not worry too much about which one it is as long as you have one.
