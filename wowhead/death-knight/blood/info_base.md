## ~~~~Blood Death Knight Overview

As one of the six Tank specializations in the game, our design as Blood Death Knights involves a reactive, optimization-driven playstyle. We focus on generating as much **Runic Power** as possible through spells and abilities, prioritizing the right one at the appropriate time, and healing ourselves with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike). Our toolkit consists of a variety of answers for many situations, ranging from self-sustain cooldowns such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_lifedrain.gif)Vampiric Blood](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55233/vampiric-blood) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon), immunities to specific mechanics, a combat resurrection for allies, and even a pet ghoul acting as a source of DPS thanks to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_pet_ghoul.gif)Raise Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=46585/raise-dead)!

## ~~~~ Blood Death Knight Changes in Midnight

Midnight brings along with it a number of changes aimed at streamlining and de-cluttering all specializations, and Blood was no exception to this. A specific focus was put on simplifying [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon)'s cooldown variability and reducing some of the direct healing done by [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike).

The result is a greatly reduced number of defensives and, generally, rotational abilities:

### Removed Abilities

Blood lost the following abilities in Midnight:

- **[Bonestorm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194844)** \- One of the ways to reduce the effective cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon), but also up to 15% maximum health per second while active, and a notable source of AoE damage every minute.

- **[Tombstone](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219809)** \- The second ability costing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) charges, but also a 30% maximum health shield and 30 Runic Power generating ability every minute

- **[Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294)** \- A single-target execute

- **[Rune Tap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194679)** \- An opt-in, short duration, short impact defensive

- **[Blood Tap](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=221699)** \- An opt-in Rune generator


### New Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_axe_2h_artifactmaw_d_01.gif)Consumption](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1263824/consumption) \- Technically a rework of the previous spell. This is now an empower spell. Instead of increasing the tick rate of plagues, it now instead consumes up to 75% of the remaining damage on them to heal you.


## Blood Death Knight Hero Talents: San'layn and Deathbringer

Both San'layn and Deathbringer add a layer of depth to the Blood Death Knight rotation. As San'layn, you will be committing to utilizing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) rotationally in order to build and maintain stacks of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_herb_talandrasrose.gif)Essence of the Blood Queen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=433925/essence-of-the-blood-queen), while relying on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_nazmir_boss_bloodofghuun.gif)The Blood is Life](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434260/the-blood-is-life) to be able to funnel and duplicate all your Shadow damage on nearby targets at the end of it. As Deathbringer, your gameplay instead revolves around an additional ability, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark), with a number of hooks tied to its application and expiration.

At level 90 and with the full power of Apex talents and the remaining three nodes on the Hero Talent trees, plus a neat last-minute buff to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike), **San'layn** is back on the menu for everything - particularly single-target and up to 5-8 consistent targets. The new tier set, which will be available in a few weeks, exacerbates this even further.

**Deathbringer** remains significantly easier to play and comes with some defensive advantages, making it a very good choice for Mythic+ specifically.

## ~~~~ Playing a Blood Death Knight in Midnight Season 1

Playing a Blood Death Knight revolves around managing a number of buffs and debuffs, all while optimizing both your generation and spending of Runic Power. Your actions will often be determined by looking up to 30 seconds in the future in order to anticipate whether wasteful casts of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_marrowrend.gif)Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182/marrowrend) can be avoided by leveraging one of the many ways to manage [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) charges - all this in order to generate more Runic Power to spend on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike). You can check how it works in detail in our [rotation guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/blood/rotation-cooldowns-pve-tank) The rotation follows three simple concepts:

These range from positional effects such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathanddecay.gif)Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265/death-and-decay), throughput "checks" such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_brittlebones.gif)Ossuary](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=219786/ossuary), rotational checks such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathwing_bloodcorruption_earth.gif)Hemostasis](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=273947/hemostasis), and maintenance buffs that reward you for playing the spec, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_skeer_bloodletting.gif)Coagulopathy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391481/coagulopathy). ## ~~~~ Gearing a Blood Death Knight in Midnight Season 1

Deathbringer and San'layn follow a slightly different gearing priority. On pieces with Strength on them (all non-jewelry and trinkets), item level gains will outweigh most Secondary stat choices. On jewelry and at equal item level, San'layn will favor **Haste**, while Deathbringer aims to avoid it.

Even with this, we strongly recommend that you use [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/topgear) to sim your own character and evaluate sidegrades with its Top Gear function. Just because this _exact gearset_ is optimal does not necessarily mean that each individual piece on its own is the best in all circumstances.

Check our [Great Vault](https://www.wowhead.com/guide=17592) to learn how to increase the chances of obtaining the bis gear for your Blood Death Knight.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Blood Death knight?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Blood Death knight players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Blacksmithing](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/blacksmithing-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/blood/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ What Race is Best for Blood Death Knight?

Horde Blood Death Knights can be any race except Dracthyr and Earthen.

Alliance Blood Death Knights can be any race except Dracthyr and Earthen.

Alliance Death Knights should strongly consider being a **Night Elf** or **Dwarf** (either regular or Dark Iron), as these are just slightly ahead due to the utility they provide. Night Elves can proactively use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ambush.gif)Shadowmeld](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=58984/shadowmeld) to avoid certain mechanics (within reason) and gain 2% passive dodge (which is additive), while Dwarves can cleanse all debuffs on themselves every 2 minutes. **Pandaren** is also an excellent choice earlier on in the expansion but falls off later on as the value of food buffs tends not to be updated as the expansion goes on.

Horde Death Knights can be any race, but do have a slight preference towards **Goblin** as the multiplicative passive Haste buff is great, and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_rocketjump.gif)Rocket Jump](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=69070/rocket-jump) comes in handy to reposition and avoid mechanics that feature unavoidable knockbacks.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Blood Death Knight

Depending on whether you are playing a **San'layn** or **Deathbringer** build, your stat preferences shift slightly around the stat you're hunting for. These priorities also remain roughly the same in AoE situations. We always recommend simulating your own character using Raidbot's Top Gear function, but general stat priority guidelines are:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **San'layn Stat Priority**<br>* * *<br>1. Strength<br>   <br>2. Haste<br>   <br>3. Mastery / Critical Strike / Versatility | **Deathbringer Stat Priority**<br>* * *<br>1. Strength<br>   <br>2. Critical Strike<br>   <br>3. Mastery / Versatility<br>   <br>4. Haste |

The Haste amplification part of San'layn is due to the multiplicative aspect of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_herb_talandrasrose.gif)Essence of the Blood Queen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=433925/essence-of-the-blood-queen) and the importance of GCD count in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon). It is small but not negligible. Deathbringer instead eschews it as none of the additional bonuses it provides are hasted, and the lion's share of its toolkit is centered on a fixed cooldown ability ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark)).

While not unlimited, gearing provides considerable freedom when customizing stats. Following the suggestions below, we've tailored recommendations of best-in-slot gear to seek during **Midnight Season 1** for Blood Death Knights in our Gearing Guide.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/medium/inv_chest_chain_03.jpg)Blood Death Knight Gearing Guide](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/blood/bis-gear)

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

Strength is your primary stat, found on plate gear. Strength increases your armor and attack power, in addition to providing a neat amount of Parry. It is the most prevalent stat on most gear pieces, and is part of the primary budget.

For secondary stats, Blood Death Knights have the following at their disposal:

- **Critical Strike**: In addition to providing its offensive bonuses, grants a 1:1 conversion to parry rating through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_30.gif)Riposte](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=161797/riposte). More explanation

Critical Strike is very strong offensively, in addition to being a near-1:1 conversion to Parry. Its strength, relative to the rest of the stats, will depend on factors including the number of enemies hitting you, how much of their abilities can be parried, and the average amount of damage each parry-able hit does.



Overall, it provides significant offensive and defensive value, and picks up even more value in M+, where the lion's share of damage is parryable. While it will not be reliable _per hit_, it statistically averages to a very generous amount of damage reduction, direct bonus damage from crits, indirect bonus damage from parry hasting, and significant burst-down protection in modest amounts, particularly when other additive sources of avoidance are in play ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon), Night Elves, etc). However, much like other stats, it is not worth stacking.

- **Haste**: Haste provides a 1.1% increase in rune generation per percent of haste thanks to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield), and has a minor impact on the duration of your global cooldown, allowing you to cast spells slightly more frequently - the value of your self-healing is strongly dependent on the timing of your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike).

- **Mastery**: [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_deathstrike.gif)Mastery: Blood Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77513/mastery-blood-shield) provides an absorb shield calculated from the amount of healing done by [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike), capped at 50% of your maximum health. More explanation

Mastery provides a tremendous amount of value against physical damage and scales in absolute value with the amount of damage you are taking - with some severe limitations. Blood Shield's maximum value is capped at 50% of your maximum health, and as a result the value of Mastery strictly caps out defensively the moment one or two [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike) s cause you to cap Blood Shield. Furthermore, the more damage you take (particularly if not all the damage is physical), the **lower** the Mastery rating required to cap out is. Every point beyond this has effectively zero value, and causes this budget to essentially be wasted. The addition of talents such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ragnaros_heart.gif)Iron Heart](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391395/iron-heart), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Improved Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=374277/improved-death-strike), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ironmaidens_whirlofblood.gif)Voracious](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=273953/voracious) to the mix reduce this threshold even further, making Mastery a nice-to-have but absolutely not worth stacking stat.

- **Versatility**: Versatility provides both a reduction in damage taken and an increase in self-healing, in addition to providing a small damage increase. It is the most globally-applicable stat, but also the most expensive in terms of item value per point.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

How is this damage calculated?

The way [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_bloodbath.gif)Infliction of Sorrow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434143/infliction-of-sorrow) calculates the damage your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodplague.gif)Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078/blood-plague) would deal is both naive, and fraught with lofty assumptions. Good players can capitalize on both.



At its core, if your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodplague.gif)Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078/blood-plague) ticks for an expected 100 damage every 3 seconds, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_bloodbath.gif)Infliction of Sorrow](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434143/infliction-of-sorrow) will hit for 130 \* (remainingDuration/currentTickRate). Both "remainingDuration" and "currentTickRate" are snapshotted to be _right now_, and do not consider whether a temporary increase in tick rate is currently in effect.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sulfurelemental_blood.gif)Frenzied Bloodthirst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434075/frenzied-bloodthirst) \- You can gain two more stacks of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_herb_talandrasrose.gif)Essence of the Blood Queen](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=433925/essence-of-the-blood-queen), up to a total of 7. In addition to this, your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike) deals 6% more damage per stack. This is tripled when [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodtap.gif)Gift of the San'layn](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434152/gift-of-the-sanlayn) is active

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_nazmir_boss_bloodofghuun.gif)The Blood is Life](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434260/the-blood-is-life) \- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) spawns a Blood Beast. It attacks for 10 seconds, and stores some of its auto-attack damage. When it fades, this damage is dealt in an AoE around the point it despawned, duplicated between all mobs in the area.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ragnaros_heart.gif)Visceral Strength](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434157/visceral-strength) \- Grants you 12% strength for 12 seconds when you consume [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_bloodnova.gif)Crimson Scourge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=81136/crimson-scourge).

### Best Death Knight Blood Opener

Your opener as a Blood Death Knight depends on your Hero Talent choice. In both cases, your goal is to set up as many maintenance buffs as you can before the fight starts to cut down on wasteful GCDs you would spend doing so otherwise.

In the following section, we will assume that you are in control of pulling and can spend two global cooldowns preparing things.

**Please select a Hero Talent option to generate an Opener sequence**

_The builds used below are covered in the [Talents page](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/death-knight/blood/talent-builds-pve-tank). Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes._

**Deathbringer**'s opener sets up three elements as quickly as possible: it primes [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathanddecay.gif)Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265/death-and-decay) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield), and makes sure to prioritize [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_deathbringerdeathknight_reapersmark.gif)Reaper's Mark](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439843/reapers-mark) ahead of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon). As the two always come back off cooldown roughly simultaneously if you do not desync them, this guarantees that this order will be preserved during the entire fight.

## Pre-Combat Check

Whilst Blood has no strict pre-combat checklist of group buffs and personal temporary buffs, there are still a couple of valuable resources you can prepare before a fight starts. We have detailed them below:

### Raid

- **Check Consumables -** Flask, food, augment rune, temporary weapon oils or whetstones are worth having at all times.


### Mythic+

- **Conserve Resources Between Pulls -**[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) persists between pulls as long as it has time remaining on it, and Runic Power decays at a rate of 1 RP/second out of combat. This allows you to keep [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) rolling by refreshing it at the end of a pull, and to pool some Runic Power to kickstart your next pull. Skip the maintenance buff minigame by getting ahead of these two resources!

- **Key Start Optimization -** The start of a key leaves your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) charges intact and leaves up to 25 Runic Power when the key starts. You can prepare both resources by hitting enemies or target dummies before the key is inserted if you are not in combat when it starts.


## ~~~~ Blood Death Knight Major Cooldowns

Blood has access to a _single_ offensive cooldown outside of its Hero Talent trees in Midnight, in the form of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon). In addition to this, a number of defensive cooldowns are available, all with differing impact and niches. We have covered how they work, including all of the intricacies and niche use cases they may have below.

Dancing Rune Weapon

### ~~~~ Dancing Rune Weapon

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) grants you 20% parry for 12 seconds when cast, replicates a small subset of abilities ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_weapon_shortblade_40.gif)Heart Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=206930/heart-strike), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_marrowrend.gif)Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182/marrowrend), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodboil.gif)Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842/blood-boil) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike)) and has two additional effects:

- If you cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_marrowrend.gif)Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182/marrowrend) while it is active, you get 3 bonus [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) for each [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) active

- If you cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodboil.gif)Blood Boil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50842/blood-boil) while it is active, each [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) applies its own copy of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_bloodplague.gif)Blood Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55078/blood-plague)


You can empower this spell through other talents, granting you 5% bonus parry for the first 8 seconds of it, and up to 10 Runic Power per second for the first 8 seconds. San'layn takes this up a notch by using this as their main activator for all Hero Talent mechanics.

## ~~~~ Death Knight Blood Advanced Tips

Bone Shield management

### Bone Shield Management

The largest remaining optimization around Blood's toolkit and rotation involves familiarizing yourself with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) generators. Every 30 seconds, you will have to use _one_ of them to refresh bones.

As Deathbringer, when playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_demonhunter_soulcleave2.gif)Reaper's Onslaught](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=469870/reapers-onslaught), this is effectively dealt with for you. When playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_shoulder_93.gif)Dark Talons](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=436687/dark-talons), you can choose to delay one [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_polearm_2h_titanargus_d_01.gif)Exterminate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441378/exterminate) explosion by ~5 seconds to fix this; alternatively, you can slot in a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_strangulate.gif)Death Grip](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49576/death-grip) to refresh your entire stack.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) management is significantly harder as San'layn, due to the hero talent not having _any_ passive generation tools. You are left to your own devices - and to make things even worse, Apex talents have effectively randomized how many [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_boneshield.gif)Bone Shield](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195181/bone-shield) charges a single [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_marrowrend.gif)Marrowrend](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=195182/marrowrend) provides, since each Apex [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_sword_07.gif)Dancing Rune Weapon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49028/dancing-rune-weapon) provides its own bonus three stacks. Proper UI tracking goes a long way towards solving this, but there is still an element of skill involved.

### Blood Death Knight Gems

In general, you will want to use [![Indecipherable Eversong Diamond](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_jewelcrafting_epic_gem_cut_green.gif)Indecipherable Eversong Diamond![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240983/indecipherable-eversong-diamond) as your unique Jewelecrafting gem. Unlike prior expansions, this gem does not require a number of other gems to empower it further.

All other gem slots should be filled with whichever gem provides the most value for you. As a sensible default, San'layn players may opt for [![Flawless Quick Garnet](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_jewelcrafting_rare_gem_cut_nature_red.gif)Flawless Quick Garnet![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240906/flawless-quick-garnet), while Deathbringer players should opt for [![Flawless Masterful Garnet](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_jewelcrafting_rare_gem_cut_void_red.gif)Flawless Masterful Garnet![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240908/flawless-masterful-garnet).

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Blood Death Knight, these tools are

The Assisted Highlight mechanism works relatively well for Blood, but it is by far not optimal. Nevertheless, it does understand the basic priorities of the spec, and specifically for Deathbringer, it is comparable in performance to that of a beginner, with the immediate difference that it tends to spend Runic Power at the right times.

While we recommend getting familiar with the spec, using Assisted Highlight is a good way to get some early assistance in terms of priorities.

We do not recommend using the Single-button Assistant as a tank, least of all for Blood. None of the defensive cooldowns are used by it, and this immediately means that you have to actively supplement it with additional actions.
