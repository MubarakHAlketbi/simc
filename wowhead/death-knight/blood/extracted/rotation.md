#### Single-Target Priority
BEST DEATH KNIGHT BLOOD SINGLE TARGET ROTATION
Blood Death Knight's single target rotation operates on a priority list, maintaining two crucial buffs and using resources to optimize Runic Power generation. Every GCD depends on the current state of resources and active abilities at your disposal. As a result, this section should be read as a priority list, and not a strict sequence.
The builds used below are covered in the Talents page. Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes.
 Deathbringer puts a strong emphasis on its signature ability,  Reaper's Mark, and the subsequent procs and effects it generates when it expires. The rotational gameplay is otherwise the same, with a much smaller focus on the management of  Bone Shield thanks to the charges  Reaper's Mark indirectly provides.
PRIORITY
Cast  Death Strike if you need the healing or are above 75 Runic PowerWhy 75?
Maintain at least 5 charges of  Bone Shield. If  Bone Shield is about to expire, press  Marrowrend Are there other generators?
Cast  Death and Decay and stand in it if you are not currently affected by the buff it provides.
Cast  Reaper's Mark on cooldown
Cast  Dancing Rune Weapon on cooldown
Cast  Blood Boil if  Dancing Rune Weapon is active and your target does not have  Blood Plague on it, or if you are about to cap on its charges
Cast  Blood Boil if you have a  Boiling Point proc
Cast  Blood Boil as a filler if you have a charge of it available
Cast  Heart Strike as a filler. Do not run out of runes, particularly around the 45-second mark when  Reaper's Mark is about to come off cooldown

DANCING RUNE WEAPON
 Dancing Rune Weapon grants you 20% parry for 12 seconds when cast, replicates a small subset of abilities ( Heart Strike,  Marrowrend,  Blood Boil and  Death Strike) and has two additional effects:
If you cast  Marrowrend while it is active, you get 3 bonus  Bone Shield for each  Dancing Rune Weapon active
If you cast  Blood Boil while it is active, each  Dancing Rune Weapon applies its own copy of  Blood Plague
You can empower this spell through other talents, granting you 5% bonus parry for the first 8 seconds of it, and up to 10 Runic Power per second for the first 8 seconds.  San'layn takes this up a notch by using this as their main activator for all Hero Talent mechanics.

BONE SHIELD MANAGEMENT
The largest remaining optimization around Blood's toolkit and rotation involves familiarizing yourself with  Bone Shield generators. Every 30 seconds, you will have to use one of them to refresh bones.

As  Deathbringer, when playing  Reaper's Onslaught, this is effectively dealt with for you. When playing  Dark Talons, you can choose to delay one  Exterminate explosion by ~5 seconds to fix this; alternatively, you can slot in a  Death Grip to refresh your entire stack.

 Bone Shield management is significantly harder as  San'layn, due to the hero talent not having any passive generation tools. You are left to your own devices - and to make things even worse, Apex talents have effectively randomized how many  Bone Shield charges a single  Marrowrend provides, since each Apex  Dancing Rune Weapon provides its own bonus three stacks. Proper UI tracking goes a long way towards solving this, but there is still an element of skill involved.

#### Opener

Your opener as a Blood Death Knight depends on your Hero Talent choice. In both cases, your goal is to set up as many maintenance buffs as you can before the fight starts to cut down on wasteful GCDs you would spend doing so otherwise.

In the following section, we will assume that you are in control of pulling and can spend two global cooldowns preparing things.
The builds used below are covered in the Talents page. Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes.

 Deathbringer's opener sets up three elements as quickly as possible: it primes  Death and Decay and  Bone Shield, and makes sure to prioritize  Reaper's Mark ahead of  Dancing Rune Weapon. #### Pre-Combat Checks
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Whilst Blood has no strict pre-combat checklist of group buffs and personal temporary buffs, there are still a couple of valuable resources you can prepare before a fight starts. We have detailed them below:
RAID
Check Consumables - Flask, food, augment rune, temporary weapon oils or whetstones are worth having at all times.
MYTHIC+
Conserve Resources Between Pulls -  Bone Shield persists between pulls as long as it has time remaining on it, and Runic Power decays at a rate of 1 RP/second out of combat. This allows you to keep  Bone Shield rolling by refreshing it at the end of a pull, and to pool some Runic Power to kickstart your next pull. Skip the maintenance buff minigame by getting ahead of these two resources!
Key Start Optimization - The start of a key leaves your  Bone Shield charges intact and leaves up to 25 Runic Power when the key starts. You can prepare both resources by hitting enemies or target dummies before the key is inserted if you are not in combat when it starts.
