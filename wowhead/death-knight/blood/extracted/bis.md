Craft  Blood Knight's Warblade with  Darkmoon Sigil: Hunt  or  Darkmoon Sigil: Void  Explanation 
Craft  Spellbreaker's Bracers with  Primal Spore Binding  (general purpose) or  Loa Worshiper's Band (if you have at least one other gem slot; we really recommend not touching this one just yet as it is very likely to get nerfed) Explanation
