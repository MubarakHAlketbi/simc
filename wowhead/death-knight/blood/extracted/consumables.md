Blood Death Knight Gems
In general, you will want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your unique Jewelecrafting gem. Unlike prior expansions, this gem does not require a number of other gems to empower it further.

All other gem slots should be filled with whichever gem provides the most value for you. As a sensible default,  San'layn players may opt for  Flawless Quick Garnet , while  Deathbringer players should opt for  Flawless Masterful Garnet .
Blood Death Knight Weapon Enchant
The best temporary weapon enchant for Blood Death Knights is  Thalassian Phoenix Oil , due to the sheer bonus value it provides compared to whetstones.
 BEST BLOOD DEATH KNIGHT CONSUMABLES IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
There are many consumables that players can use in combat in Midnight. Here are the best flasks, potions, and weapon buffs that Blood Death Knights can use in PvE content.

There is a small difference in stat priority for San'layn specifically: Whenever there is a choice of secondary stats, if you are a San'layn player, you will likely want to prioritize Haste (or crit if you already have high Haste). If you are a Deathbringer, you'll likely prioritize anything but Haste.

To be sure of this, you should ideally be simming your character using raidbots to determine the best configuration for you.

Also note that consumables are being actively hotfixed, particularly combat potions.  Potion of Recklessness  is currently far ahead, but this may not stay true for long.
 Blood Death Knight Flask
The best Flask for Blood Death Knights is  Flask of the Blood Knights  or  Flask of the Shattered Sun . Unlike prior expansions, no consumables or other effects are tied to the use of a flask.

If you are specifically after damage reduction in order to guarantee survival on something, and you are certain that a flask will offset this,  Flask of Thalassian Resistance  is available. In addition to this, stacking versatility to just enough so that it is your first stat by rating allows you to use  Potion of Recklessness  as a direct defensive cooldown.
 Blood Death Knight Combat Potions
There are a couple of different potions that have different useful scenarios for Blood Death Knights:
Raiding:  Potion of Recklessness / Light's Potential 
Mythic+:  Potion of Recklessness / Light's Potential 
If you are after a direct damage reduction solution to a predictable upfront hit, and you are willing to forsake some general throughput and indirect defensive value from other stats, stacking versatility to just enough so that it is your first stat by rating allows you to use  Light's Potential  as a direct defensive cooldown.

 Potion of Zealotry  can be used in pure single target scenarios. This needs to not feature an additional target, as any non-periodic hit on any other target than the one the debuff is being stacked on causes the entire stack to fade - and the potential of the potion to plummet.
 Blood Death Knight Health Potions
The only healing potion available for Blood Death Knights is  Silvermoon Health Potion .

If you have a warlock in your group, don't forget your healthstones!
 Blood Death Knight Weapon Buff
The best temporary weapon enchant for Blood Death Knights is  Thalassian Phoenix Oil , due to the sheer bonus value it provides compared to whetstones.
 Blood Death Knight Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Blood Death Knight Food
Blood Death Knights have two choices at their disposal in Midnight: a primary stat feast, and a secondary stat feast. The secondary stat feat gives slightly more rating and will typically be better, but this is not always guaranteed.
Primary stat:  Harandar Celebration
Secondary stats:  Blooming Feast
We strongly recommend simming these choices.
