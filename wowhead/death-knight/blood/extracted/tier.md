Tier sets are unique class armor sets that provide powerful spec-specific bonuses when multiple pieces of the set are worn. These bonuses will change based on your character's active talent specialization, ensuring that the bonus is appropriate and valuable to your spec.

This guide provides relevant information about your Blood Death Knight tier, including how the tier bonuses work, their effect on the Blood Death Knight playstyle, how to acquire tier set pieces, and what gearing strategy you should be using.

 TIER SET BONUSES FOR BLOOD DEATH KNIGHT
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Blood Death Knight gear page!

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Blood Death Knight
Our Season 1 tier set is completely passive, and buffs two core rotational buttons- it brings back a long-lost effect by making  Blood Boil generate Runic Power, and attempts to ham-fist some synergy by giving  Death Strike a borderline unnoticeable chance to grant a bonus charge of  Blood Boil.

Similarly to every expansion, this tier set acts as a "welcome to Midnight" act by being a passive gain, and while some talents benefit more from it, they are already default picks.
Relentless Rider's Lament
 Death Knight Blood 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Blood Boil deals 8% bonus damage and each cast (including Boiling Point echoes) grants 3 Runic Power.
 Death Knight Blood 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Death Strike deals 8% bonus damage and each cast has a 10% chance to grant an additional Blood Boil charge.

Best Tier Set Pieces for Blood Death Knight
Our tier set bonus, Relentless Rider's Lament, is comprised of the following items from Midnight Season 1:
 Relentless Rider's Crown (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)
 Relentless Rider's Dreadthorns (Shoulders from Fallen-King Salhadaar - The Voidspire)
 Relentless Rider's Cuirass (Chest from Chimaerus - The Dreamrift)
 Relentless Rider's Bonegrasps (Gloves from Vorasius - The Voidspire)
 Relentless Rider's Legguards (Legs from Vaelgor & Ezzorak - The Voidspire)
Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to the presence of a cantrip pair of gloves, for raid, Blood Death Knights will prefer the following pieces at all times:
Helm
Legs
Shoulders
Chest
For AoE, where the value of  Voidclaw Gauntlets is noticeably weaker,  Deathbringer players may choose to eschew Shoulders, but the difference is small.

This is a minor optimization, and assumes that you already have  Voidclaw Gauntlets at maximum Myth track upgrade level.
Tier Set Bonus Rotation Changes for Blood Death Knight
There are no rotational changes brought forward by the tier set. The chance to refund a  Blood Boil charge on the 4-piece is too small to actively play around, and while there is a damage amplification effect on  Blood Boil, the 4-piece also adds one to  Death Strike - rendering any potential rotational deviation for  San'layn a loss.

You will simply have slightly more Runic Power to spend, magnified further by Boiling Point procs.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR BLOOD DEATH KNIGHT TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
