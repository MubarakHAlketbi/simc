 Further Details

Unlike  San'layn,  Deathbringer provides a mostly plug-and-play experience in Mythic+. It does so at the cost of a significant amount of single-target and AoE damage, but it in turn also brings defensive perks that are difficult to ignore: an entire 7.5% damage reduction, significantly more healing from  Blood Plague, and a completely pain-free  Bone Shield experience.

For Mythic+, we defaulted to  Reaper's Onslaught. It is worse in single target and low target-count scenarios, but it does shine in large AoE and simplifies the spec even further.

We have provided two builds, with one of them featuring  Umbilicus Eternus for ease of use. Unlike previous expansions, this is a relatively cost-free choice.
