Unholy Death Knight Flask in Midnight Season 1
The best Flask for Unholy Death Knights is  Flask of the Shattered Sun , though  Flask of the Magisters  is an option if you currently have low Mastery levels and need a bit of a boost.
Unholy Death Knight Combat Potions in Midnight Season 1
 Potion of Recklessness  is always the best option for Unholy Death Knights, as our love for secondary stats knows no bounds. If  Draught of Rampant Abandon  is cheaper, it can be used instead, as Strength is still plenty valuable for us.
Unholy Death Knight Health Potions in Midnight Season 1
The best healing potion for Unholy Death Knights is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Unholy Death Knight Weapon Buff in Midnight Season 1
For their weapons, Unholy Death Knights have a few options. The best one is generally  Thalassian Phoenix Oil , as the secondary stats this provides are quite good. Alternatively, you can use  Refulgent Whetstone  or  Refulgent Weightstone , depending on your weapon type. Make sure that you buy the correct one - Whetstone for Swords and Axes, Weightstone for Maces.
Unholy Death Knight Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Unholy Death Knight Food in Midnight Season 1
Unholy Death Knights are most likely using primary stat foods in Midnight. As always, sim your character to check which food is the best for your configuration of secondary stats.
