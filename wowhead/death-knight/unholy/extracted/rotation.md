ARMY OF THE DEAD‎
 Army of the Dead is our biggest cooldown and serves as a trigger for many other talents. The most prominent among those would be  Raise Abomination,  Summon Gargoyle, and  Apocalypse Now, where this ability will summon additional pets to fight for us.

With the sheer quantity of pets being summoned by this ability, if you have talented  Commander of the Dead, it's vital to ensure that  Army of the Dead and  Dark Transformation stay in sync, so you are using both at the same time. Generally, this will mean ensuring you hit  Dark Transformation as soon as possible after it comes off cooldown once between every  Army of the Dead cast.

#### Dark Transformation
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before entering combat, it is important for Unholy Death Knights to do the following. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Summon Timmy - Make sure to summon your primary pet (Timmy) by casting  Raise Dead.

DARK TRANSFORMATION‎
Similar to Army of the Dead, Dark Transformation also triggers several other talents. The most important among these are  Commander of the Dead,  Outnumber, and  Ride or Die!.

With Commander of the Dead, it is particularly important to keep your cooldowns synchronized. With Dark Transformation being an off-global cooldown ability, you can safely macro this into Army of the Dead and only have to worry about a single use between each Army window.

In addition to this, if you talent Outnumber, it becomes incredibly important to try to time your Dark Transformation with add spawns, as the Lesser Ghoul  Sweeping Claws attacks are incredibly potent, and will constitute a major portion of our AoE damage potential.

#### Putrefy
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before entering combat, it is important for Unholy Death Knights to do the following. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Summon Timmy - Make sure to summon your primary pet (Timmy) by casting  Raise Dead.

PUTREFY‎
 Putrefy is a fairly unique ability in how it should be used. With other talents allowing you to trigger Putrefy, some of which even use the charges you have for you. This is usually a short-duration cooldown, averaging about 15 seconds between uses, making it fairly rotational.

The major talents to consider for when to use Putrefy are  Soul Reaper (in addition to  Reaping).

Now, there is a pair of talents that can throw a wrench into this logic with  Soul Reaper and  Reaping. Due to Soul Reaper consuming all your current Putrefy charges, it can be worth not pressing Putrefy if Soul Reaper will be available to use in the next 15 seconds or so. While your target is below 35% health, this will mean you never manually press Putrefy.

Outside of this, if you talent Reaping, you should try to bank a charge for Dark Transformation windows to save you the global and rune you would have spent on Putrefy otherwise. It is important to note that trying to bank 2 charges of Putrefy should never be done, as that will end up wasting cooldown duration and cooldown reduction duration on Putrefy, leading to lost casts.
