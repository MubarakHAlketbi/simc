## ~~~~ Unholy Death Knight Overview

Unholy is a Two-Handed, plate-wearing DPS specialization that focuses on diseases and pets. We summon armies of undead minions and infect our enemies with multiple plagues. We are generally a very bursty spec in both single-target and AoE. New Unholy Death Knights start at Level 8 instead of Level 1 since we are a Hero Class. All races can play Unholy Death Knights, except Dracthyr and Earthen. Unholy Death Knight is a spec for players who like big burst cooldowns and summoning lots of minions.

## Unholy Death Knight Hero Talents: Rider of the Apocalypse and San'layn

Both Rider of the Apocalypse and San'layn have their own niches in which they can excel. While San'layn is a 45-second cooldown focused Hero Talent tree, Rider contrasts this with its focus on 90-second windows instead.

In higher-end Mythic+ scenarios, this usually leans more towards the 45-second cycle, which San'layn achieves wonderfully, though it does require consistent build-up and burst. Comparatively, Rider of the Apocalypse does very well in its 90-second burst windows and doesn't require anywhere near as much setup and execution, leading to an easier time learning and playing the specialization.

In most raiding scenarios, the choice tends to be less clear. Depending on the fight, those 45-second or 90-second windows could be more valuable and will likely be something you swap between depending on the boss.

Defensively, San'layn's self-healing makes us nearly impossible to kill. On the other hand, Rider of the Apocalypse provides us with powerful mobility tools that Death Knights don't often have.

All in all, the choice is up to you, and neither will disappoint even in a scenario that isn't built to their strengths.

## ~~~~ Playing an Unholy Death Knight in Midnight Season 1

Unholy Death Knight primarily operates based on resources and current procs. This generally means that what we do at any given moment will be based on both of these factors, which makes a typical rotation out of the question, and instead operates on a priority list.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

## ~~~~ Baseline Unholy Death Knight Spells

### Defensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_raisedead.gif)Lichborne](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49039/lichborne) ( **LB**) \- Increase leech by 6% and causes you to become immune to Charm, Fear, and Sleep effects. It can also break these effects if they are already active. While transformed by Lichborne, we are able to utilize [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathcoil.gif)Death Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47541/death-coil) to heal ourselves.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_antimagicshell.gif)Anti-Magic Shell](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48707/anti-magic-shell) ( **AMS**) \- Become immune to magical DoT/debuff effects, and absorb magical damage done while active. Does not remove existing magical DoT/debuff effects when used, unless talented into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_nethercloak.gif)Unyielding Will](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=457574/unyielding-will). Any magical damage absorbed is converted into Runic Power at a rate of 0.833 Runic Power per 1% of player health absorbed.


### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_festering_strike.gif)Festering Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85948/festering-strike) ( **FS**) \- Deal Physical damage to an enemy, and apply 2-3 stacks of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_festering_strike.gif)Lesser Ghoul](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1254252/lesser-ghoul), allowing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_scourgestrike.gif)Scourge Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=55090/scourge-strike) to summon a [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ghoulnorthrend.gif)Lesser Ghoul](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=275430/lesser-ghoul).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathcoil.gif)Death Coil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=47541/death-coil) ( **DC**) \- Single-target Runic Power spending ability with a 30-yard range. Additionally, increases the duration of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_disease_02.gif)Virulent Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191587/virulent-plague), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_empowereddreadplague.gif)Dread Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240996/dread-plague) on the target hit.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deathanddecay.gif)Death and Decay](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=43265/death-and-decay) ( **DnD**) \- Ground-targeted AoE cooldown that deals minuscule damage.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_nullifydisease.gif)Epidemic](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=207317/epidemic) ( **Epi**) \- AoE Runic Power spending ability, which actually has a 40-yard range despite the the tooltip saying 30-yard range. Additionally, increases the duration of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_disease_02.gif)Virulent Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191587/virulent-plague), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_empowereddreadplague.gif)Dread Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240996/dread-plague) on all targets hit.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_darkconviction.gif)Rune Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=316239/rune-strike) \- Low level Rune spending ability, replaced by [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_festering_strike.gif)Festering Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=85948/festering-strike) when you reach level 10.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_creature_disease_02.gif)Virulent Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191587/virulent-plague) \- A Disease which can be applied to any number of targets. Has a 3 second period between ticks, which can not be reduced by Haste.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_empowereddreadplague.gif)Dread Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240996/dread-plague) \- A Disease which can be applied to only one target at a time. Has a 3 second period between ticks, which can not be reduced by Haste. Any enemies who die with this effect will trigger [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warlock_everlastingaffliction.gif)Dread Plague](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242564/dread-plague), dealing a significant amount of AoE damage, and applying Dread Plague to one of the targets hit.


### Utility Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_strangulate.gif)Death Grip](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49576/death-grip) ( **Grip**) \- Pull an enemy within 30 yards to your location.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_chainsofice.gif)Chains of Ice](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=45524/chains-of-ice) ( **Chains**) \- Slow an enemy for 8 seconds.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_demonicempathy.gif)Death's Advance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48265/deaths-advance) ( **DA**) \- Increase movement speed, and become immune to slow and forced movement effects for 10 seconds.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_shamanrage.gif)Dark Command](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=56222/dark-command) ( **Taunt**) \- Taunts the target, forcing them to attack you.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_pathoffrost.gif)Path of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=3714/path-of-frost) ( **PoF**) \- Freeze water below you and your party members within 50 yards, allowing them to walk on water for 10 minutes, or until they take damage.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_deadofnight.gif)Raise Ally](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=61999/raise-ally) ( **B Res**) \- Revive a fallen friendly player, useable in combat.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_teleportundercity.gif)Death Gate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50977/death-gate) ( **DG**) \- Create a gate to teleport back to Acherus, the Ebon Hold. Casting while within Acherus, the Ebon Hold will cause the gate to lead back to where the player last used Death Gate from.


### Passive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_skeer_bloodletting.gif)Dark Succor](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=178819/dark-succor) \- When you deal a killing blow to an enemy, it allows your next [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_butcher2.gif)Death Strike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=49998/death-strike) to cost no Runic Power and also increases its healing.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mount_undeadhorse.gif)On a Pale Horse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51986/on-a-pale-horse) \- Increases mounted movement speed by 20%.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_zone_westernplaguelands_01.gif)Mastery: Dreadblade](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77515/mastery-dreadblade) \- Increases player Shadow and minion damage based on Mastery rating. It does not apply to shadow damage effects that come from items or spells not in the player's spellbook.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_frozenruneweapon.gif)Runeforging](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=53428/runeforging) \- Allows engraving your weapons with Runeforge enchants at a Runeforge, which can be found in Acherus, the Ebon Hold.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_rune.gif)Runic Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=51462/runic-corruption) ( **RC**) \- Spending Runic Power has a chance to increase your Rune regeneration rate for 3 seconds, reduced by haste. At 100% haste, this would be 1.5 seconds. This ensures that each Runic Corruption proc will only generate up to a maximum of 0.9 Runes in total (0.3 for each regenerating Rune).

More Details~~~~



Clawing Shadows damage reduction per target hit works multiplicatively, where every target takes 20% less damage than the last target hit. This overall results in the following damage taken.













|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| Target N | Damage % |
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 80% |
| 3 | 64% |
| 4 | 51.2% |
| 5 | 40.96% |
| 6 | 32.768% |
| 7 | 26.2144% |











|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| N Targets | Total Damage% |
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 180% |
| 3 | 244% |
| 4 | 295.2% |
| 5 | 336.16% |
| 6 | 368.928% |
| 7 | 395.1424% |















So, with all that in mind, if you were to press Scourge Strike against 7 targets, and the first target hit (your main target) took 100 damage, the final target hit would take 26 damage. Overall, this effect means the damage does scale up with target count, but in such a way as to make sure we can never deal significant damage from Scourge Strike in AoE scenarios.

## Unholy Death Knight Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Unholy Death Knight primarily operates based on resources and current procs. This generally means that what we do at any given moment will be based on both of these factors, which makes a typical rotation out of the question, and instead operates on a priority list.

## ~~~~ Unholy Death Knight Major Cooldowns

Unholy has a few major cooldowns with mechanics that may make you want to use them at specific times. Below, there's a section to go over each of these, explaining when and why you should use them when you do.

Army of the Dead

Dark Transformation

Putrefy

### Army of the Dead‎

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_armyofthedead.gif)Army of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=42650/army-of-the-dead) is our biggest cooldown and serves as a trigger for many other talents. The most prominent among those would be [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_patchwerk.gif)Raise Abomination](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242608/raise-abomination), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_summongargoyle.gif)Summon Gargoyle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1242147/summon-gargoyle), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_rideroftheapocalypsedeathknight_apocalypsenow.gif)Apocalypse Now](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=444040/apocalypse-now), where this ability will summon additional pets to fight for us.

With the sheer quantity of pets being summoned by this ability, if you have talented [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_fumble.gif)Commander of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390259/commander-of-the-dead), it's **vital** to ensure that [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_armyofthedead.gif)Army of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=42650/army-of-the-dead) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_festergutrotface.gif)Dark Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233448/dark-transformation) stay in sync, so you are using both at the same time. Generally, this will mean ensuring you hit [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_festergutrotface.gif)Dark Transformation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233448/dark-transformation) as soon as possible after it comes off cooldown once between every [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_armyofthedead.gif)Army of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=42650/army-of-the-dead) cast.

### Dark Transformation‎

Similar to Army of the Dead, Dark Transformation also triggers several other talents. The most important among these are [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_fumble.gif)Commander of the Dead](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390259/commander-of-the-dead), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_darksummoning.gif)Outnumber](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1241705/outnumber), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/achievement_boss_fourhorsemen.gif)Ride or Die!](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265959/ride-or-die).

With Commander of the Dead, it is particularly important to keep your cooldowns synchronized. With Dark Transformation being an off-global cooldown ability, you can safely macro this into Army of the Dead and only have to worry about a single use between each Army window.

In addition to this, if you talent Outnumber, it becomes incredibly important to try to time your Dark Transformation with add spawns, as the Lesser Ghoul [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathknight_thrash_ghoul.gif)Sweeping Claws](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1278150/sweeping-claws) attacks are incredibly potent, and will constitute a major portion of our AoE damage potential.

### Putrefy‎

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_deathknight_putrefy.gif)Putrefy](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247378/putrefy) is a fairly unique ability in how it should be used. With other talents allowing you to trigger Putrefy, some of which even use the charges you have for you. This is usually a short-duration cooldown, averaging about 15 seconds between uses, making it fairly rotational.

The major talents to consider for when to use Putrefy are [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_soulreaper.gif)Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294/soul-reaper) (in addition to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathwing_bloodcorruption_death.gif)Reaping](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=377514/reaping)).

Now, there is a pair of talents that can throw a wrench into this logic with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_soulreaper.gif)Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294/soul-reaper) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathwing_bloodcorruption_death.gif)Reaping](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=377514/reaping). Due to Soul Reaper consuming all your current Putrefy charges, it can be worth not pressing Putrefy if Soul Reaper will be available to use in the next 15 seconds or so. While your target is below 35% health, this will mean you never manually press Putrefy.

Outside of this, if you talent Reaping, you should try to bank a charge for Dark Transformation windows to save you the global and rune you would have spent on Putrefy otherwise. It is important to note that trying to bank 2 charges of Putrefy should never be done, as that will end up wasting cooldown duration and cooldown reduction duration on Putrefy, leading to lost casts.

### Unholy Death Knight Flask in Midnight Season 1

The best Flask for Unholy Death Knights is [![Flask of the Shattered Sun](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_flask_sindoreipotion_red--.gif)Flask of the Shattered Sun![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241326/flask-of-the-shattered-sun), though [![Flask of the Magisters](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_flask_sindoreipotion_black.gif)Flask of the Magisters![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241322/flask-of-the-magisters) is an option if you currently have low Mastery levels and need a bit of a boost.

### Unholy Death Knight Combat Potions in Midnight Season 1

[![Potion of Recklessness](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_voidpotion_red.gif)Potion of Recklessness![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241288/potion-of-recklessness) is always the best option for Unholy Death Knights, as our love for secondary stats knows no bounds. ### Unholy Death Knight Weapon Buff in Midnight Season 1

For their weapons, Unholy Death Knights have a few options. The best one is generally [![Thalassian Phoenix Oil](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_enchanting_manaoil_red.gif)Thalassian Phoenix Oil![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=243734/thalassian-phoenix-oil), as the secondary stats this provides are quite good. Alternatively, you can use [![Refulgent Whetstone](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_blacksmithing_whetstones_silver.gif)Refulgent Whetstone![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=237371/refulgent-whetstone) or [![Refulgent Weightstone](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_blacksmithing_weightstone_silver.gif)Refulgent Weightstone![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=237369/refulgent-weightstone), depending on your weapon type. Make sure that you buy the correct one - Whetstone for Swords and Axes, Weightstone for Maces.

## ~~~~ Talent Build for Unholy Death Knight Beginners

For beginner Unholy Death Knights, Rider of the Apocalypse is generally the best hero talent choice you can make. This is due to the simplicty of Riders of the Apocalypse, having no rotational consideration at all. In addition to this, the build avoids adding a few extra buttons to the rotation by skipping out on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_soulreaper.gif)Soul Reaper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343294/soul-reaper), and taking [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_unstableaffliction_3_purple.gif)Blightburst](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1254552/blightburst) to avoid having to press [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_deathvortex.gif)Outbreak](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77575/outbreak) to apply our diseases.

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Unholy Death Knight, these tools are essentially perfect. The assisted highlight and one-button rotation modes use all of our cooldowns, have solid rotational priority logic, and will generally consider what a top player would be pressing next. The only things you need to consider for yourself are your non-Unholy specific buttons, such as combat potions, on-use racials, and on-use trinkets.

The **Wowhead Client** is a little application we use to keep our database up to date, and to provide you with some nifty extra functionality on the website!

1. It maintains a WoW addon called the **Wowhead Looter**, which collects data as you play the game!

2. It uploads the **collected data** to Wowhead in order to keep the database up-to-date!


You can also use it to keep track of your completed quests, recipes, mounts, companion pets, and titles!
