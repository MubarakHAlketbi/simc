COMBUSTION
All spells Critically Strike during  Combustion, making you a  Hot Streak! machine gun. It has many interactions that either grant other buffs, increase proc chance, speed up cooldown recovery, or boost damage. Key takeaways are:
 Combustion can be cast while casting. Always cast a hard-cast into  Combustion, but be sure to activate it before the cast completes. Viable spells are:
 Pyroblast or  Flamestrike (AoE) with a  Pyroclasm proc.
 Frostfire Bolt if Frostfire.
 Scorch if Sunfury.
 Fireball.
When playing  Firestarter,  Combustion should be delayed until the target reaches 90% health (Typically around 18 seconds).
If not playing  Firestarter,  Combustion should still be delayed on the pull (Around 8-10 seconds).
We do this to pre-stack buffs like  Fired Up and because  Fire Blast charges are plentiful.
After the opener,  Combustion should generally be used on cooldown. Sometimes you will delay  Combustion for better overlaps with AoE, Damage amps, or to avoid forced downtime. Plan ahead so you don't end up delaying and having it backfire, by costing you a use.
 Fired Up is our new Apex talent. It has a chance to proc when you spend  Hot Streak!.
 Combustion can be extended by  Fired Up procs.
During  Combustion, your first 3  Fired Up procs are almost guaranteed.
 Fiery Rush speeds up the  Fire Blast cooldown during  Combustion.
Critical Strike is not useless during  Combustion.  Burn It All and  Slow Burn convert Crit into other effects. Unfortunately, this does not boost Critical Strike much.
When  Combustion ends,  Burnout causes all targets with an active  Mastery: Ignite to take extra damage. This damage depends on the "size" of the Ignite.
To maximize  Burnout in Single Target,  Meteor should be used within 10 seconds of  Combustion ending. This buff gets randomly extended, so shoot for <8 seconds remaining to give yourself some wiggle room.
For  Sunfury the  Combustion window is longer, summons an  Arcane Phoenix, and ends with  Hyperthermia:
 Hyperthermia is a buff that allows you to rapidly fire  Pyroblast or  Flamestrike casts.
These casts are free, guaranteed to Crit, and ramp in damage.
You also gain a Haste buff from  Ashes of Inspiration during this time.
For  Frostfire the  Combustion window is shorter and has no follow-up buffs.

DOUBLE PYROBLAST AND FINDING YOUR RHYTHM
This guide has many mentions to "Double Pyroblast". When a  Fireball (or other cast) completes, and is followed by an instant  Pyroblast, both spells go off at the same time. If both Critically Strike, you will gain another  Hot Streak!. Normally this is hard to predict, but  Firestarter and  Combustion make it guaranteed. This works because two spells were in flight, and two spells Crit. If two spells Crit at the same time, you skip  Heating Up, straight to  Hot Streak!. That is Double Pyroblast.

The beauty of Double Pyroblast is that it places the travel time of the spells during Global Cooldown, meaning that you won't lost time waiting for them to hit. There are many ways you can pull this off, but the key factors are:
You are guaranteed to Crit.
You are currently casting a spell that can generate  Hot Streak! ( Fireball,  Scorch, etc.).
You have  Hot Streak! in hand before the cast completes.
You queue a  Hot Streak! spender ( Pyroblast or  Flamestrike) as the cast completes.

#### Firestarter Examples
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Fire Mage to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Arcane Intellect to buff you and your party with Intellect.
Apply Your Shield - Use  Blazing Barrier to give yourself an absorb shield.
Threat Protection (Optional) - Use  Mirror Image right before combat to help your Tank establish threat.

COMBUSTION
All spells Critically Strike during  Combustion, making you a  Hot Streak! machine gun. It has many interactions that either grant other buffs, increase proc chance, speed up cooldown recovery, or boost damage. Key takeaways are:
 Combustion can be cast while casting. Always cast a hard-cast into  Combustion, but be sure to activate it before the cast completes. Viable spells are:
 Pyroblast or  Flamestrike (AoE) with a  Pyroclasm proc.
 Frostfire Bolt if Frostfire.
 Scorch if Sunfury.
 Fireball.
When playing  Firestarter,  Combustion should be delayed until the target reaches 90% health (Typically around 18 seconds).
If not playing  Firestarter,  Combustion should still be delayed on the pull (Around 8-10 seconds).
We do this to pre-stack buffs like  Fired Up and because  Fire Blast charges are plentiful.
After the opener,  Combustion should generally be used on cooldown. Sometimes you will delay  Combustion for better overlaps with AoE, Damage amps, or to avoid forced downtime. Plan ahead so you don't end up delaying and having it backfire, by costing you a use.
 Fired Up is our new Apex talent. It has a chance to proc when you spend  Hot Streak!.
 Combustion can be extended by  Fired Up procs.
During  Combustion, your first 3  Fired Up procs are almost guaranteed.
 Fiery Rush speeds up the  Fire Blast cooldown during  Combustion.
Critical Strike is not useless during  Combustion.  Burn It All and  Slow Burn convert Crit into other effects. Unfortunately, this does not boost Critical Strike much.
When  Combustion ends,  Burnout causes all targets with an active  Mastery: Ignite to take extra damage. This damage depends on the "size" of the Ignite.
To maximize  Burnout in Single Target,  Meteor should be used within 10 seconds of  Combustion ending. This buff gets randomly extended, so shoot for <8 seconds remaining to give yourself some wiggle room.
For  Sunfury the  Combustion window is longer, summons an  Arcane Phoenix, and ends with  Hyperthermia:
 Hyperthermia is a buff that allows you to rapidly fire  Pyroblast or  Flamestrike casts.
These casts are free, guaranteed to Crit, and ramp in damage.
You also gain a Haste buff from  Ashes of Inspiration during this time.
For  Frostfire the  Combustion window is shorter and has no follow-up buffs.

DOUBLE PYROBLAST AND FINDING YOUR RHYTHM
This guide has many mentions to "Double Pyroblast". When a  Fireball (or other cast) completes, and is followed by an instant  Pyroblast, both spells go off at the same time. If both Critically Strike, you will gain another  Hot Streak!. Normally this is hard to predict, but  Firestarter and  Combustion make it guaranteed. This works because two spells were in flight, and two spells Crit. If two spells Crit at the same time, you skip  Heating Up, straight to  Hot Streak!. That is Double Pyroblast.

The beauty of Double Pyroblast is that it places the travel time of the spells during Global Cooldown, meaning that you won't lost time waiting for them to hit. There are many ways you can pull this off, but the key factors are:
You are guaranteed to Crit.
You are currently casting a spell that can generate  Hot Streak! ( Fireball,  Scorch, etc.).
You have  Hot Streak! in hand before the cast completes.
You queue a  Hot Streak! spender ( Pyroblast or  Flamestrike) as the cast completes.
