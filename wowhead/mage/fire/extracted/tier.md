 TIER SET BONUSES FOR FIRE MAGE
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Fire Mage
The Fire Mage tier set is simple but effective. The 2-piece bonus buffs  Pyroblast and  Flamestrike, and the 4-piece bonus gives you more  Fire Blast charges to increase your  Hot Streak! generation. These are ideal bonuses, and this tier set is fairly strong because of it (around a 6.7% damage increase).
Voidbreaker's Accordance
 Mage Fire 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Pyroblast and Flamestrike deal 8% increased damage.
 Mage Fire 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Casting Pyroblast or Flamestrike reduces the cooldown of Fire Blast by 0.50 sec.






Best Tier Set Pieces for Fire Mage
Our tier set bonus, Voidbreaker's Accordance, is comprised of the following items from Midnight Season 1:
 Voidbreaker's Veil (Helm from Lightblinded Vanguard - The Voidspire)

Additional item slots will be available for transmog from the Great Vault or once the Catalyst crafting system becomes available, but those don't contribute to the tier set bonus.

To unlock the tier set bonus, you'll need to equip 4 pieces of the tier set. Due to their itemization, Fire Mage prefer equipping tier pieces on these slots:

Ideally, you'll have Chest as your off-piece as the tier option is poorly itemized for Fire Mage.

This is only a minor optimization; wearing five pieces of the tier is fine if you don't have a high item-level alternative. Fully Best-In-Slot setups will often choose to replace the Chest piece instead. Many options are possible for gearing on Fire, and we urge players to simulate themselves for more accurate answers.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR FIRE MAGE TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
