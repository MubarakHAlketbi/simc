 BEST FIRE MAGE GEMS AND ENCHANTS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below is a list of the recommended gems and enchants to use on your gear during Midnight Season 1.
Slot	Best Enchant
Weapon	 Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei

Legs	 Sunfire Silk Spellthread 
Boots	 Enchant Boots - Lynx's Dexterity 
Rings	 Enchant Ring - Eyes of the Eagle 
Gems	One  Powerful Eversong Diamond  and a mix of  Flawless Masterful Peridot  and  Flawless Quick Amethyst  with one  Flawless Quick Lapis .
Fire Mage Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Powerful Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond, and a combination of at least 1 Peridot, 1 Lapis, 1 Garnet, and 1 Amethyst, then  Flawless Quick Amethyst  in all other sockets.
Peridot:  Flawless Masterful Peridot 
Amethyst:  Flawless Quick Amethyst 
Garnet:  Flawless Quick Garnet 
Lapis:  Flawless Quick Lapis 
As the damage increase provided by  Powerful Eversong Diamond  is low, and some Fire Mages may opt for  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  instead, since it does not require as many gem slots. As always, sim yourself.
Fire Mage Weapon Enchant
Early on,  Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei  is going to be your weapon enchant. As the expansion goes on, secondary stat-based weapon enchants will likely pull ahead.

Fire Mage Flask
The best Flask for Fire Mages is  Flask of the Magisters , though  Flask of the Blood Knights  can be better depending on your stats.  Flask of Thalassian Resistance  is also a decent option for  Pyroclasm builds (if they get buffed).
Fire Mage Combat Potions
When it comes to combat potions, Fire Mage uses  Light's Potential .
Fire Mage Health Potions
The best healing potion for Fire Mages is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Fire Mage Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Fire Mages will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Fire Mage Augment Rune
In Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune. These provide a small gain, but are likely to be expensive. Augment runes are not necessary for most players!
Fire Mage Food
In Midnight, personal stat food is no better than feast buffs. Intellect food is equivalent to Secondary stats. As always, sim your character to check which food is the best for your configuration of Secondary stats.
Feast:  Harandar Celebration and  Quel'dorei Medley are equal
Personal Food:  Champion's Bento
In addition to food, you can also consume any tea (ex:  Sanguithorn Tea) for a movement speed increase that stacks with your food buff.
