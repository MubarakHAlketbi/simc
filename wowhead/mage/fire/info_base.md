## ~~~~ Baseline Fire Mage Spells

All spells will have a basic description; click the ~~~~ icon for a more in-depth explanation of each spell.

### Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_flamebolt.gif)Fireball](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=133/fireball) ( **FB**) \- Hurl a fiery ball that deals moderate damage at the target. Details ~~~~

This is your filler ability. Typically you use it to fish for Critical Strikes in order to generate [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_hotstreak.gif)Heating Up](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48107/heating-up).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireball.gif)Fire Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=319836/fire-blast) ( **IB**) \- Instant cast spell that deals damage. Fire Mage talents heavily modify this ability. Details ~~~~

With Fire talents, this ability gains charges, can be cast while casting, and always Critically Strikes. This makes it perfect to secure the second Critical Strike needed for [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_hotstreak.gif)Heating Up](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48107/heating-up) to become [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ironmaidens_grapeshotblast.gif)Hot Streak!](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48108/hot-streak).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_wispsplode.gif)Arcane Explosion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1449/arcane-explosion) ( **AE**) \- Instant cast. Deal Arcane damage around your character. Details ~~~~

This ability has poor damage, but it can be useful when you only need a little damage on a lot of targets. It can also be useful to check for invisible enemies. It has a very high [![Mana](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_elemental_mote_mana.gif)Mana](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/mana-0) cost.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_frostbolt02.gif)Frostbolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116/frostbolt) \- Launch a frosty bolt that deals moderate Frost damage and slows enemies. Details ~~~~

Though not used often, this ability can be useful to apply a slow. It is a decent way to begin combat while leveling. Damage is decent initially, but falls off quick once you level up and unlock more Fire talents.

## Fire Mage Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Fire Mage gameplay is simple with very few skills, but most of the difficulty of Fire comes down to execution. You will often be reacting based on whether one of your spells scores a Critical Strike, which grants [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_hotstreak.gif)Heating Up](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48107/heating-up). This can be converted with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireball.gif)Fire Blast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=108853/fire-blast) into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ironmaidens_grapeshotblast.gif)Hot Streak!](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48108/hot-streak) for instant cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_fire_fireball02.gif)Pyroblast](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=11366/pyroblast) s.
