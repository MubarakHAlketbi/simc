For maximum damage, you will want to use  Powerful Eversong Diamond  as your Thalassian Diamond, and a combination of at least 1 Amethyst, 1 Garnet, 1 Peridot, and 1 Lapis.

Frost Mage Weapon Enchant

Frost Mage Flask
The best Flask for Frost Mages is  Flask of the Magisters .
Frost Mage Combat Potions
While there are many options for potions, Frost Mages will always use  Light's Potential .
Frost Mage Health Potions
The best healing potion for Frost Mages is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Frost Mage Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Frost Mages will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Frost Mage Augment Rune
With The War Within, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune. If you've farmed enough Renown, you can also use  Ethereal Augment Rune or  Soulgorged Augment Rune.
Frost Mage Food
In Midnight, Frost Mages are most likely using personal stat food instead of feast buffs because the large item level jump has reduced the value of Intellect relative to Secondary stats. As always, sim your character to check which food is the best for your configuration of Secondary stats.
Feast:  Harandar Celebration
Personal Food:  Champion's Bento
In addition to food, you can also consume tea for a gathering profession and movement speed increase that stacks with your food buff.
