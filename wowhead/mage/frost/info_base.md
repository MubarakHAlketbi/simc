## ~~~~Frost Mage Overview

Frost Mage is a cloth wearing ranged damage dealer with a proc-based playstyle that excels at damaging and kiting stacked enemies. With constant slows applied by most of their spells, Frost Mages often have an easy time pulling and kiting large numbers of enemies on their own. In addition, Mages as a class have some of the best personal utility in the game, although they may still struggle to survive without a healer sometimes. Examples of this utility include mobility spells like Blink and defensive cooldowns like Ice Block.

This allows the Mage to avoid damage from falling and to increase the distance traveled before landing after jumping or being knocked back.

## Frost Mage Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Frost Mage is a relatively straightforward specialization in Midnight. While there are nuances to learn, the rotation is mainly based on consuming stacks of a debuff on your targets, keeping cooldowns running, and reacting to some random procs.

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Utilize Shatter:** Avoid wasting stacks of the [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_mage_freezing.gif)Freezing](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1221389/freezing) debuff. You can consume them and trigger [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_mage_freezing.gif)Shatter](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1246769/shatter) with some spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_frostblast.gif)Ice Lance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=30455/ice-lance) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_mage_cometstorm2.gif)Comet Storm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1247777/comet-storm). |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Utilize your Major Cooldowns:** [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_rayoffrost.gif)Ray of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=205021/ray-of-frost) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_frozenorb.gif)Frozen Orb](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=84714/frozen-orb) deal a lot of damage and should be used often. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Use Procs and Minor Cooldowns:** Spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_deathknight_chillstreak.gif)Flurry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=44614/flurry) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_glacialspike.gif)Glacial Spike](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199786/glacial-spike) are powerful and you'll want to use them often. In AoE, this also includes [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_icestorm.gif)Blizzard](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190356/blizzard). You will sometimes get procs like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_wintersgrasp.gif)Fingers of Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=112965/fingers-of-frost) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_brainfreeze.gif)Brain Freeze](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190447/brain-freeze) that you'll typically want to spend quickly. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Always be Casting:** It's important to always be doing something. Even if the spells you are casting are suboptimal, doing so is significantly better than casting nothing while trying to decide what to do. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_frostbolt02.gif)Frostbolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=116/frostbolt)/ [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_frostfiremage_frostfirebolt.gif)Frostfire Bolt](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=431044/frostfire-bolt) is the main filler spell here for Frost Mage and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_frostblast.gif)Ice Lance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=30455/ice-lance) can be used while moving. | |
