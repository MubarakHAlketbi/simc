 BEST ARCANE MAGE GEMS AND ENCHANTS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below is a list of the recommended gems and enchants to use on your gear during Midnight Season 1.
Slot	Best
Weapon	 Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei 
Head	 Enchant Helm - Empowered Rune of Avoidance 
Shoulders	 Enchant Shoulders - Silvermoon's Mending 
Chest	 Enchant Chest - Mark of the Worldsoul 
Legs	 Arcanoweave Spellthread 
Boots	 Enchant Boots - Shaladrassil's Roots 
Ring	 Enchant Ring - Eyes of the Eagle 
Eversong Diamond	 Powerful Eversong Diamond 
Other Gems	 Flawless Quick Amethyst 
Arcane Mage Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Powerful Eversong Diamond  as your Eversong Diamond, and a combination of at least 1 of each other gem type, then  Flawless Quick Amethyst  in all remaining sockets. As always though, sim for best results!
Amethyst:  Flawless Quick Amethyst 
Peridot:  Flawless Masterful Peridot 
Garnet:  Flawless Masterful Garnet 
Lapis:  Flawless Masterful Lapis 
Arcane Mage Weapon Enchant
 Enchant Weapon - Acuity of the Ren'dorei  is most likely going to be the best weapon enchant option for people, however any one of the other max level options are just as well off.
 BEST ARCANE MAGE CONSUMABLES IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below, we've listed the best flasks, potions, and weapon buffs that Arcane Mages can use in PvE content. While Haste is generally recommended as the safest option for gearing characters and for the added benefit of smoothing out gameplay feel, you should ideally be simming your character using raidbots to determine the best configuration for you.

Type	Best
Flask	 Flask of Thalassian Resistance 
Combat Potion	 Light's Potential 
Health Potion	 Silvermoon Health Potion 
Weapon Buff	 Thalassian Phoenix Oil 
Augment Rune	 Void-Touched Augment Rune
Food	 Silvermoon Parade and  Sanguithorn Tea (they stack!)
Arcane Mage Flask
The best Flask for Arcane Mages is  Flask of Thalassian Resistance , overall the other options are completely useable too though.
Arcane Mage Combat Potions
While there are many options for potions, Arcane Mages will always use  Light's Potential .
Arcane Mage Health Potions
The best healing potion for Arcane Mages is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Arcane Mage Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Arcane Mages will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Arcane Mage Augment Rune
The new Augment Rune this expansion is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Arcane Mage Food
We'll just use whatever max level primary stat food is available for this season! In addition to food, you can also consume any tea (ex:  Sanguithorn Tea) for a movement speed increase that stacks with your food buff.
Feast:  Silvermoon Parade
Personal Food: Anything that gives max stats is basically useable, sim for best results!
