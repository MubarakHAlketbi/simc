#### Overall BiS
Best in Slot Gear for Shadow Priest
This best-in-slot set aims to combine overbudget items from the raid (such as  Eye of Midnight), with haste-heavy offpieces. You should end up with haste in most slots.

1. Be very careful that you do not craft a Strength or Agility weapon. It will put you behind a significant amount of time.

If you will not get myth track items or need the power increase to progress the content then craft, but hold off as long as you can to avoid having to replace items and waste crests or sparks.
