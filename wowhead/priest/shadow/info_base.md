## ~~~~ Shadow Priest Overview

Shadow Priest is a ranged, cloth-wearing DPS specialization with a mix of direct damage and Damage over Time (DoT) abilities. The spec predominantly focuses on dealing Shadow damage, and most of its defensive capabilities are linked to its self-healing. Priests, in general, are very slow, and Shadow is no exception to this trend. With no baseline displacement or movement abilities, you'll have to plan movement in encounters ahead of time, as well as use the few kiting tools you have available to their maximum advantage. In addition to empowering their own damage, Shadow Priests also have the ability to empower the damage of their allies and are a DPS spec focused on dealing the majority of their damage output in burst windows every minute with numerous, stacked cooldowns.

## Shadow Priest Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Shadow Priest operates on a single resource system with a single hard-hitting spender. The rotation generally adheres a basic priority and a few overarching principles with several moving parts and short cooldown abilities to keep track of. AoE is much of the same, except you deal damage to targets evenly by cleaving off a single priority target using [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_priest_tentacleslam.gif)Tentacle Slam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1227280/tentacle-slam) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_priest_psychiclink.gif)Psychic Link](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=199484/psychic-link). Shadow's rotation can be boiled down to the following.
