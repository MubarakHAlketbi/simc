In this guide, we will explain the best gems, flasks, potions, and enchants to use for Balance Druid in Midnight Season 1, as well as cheaper alternatives. Make sure to also check our Midnight Profession Guide for all profession details.

Balance Druid Gems
Due to differences in stat distribution you may want to use gems with different stats on them. Generally Balance always opt into ones with Mastery. There are also effects like  Prismatic Focusing Iris  that increases in power the more different colored gems you have.
Garnet:  Flawless Masterful Garnet 
Peridot:  Flawless Masterful Peridot 
Amethyst:  Flawless Quick Amethyst 
Lapis:  Flawless Masterful Lapis 
Balance Druid Weapon Enchant
The option for weapon enchants comes down to flat secondary stats in  Thalassian Phoenix Oil  or a damage proc in  Smuggler's Enchanted Edge . The damage proc is currently simming quite low, so we opt for the secondary stats instead.

Balance Druid Food
In Midnight you have a choice between secondary stat food or Intellect food. There are personal food and feast versions of both. Generally you want to opt for the food that gives Intellect over the ones that give secondary stat.
Feast:  Harandar Celebration
