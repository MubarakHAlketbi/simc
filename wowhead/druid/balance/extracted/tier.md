 TIER SET BONUSES FOR BALANCE DRUID
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Balance Druid gear page!

Balance Druid Gear Guide

Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Balance Druid
The Midnight Season 1 Tier Set brings a substantial buff to your  Shooting Stars which adds a lot of value to the  Starweaver and  Hail of Stars feedback loop of granting  Solstice uptime. This also saturates the proc rate of  Orbit Breaker.
Sprouts of the Luminous Bloom
 Druid Balance 12.0 Class Set 2pc - 2 Set - Shooting Stars deal 40% more damage and generate 2 additional Astral Power outside Eclipse.

 Druid Balance 12.0 Class Set 4pc - 4 Set - Starfall and Starsurge damage increased by 5% and casting them has a 30% chance to strike an affected target with an exploding Shooting Star that also deals Astral damage to all nearby enemies.

Tier Set Bonus Rotation Changes for Balance Druid
While the 12.0 tier set does not inherently change the rotation much, it leans into and heavily buffs the already established gameplay.  Shooting Stars is already a decent chunk of your damage profile, and becomes considerably stronger with this tier set.

The 4-set gives a 40% chance to proc an exploding Shooting Star that deals uncapped AoE damage to enemies around it whenever you cast any  Starsurge or  Starfall. With the gameplay revolving around  Starweaver this is potentially a very powerful interaction when you're chaining free spender procs, but can also feel equally bad when you go several casts without netting a single proc.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR BALANCE DRUID TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
