## ~~~~ Balance Druid Overview

Balance's class fantasy is controlling the sun and the moon to do damage from range with Nature and Arcane spell schools. You are in a more powerful state and gain bonus damage by being in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_forceofnature.gif)Moonkin Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=24858/moonkin-form).

Balance Druid is a specialization that revolves around basic builder-spender gameplay with strong cooldown windows. Since it's a Druid spec, you also have access to a high amount of utility abilities that allow for opportunities to succeed outside of simply dealing damage.

## ~~~~ Balance Druid Strengths and Weaknesses

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strengths** |  | **Weaknesses** |
| - **Long-term, sustained multi-target**: Balance has a high amount of multi-target damage due to most of our multi-target power being in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_firestarter.gif)Sunfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=93402/sunfire), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Starfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194153/starfire).<br>  <br>- **Access to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form)**: This is a situationally powerful tool whenever dealing with moments of high damage intake.<br>  <br>- **Control and Utility**: Druid has access to a significant amount of utility and control in the form of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_lightning.gif)Innervate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=29166/innervate), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_stranglevines.gif)Entangling Roots](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=339/entangling-roots), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_hunter_beastsoothe.gif)Soothe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2908/soothe) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_typhoon.gif)Typhoon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=132469/typhoon). You also have access to choose between useful spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_massentanglement.gif)Mass Entanglement](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=102359/mass-entanglement) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_bash.gif)Mighty Bash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5211/mighty-bash). |  | - **Long multi-target ramp up time**: Balance suffers in general from a significant ramp-time in AoE scenarios, where you want to spread [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire) on as many targets as possible before entering your damage windows.<br>  <br>- **Lack of talent strength**: The current power of Balance is strong, while each individual talent does not provide much power. Most of your spec talents are passive interactions that provide on average 1-2% damage each. This results in very little agency over your playstyle based on talent selection. |

## ~~~~ Balance Druid Changes in Midnight

With Midnight, WoW's core combat systems have received substantial changes. Similar to most other specs, Balance Druid received a number of impactful changes, including the removal and addition of certain abilities and talents.

### Removed Abilities

Balance lost the following abilities in Midnight:

- **[Nature's Vigil](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=124988)** \- Removed and replaced with the passive node [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_giftofthewild.gif)Gift of the Wild](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1262034/gift-of-the-wild) that increases the effectiveness of your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_regeneration.gif)Mark of the Wild](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1126/mark-of-the-wild) on yourself.

- **[Renewal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=292628)** \- Removed. Still have access to the passive version [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_healingtouch.gif)Aessina's Renewal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=474678/aessinas-renewal).

- **[Stellar Flare](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202347)** \- Removed and not replaced.

- **[Warrior of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202425)** \- Removed and not replaced.


### New Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) changed from a passive buff to an active ability. You can find more info about it below.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_ability_druid_totaleclipse.gif)Total Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240206/total-eclipse) RNG talent interaction that gives you a chance whenever you press [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) to also grant the opposite Eclipse.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Meteor Storm](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240262/meteor-storm) New talent in a choice node with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Aetherial Kindling](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=327541/aetherial-kindling) making your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall) deal its full damage in half the time.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_moonglow.gif)Elune's Challenge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1240283/elunes-challenge) New talent that greatly reduces the cast time of your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_wrathv2.gif)Wrath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5176/wrath) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Starfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194153/starfire) whenever you exit Eclipse with less than 40 Astral Power.


The biggest change coming into Midnight is the rework of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse), turning it into an active button that you press, similar to other short cooldowns like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_dreamstate.gif)Fury of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202770/fury-of-elune). This version of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) is also on the GCD, though it is a shorter half-second GCD.

The change to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Mastery: Astral Invocation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393014/mastery-astral-invocation) is also meaningful, fully removing its interaction with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_mage_firestarter.gif)Sunfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=93402/sunfire) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire), significantly increasing the damage you're able to do in short AoE bursts or on priority add spawns in a raid scenario. Removing power from Sunfire and Moonfire also opened up to buff the raw number of your DoTs, as well as buff the damage of interactions like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_priest_divinestar_shadow2.gif)Shooting Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202342/shooting-stars) that are directly tied to your DoT effects.

Because of the buffs to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_priest_divinestar_shadow2.gif)Shooting Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202342/shooting-stars) and the Midnight Season 1 4-set we are going to be playing around builds with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_invocation.gif)Starweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393940/starweaver) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossgorefiend_touchofdoom.gif)Touch the Cosmos](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450356/touch-the-cosmos) on single target. The talent [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Hail of Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=469004/hail-of-stars) increases the frequency of your Shooting Stars and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Orbit Breaker](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383197/orbit-breaker) procs whenever you cast a free [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane03.gif)Starsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=78674/starsurge) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall). This feedback loop can be really fun to play around, but does require some knowledge when it comes to timing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) around your procs. Due to the nature of this interaction, we are also opting into playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_incarnation.gif)Incarnation: Chosen of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=102560/incarnation-chosen-of-elune) on single target.

We also received some more minor changes. Keeper of the Grove now generates less Astral Power overall - and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Harmony of the Grove](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=428731/harmony-of-the-grove)'s damage buff got nerfed in order to move some damage away from your cooldown windows and over to your general rotation. This is arguably a positive change, as most of Balance's damage during The War Within came from overlapping [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Harmony of the Grove](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=428731/harmony-of-the-grove) with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_natureguardian.gif)Celestial Alignment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194223/celestial-alignment) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druid_convokethespirits.gif)Convoke the Spirits](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391528/convoke-the-spirits).

## Balance Druid Hero Talents: Keeper of the Grove and Elune's Chosen

When playing Keeper of the Grove, your main priority is to use [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Force of Nature](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=205636/force-of-nature) as often as possible. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Harmony of the Grove](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=428731/harmony-of-the-grove) is a pretty strong capstone talent in this tree, offering a 24% damage gain when your Treants are active. The only real downside of Keeper of the Grove is the lower amount of AoE damage that it can provide, especially in comparison to Elune's Chosen. Keeper of the Grove will generally always be your primary Hero Talent choice in raid due to how taxing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Starfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194153/starfire) can be on your movement.

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_cloth_outdooremeralddream_d_01_buckle.gif)Control of the Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434249/control-of-the-dream) vs [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_xavius_dreamsimulacrum.gif)Power of the Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434220/power-of-the-dream) is an interesting choice node within this Hero Talent tree. Note that in simulations, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_cloth_outdooremeralddream_d_01_buckle.gif)Control of the Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434249/control-of-the-dream) performs ~1% higher than [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_xavius_dreamsimulacrum.gif)Power of the Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434220/power-of-the-dream). It is important to consider [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Force of Nature](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=205636/force-of-nature)'s own cooldown and usage here, with a general rule of thumb being not to hold either [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_natureguardian.gif)Celestial Alignment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194223/celestial-alignment) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_forceofnature.gif)Force of Nature](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=205636/force-of-nature) for more than 15 seconds. The bonus time granted by [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_cloth_outdooremeralddream_d_01_buckle.gif)Control of the Dream](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=434249/control-of-the-dream) is, of course, not included in this calculation.

When playing Elune's Chosen, your choice is between [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_dreamstate.gif)Fury of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202770/fury-of-elune) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_fullmoon.gif)Full Moon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202771/full-moon). [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_eluneschosendruid_boundlessmoonlight.gif)Boundless Moonlight](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=424058/boundless-moonlight) is the core mechanic of the Elune's Chosen tree, due to the effect it has on both abilities. It causes Fury of Elune to end with an energy pulse that deals AoE damage to enemies and causes Full Moon to deal additional damage to your target and generate Astral Power. Elune's Chosen is, therefore, a tree that excels at cleave situations. Talents within the tree, such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_cresentburn.gif)Lunar Calling](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=429523/lunar-calling), Fury of Elune and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_moonglow.gif)Lunation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=429539/lunation) lean into this theme. In general though, Elune's Chosen does not perform well in most single target situations.

Although you are denied access to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Solar Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1233346/solar-eclipse) when playing Elune's Chosen, your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Starfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194153/starfire) s will begin hitting quite hard and become a significant part of single target damage. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_dreamstate.gif)Fury of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202770/fury-of-elune) also comes out as the best option in both single target and AoE within this tree, making any additional targets hit by the ability a strict bonus that emphasizes the cleave potential of the tree. This does, however, require mobs to be reasonably stacked to get the most out of this cleave potential.

This season, Elune's Chosen looks like a much higher DPS option than Keeper of the Grove in Mythic+, while the reverse is true in raids.

## ~~~~Gearing a Balance Druid in Midnight Season 1

As a general rule, Balance Druid stat priority when gearing is **Intellect > Mastery > Haste > Versatility > Critical Strike**. This can change slightly based on talents and what type of content you're doing. When playing Keeper of the Grove with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druid_convokethespirits.gif)Convoke the Spirits](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391528/convoke-the-spirits) you will naturally want less Haste and more Versatility or Critical Strike since [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druid_convokethespirits.gif)Convoke the Spirits](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391528/convoke-the-spirits) does not scale with Haste. However, you're unlikely to drop Haste as your 2nd best stat.

For Elune's Chosen on the other hand, the value of Haste goes up since the Hero Talent revolves around pressing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_starfire.gif)Starfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194153/starfire) in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipse.gif)Eclipse (Lunar)](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48518/eclipse-lunar) to reduce the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_dreamstate.gif)Fury of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202770/fury-of-elune).

Mastery is still your definite best stat for all the different talent options.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Balance Druid?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Balance Druid players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Leatherworking](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/leatherworking-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/druid/balance/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

**Intellect** is your Primary stat, found on leather gear. It increases your Spell Power and damage/healing done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Balance Druid gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike**: Chance for attacks and heals to deal double damage.

- **Haste**: Increases attack speed, reduces your Global Cooldown, and increases number of DOT ticks.

- **Mastery**: [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Mastery: Astral Invocation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393014/mastery-astral-invocation) \- Increases your Nature and Arcane Damage.

- **Versatility**: Increases damage and healing, while also reducing damage taken.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

## ~~~~ Baseline Balance Druid Spells

### ~~~~ Healing Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_resistnature.gif)Regrowth](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8936/regrowth) \- Heals your current target and places a small Heal over Time (HoT) effect on them.


### ~~~~ Offensive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_wrathv2.gif)Wrath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=190984/wrath) \- Shoot a missile at your target that deals Nature damage and generates Astral Power.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire) (MF) - Place a Damage over Time (DoT) effect on your target that deals Arcane damage and generates Astral Power.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_monsterclaw_03.gif)Swipe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213764/swipe) \- Deal Physical damage to all targets in an 8-yard cone in front of you. Requires you to be in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) to utilize.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_mangle2.gif)Mangle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33917/mangle) \- Deal Physical damage to your target and generates 12 Rage. Requires you to be in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form) to utilize.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_shadow_vampiricaura.gif)Shred](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5221/shred) \- Deal Physical damage to your target and generates a combo point. Requires you to be in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) to utilize.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_thrash.gif)Thrash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77758/thrash) \- Deal Physical damage to targets within 8yds and apply a DoT effect on them. Only usable in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) \- Spend all available combo points to deal Physical damage to your target. Requires you to be in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) to utilize.


### ~~~~ Utility Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_stoneclawtotem.gif)Barkskin](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22812/barkskin) (Bark) - Gain a 20% reduction to all damage taken for the next 8 seconds. While [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_stoneclawtotem.gif)Barkskin](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22812/barkskin) is active, you do not suffer any delays from casting spells upon taking damage. Usable while stunned, frozen, incapacitated, feared, or asleep, and in all shapeshift forms.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_stranglevines.gif)Entangling Roots](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=339/entangling-roots) \- Roots the target in place for 30 seconds. Damage may cancel the effect.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_regeneration.gif)Mark of the Wild](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1126/mark-of-the-wild) (Mark) - Buff your raid/party members, increasing their Versatility by 3% for one hour.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_forceofnature.gif)Moonkin Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=24858/moonkin-form) \- Shapeshift into Moonkin Form, increasing your spell damage by 10% and gaining 125% armor.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form) \- Shapeshift into Bear Form, increasing your armor by 220%, Stamina by 25%, and greatly increasing threat generation.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) \- Shapeshift into Cat Form, increasing your auto-attack damage by 40% and movement speed by 30%.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_travelform.gif)Travel Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=783/travel-form) \- Shapeshift into a travel form, depending on your terrain. This form has no combat benefits..

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_physical_taunt.gif)Growl](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6795/growl) \- Taunt your current target to attack you.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_dash.gif)Dash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1850/dash) \- Shapeshift into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) and increase your movement speed by 60% for 10 seconds. Note that the movement speed effect is only active while in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form).

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_lunarguidance.gif)Revive](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=50769/revive) \- Resurrect the targeted ally. Only usable outside of combat.

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_reincarnation.gif)Rebirth](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20484/rebirth) (Battle Ress) - Resurrect the targeted ally. Usable in combat, but has a 10 minute cooldown.


### ~~~~ Passive Abilities

- [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Mastery: Astral Invocation](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393014/mastery-astral-invocation) \- Your Arcane spells and Nature spells deal more damage. Astral Damage spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane03.gif)Starsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=78674/starsurge) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall) deal **both Nature and Arcane Damage**

## Pre-Combat Check

Before any combat happens, make sure as an Balance Druid to have the following done. Forgetting any of these will hurt your performance significnatly, and this applies to Raids, Dungeons, Delves and even Open-World content.

### Druid Buffs

- **Use Your Raid Buff -** Remember to apply [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_regeneration.gif)Mark of the Wild](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1126/mark-of-the-wild) before a pull!.

- **Innervate Your Healer -** Talk with your healer or raid leader and decide which healer to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_lightning.gif)Innervate](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=29166/innervate)!


### Consumables Check

- **Flasks & Food -** Use [![Flask of the Magisters](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_flask_sindoreipotion_black.gif)Flask of the Magisters![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier2.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241322/flask-of-the-magisters) and Intellect food.

- **Weapon Oils -** Remember to use weapon oils like [![Thalassian Phoenix Oil](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_enchanting_manaoil_red.gif)Thalassian Phoenix Oil![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier1.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=243733/thalassian-phoenix-oil)!

- **Bring Healing -** Always have a [![Healthstone](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/warlock_-healthstone.gif)Healthstone](https://www.wowhead.com/item=5512/healthstone) and a [![Silvermoon Health Potion](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_12_profession_alchemy_lightpotion_orange.gif)Silvermoon Health Potion![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/TextureAtlas/live/professions-chaticon-quality-tier1.png)](https://www.wowhead.com/item=241305/silvermoon-health-potion)!


## ~~~~ Balance Druid Major Cooldowns

Balance has access to a variety of different cooldowns on its spec tree. All of them come with some extra wrinkles in how they work rather than simply being flat increases, so the section below covers _how_ they work, and most importantly, how to get the most out of each use.

Celestial Alignment

Convoke the Spirits

### Celestial Alignment

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_natureguardian.gif)Celestial Alignment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=194223/celestial-alignment) grants you both Eclipse effects, which increases the damage that all your abilities do. Additionally, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_incarnation.gif)Incarnation: Chosen of Elune](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=102560/incarnation-chosen-of-elune) is an improved version of Celestial Alignment, increasing its duration, and providing additional Critical Strike. Your rotation does not change during Celestial Alignment, but you should generally try to spend as much [![Astral Power](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Astral Power](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/astral-power-8) as possible during this window.

Incarnation: Chosen of Elune is a very real choice of cooldown in Midnight, with the buffs to interactions revolving [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_priest_divinestar_shadow2.gif)Shooting Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202342/shooting-stars) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_invocation.gif)Starweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393940/starweaver). These buffs lean into a more flat damage profile, and therefore prefers Incarnation: Chosen of Elune for the increased duration and Critical Strike buff.

### Convoke the Spirits

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druid_convokethespirits.gif)Convoke the Spirits](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=391528/convoke-the-spirits) is primarily a single-target cooldown since it uses a lot of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_wrathv2.gif)Wrath](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5176/wrath) s and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane03.gif)Starsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=78674/starsurge) s. You want to have as many mobs afflicted by [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire) as well, because Wrath/Starsurge can get replaced with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire).

The free Starsurges cast by Convoke the Spirits also provide 1 second value for [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Hail of Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=469004/hail-of-stars), which usually adds up to about 6 or 7 seconds.

The spells cast by Convoke the Spirits work off of two separate pools of spells. One pool is filled with regular Balance spells like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall), Starsurge, Wrath, Moonfire and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_fullmoon.gif)Full Moon](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=274283/full-moon). The other pool is filled with Feral/Restoration Druid spells and which ones are cast depends on your proximity to the target. On the surface, this means that using Convoke the Spirits in melee is a DPS gain, but this equals out because of the existence of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_thorns.gif)Blooming Infusion](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=429433/blooming-infusion). Feral Druid spells also do very low damage so even if there is a difference it is negligible in the grand scheme of things.

Full Moon cast by Convoke the Spirits work off a stacked deck. 1 in every 5 casts will be a guaranteed Full Moon, and the deck doesn't start over until all 5 casts are done. The deck gets changed to 1 in every 2 casts instead if you are talented into 1-minute Convoke through [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_ardenweald_druid.gif)Elune's Guidance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393991/elunes-guidance).

## ~~~~ Balance Druid Advanced Tips

Balance Druid has some optimizations revolving around talents such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_invocation.gif)Starweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393940/starweaver).

Eclipse

Starweaver

### Eclipse

With [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) now being a cooldown there are some optimizations to consider.

1. You want to spend your time inside Eclipse on high value casts. Your spenders like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane03.gif)Starsurge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=78674/starsurge) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Starfall](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=191034/starfall) are a good portion of your damage. You should pool [![Astral Power](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Astral Power](https://www.wowhead.com/resource/astral-power-8) outside of Eclipse and then spend as much as possible inside to optimize the number of spenders cast inside.

2. Approaching Eclipse changes depending on whether you play [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_invocation.gif)Starweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393940/starweaver) or [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane01.gif)Rattle the Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393954/rattle-the-stars), where Rattle the Stars relies more on pooling Astral Power than Starweaver does, as Starweaver can cover it's spenders by chaining procs

3. **Remember that entering Eclipse resets your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Solstice](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343647/solstice) to 6 seconds no matter what your previous duration was!**


### Starweaver

Historically the choice between [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_invocation.gif)Starweaver](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393940/starweaver) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_arcane_arcane01.gif)Rattle the Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393954/rattle-the-stars) has been a one-sided choice, but with adjustments to the damage of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_priest_divinestar_shadow2.gif)Shooting Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202342/shooting-stars) this has changed a bit.

1. **Maximize uptime on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_balancedruid_moonandstars.gif)Solstice](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=343647/solstice):** Shooting Stars is now a considerable amount of your damage, so maintaining uptime on Solstice through the [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_starfall.gif)Hail of Stars](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=469004/hail-of-stars) interactions with Starweaver and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bossgorefiend_touchofdoom.gif)Touch the Cosmos](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=450356/touch-the-cosmos) is important.

2. Whenever playing Starweaver you also want to bank up procs before entering your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_eclipseorange.gif)Eclipse](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1239669/eclipse) in order to spend [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_druid_ascendanceeclipses.gif)Ascendant Eclipses](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1261564/ascendant-eclipses) as soon as possible inside your [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_earthandsky.gif)Balance of All Things](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=394048/balance-of-all-things) window.

### Item Level vs. Stat Priority for Balance Druid

Mastery is generally always the best stat for Balance Druid after intellect. However, it is very rarely worth dropping Intellect in favor for Mastery depending on the ilvl difference.

Stat priority for Balance Druids is based on data from Simulations and logs. There will be variance based on what's available in Midnight Season 1 but you can see the overall picture in [our BiS page for Balance Druid](https://www.wowhead.com/guide=3100).
