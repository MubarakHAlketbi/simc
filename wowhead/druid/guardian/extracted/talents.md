### tab_group_0 — Tabs: ['Druid of the Claw']

#### Druid of the Claw
This is the standard Guardian raid build. It plays heavily around  Killing Blow,  Harnessed Rage and  Red Moon. Which means we want to use  Maul/ Raze above 80 rage as frequently as possible to fully utilize both  Killing Blow and  Harnessed Rage which keeps our rotation flowing smoothly. Fights where you do not need  Ursoc's Guidance can instead take  Flashing Claws.
