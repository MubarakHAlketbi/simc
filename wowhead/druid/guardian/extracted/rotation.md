#### Single-Target Priority
BEST GUARDIAN DRUID SINGLE TARGET ROTATION
With  Druid of the Claw selected, the priority is as follows:
The builds used below are covered in the Talents page. Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes.
PRIORITY
Maintain  Moonfire on your primary target.
Maintain 3-5 stacks of  Thrash.
Cast  Red Moon on cooldown.
Cast  Mangle on cooldown.
Cast  Thrash on cooldown.
Spend Rage on either  Maul (offensively) or  Ironfur(defensively.
Cast  Frenzied Regeneration if your health dips low.
Cast  Moonfire with  Galactic Guardian procs.
Use your cooldowns  Barkskin/ Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc as frequently as possible.
Cast  Swipe if you have nothing else to press.

While this buff is active, you want to try to get as many casts of  Thrash and  Mangle off as possible, while also spending your Rage on  Maul,  Raze, or  Ironfur to avoid capping Rage.

SPENDING RAGE
Whether you want to improve surviving or want to improve your damage. Spending rage will be the main focal point of both.
Pressing  Ironfur when you don't actually need it defensively is now quite a significant dps loss.
As our goal is to stay above 80 rage with each cast of  Maul due to  Harnessed Rage, pressing Ironfur drains that rage quickly and stops the cycle of high rage expenditure.
This means we end up filling our GCD's with useless abilities like  Swipe which generate no rage and deal very little damage. The more you are swiping the less DPS you are doing efficiently.
Alternatively, not pressing Ironfur when a large physical hit is coming or significant flow of melee swings, can easily lead to death. So finding a comfortable balance between these 2 is integral.

#### Heart of the Wild
PRE-COMBAT CHECK

Be in Bear Form

Pressing  Heart of the Wild casts a single spell depending on which form you are in.
For Cat Form this is  Feral Frenzy - A large single target DoT.

This means we end up filling our GCD's with useless abilities like  Swipe which generate no rage and deal very little damage. The more you are swiping the less DPS you are doing efficiently.
Alternatively, not pressing Ironfur when a large physical hit is coming or significant flow of melee swings, can easily lead to death. So finding a comfortable balance between these 2 is integral.
