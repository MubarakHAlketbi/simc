 BEST GUARDIAN DRUID CONSUMABLES IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below, we've listed the best flasks, potions, and weapon buffs that Guardian Druids can use in PvE content. Guardian Druid Flask
The best Flask for Guardian Druids is  Flask of the Blood Knights . Although all 4 of our stats are fairly close and if you find you have too much haste you can opt for another secondary stat comfortably.
Guardian Druid Combat Potions
The best stat boosting potion for Guardian Druids is  Draught of Rampant Abandon  or  Light's Potential  if the void zones are too bothersome.
Guardian Druid Health Potions
The best healing potion for Guardian Druids is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Guardian Druid Weapon Buff
For their weapons, Guardian Druids will always use  Thalassian Phoenix Oil .
Guardian Druid Augment Rune
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Guardian Druid Food
In Midnight, Guardian Druid eats the feast ( Silvermoon Parade) for buffs because primary stat is always the highest increase for us.
