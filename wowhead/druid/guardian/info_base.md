## ~~~~Guardian Druid Overview

Guardian Druid is a shapeshifting, leather-wearing Tank specialization with a mix of damage reduction, self-healing, and high mobility. We can utilize shapeshifting to maximize mobility, while our toolkit allows us to survive some of the most dangerous encounters with ease, as our thick hide protects us from great threats.

Guardian is a great spec for new players, as it has easily one of the most simplistic rotations in all of WoW.

## Guardian Druid Hero Talents: Druid of the Claw and Elune's Chosen

For Hero Talents, Guardian Druids pick between Druid of the Claw and Elune's Chosen.

The Druid of the Claw Hero Tree represents synergy between Guardian and Feral (i.e., Cat) Druids, with a primary focus on big Bleed effects. As a Guardian Druid of the Claw, you shapeshift to increase damage in certain scenarios. With talents such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_mastershapeshifter.gif)Wildshape Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441678/wildshape-mastery), you will be much safer in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_catform.gif)Cat Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=768/cat-form) than in the past, where a single swing from a boss could kill you. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ravage.gif)Wildpower Surge](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441691/wildpower-surge) in combination with [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_mastershapeshifter.gif)Fluid Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449193/fluid-form) sparks the return of the controversial 'Catweaving' playstyle, which is currently a considerable single-target DPS increase after recent buffs.

On the other hand, a Guardian Druid opting into Elune's Chosen emphasizes astral influences. This tree revolves heavily around [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_moonglow.gif)Lunar Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204066/lunar-beam) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire). It's a much simpler tree compared to Druid of the Claw, since it doesn't affect our rotation much besides enhancing its Arcane aspects. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_moonglow.gif)Lunar Beam](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204066/lunar-beam) becomes a core offensive and defensive button, granting a large amount of Mastery on a relatively short cooldown.

While both of these trees are fairly even defensively, Druid of the Claw pulls ahead significantly in single-target DPS.

## ~~~~ Playing a Guardian Druid in Midnight Season 1

The Guardian Druid rotation is fairly simple, following three rules:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Avoid Rage waste:** As we have an off-GCD Rage spending ability in [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ironfur.gif)Ironfur](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=192081/ironfur), our goal is to waste as little Rage as possible. This also feeds into one of our strongest talents, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druid_convokethespirits.gif)Ursoc's Guidance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=393414/ursocs-guidance), which reduces downtime on one of our biggest cooldowns significantly based on Rage spent. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Keep abilities on cooldown:** To ensure we are generating as much Rage and dealing as much damage as possible, keep rotational abilities such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_mangle2.gif)Mangle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33917/mangle) and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_thrash.gif)Thrash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77758/thrash) on cooldown. Secondly, using some of our longer offensive/defensive cooldowns such as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_incarnation.gif)Incarnation: Guardian of Ursoc](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=102558/incarnation-guardian-of-ursoc), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_stoneclawtotem.gif)Barkskin](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22812/barkskin), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_hand_1h_artifactursoc_d_01.gif)Rage of the Sleeper](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=200851/rage-of-the-sleeper) as often as possible will keep us feeling strong and fluid. |

* * *

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Utilize procs:** Guardian has a few powerful procs that play key roles in making your rotation smooth and effective. These include [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druidoftheclawdruid_ravage.gif)Ravage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441583/ravage) for Druid of the Claw, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_frost_iceclaw.gif)Galactic Guardian](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213708/galactic-guardian), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_monsterfang_01.gif)Tooth and Claw](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=135288/tooth-and-claw). These become the highest priority spells to cast to avoid wasting any valuable procs in the future. | |

## ~~~~ Gearing a Guardian Druid in Midnight Season 1

Follow this stat priority: **Haste > Versatility > Mastery = Critical Strike**. Don't neglect the value of **Agility**, as a substantial increase in item level is almost always worth it over slightly more optimal stats, and all four of our Secondary stats are quite even in strength. We always recommend using [Raidbots](https://www.raidbots.com/simbot/stats) to sim yourself, since every character has different circumstances.

## ~~~~ What Professions are Best for Guardian Druid?

Compared to previous expansions, professions in Midnight provide minimal benefits towards the performance of your spec. This means that Guardian Druid players can choose any profession according to personal preference. You might consider [Leatherworking](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/leatherworking-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft your own armor, as many profession items can be very close to your [Best in Slot gear](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/druid/guardian/bis-gear). On the other hand, you may opt for [Alchemy](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to craft consumables or [Enchanting](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/professions/enchanting-overview-trainer-locations-recipes-tools) to enchant gear without relying on the Auction House.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Guardian Druid

Defensively, Guardian prefers Versatility and Haste. Versatility reduces your overall damage taken and increases your personal healing, while Haste speeds up your rotation and Rage generation.

Offensively, all 4 Secondary stats are extremely close and your preferred stats can vary heavily based on your current stat allocation. If you have exceptionally low amounts of Critical Strike or an exceptionally high amount of Haste, Critical Strike may be stronger than Haste for you.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

Agility is Guardian's Primary stat. Agility increases your Attack Power, grants a small amount of Dodge, and increases your armor when [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ironfur.gif)Ironfur](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=192081/ironfur) is active. Agility is also your strongest overall stat. It is the best stat per-point offensively, and it also has a defensive conversion due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ironfur.gif)Ironfur](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=192081/ironfur)’s Agility scaling. You will want to prioritize Agility over Secondary stats in most situations.

For secondary stats, the Guardian Druid has the following:

- **Critical Strike**: Grants Dodge (subject to heavy diminishing returns), and causes your damage and healing to have a chance to critically strike, dealing double damage/healing. Guardian Druid has no notable interactions with Critical Strike. Most of Guardian’s abilities can critically hit, with the exception of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bullrush.gif)Frenzied Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22842/frenzied-regeneration) and the extra healing produced from our Mastery ( [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_primaltenacity.gif)Mastery: Nature's Guardian](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=155783/mastery-natures-guardian)).

- **Haste**: Haste has many effects for Guardian. Firstly, it increases melee attack speed. Secondly, it decreases the global cooldown. Thirdly, it reduces the cooldown of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_mangle2.gif)Mangle](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=33917/mangle), [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_thrash.gif)Thrash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77758/thrash), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_bullrush.gif)Frenzied Regeneration](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22842/frenzied-regeneration). Finally, it increases the tick rate of the [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_thrash.gif)Thrash](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=77758/thrash) Bleed and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_starfall.gif)Moonfire](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=8921/moonfire)'s DoT component. Many of these effects increase your DPS, making Haste a valuable stat for offensive purposes. Rage generation via melee swings is also increased by Haste.

- **Mastery**: [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_primaltenacity.gif)Mastery: Nature's Guardian](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=155783/mastery-natures-guardian) increases maximum Health and healing received, as well as increasing Attack Power. This is a strong defensive stat, and due to the changes to ability damage scaling is also a decent offensive stat.

- **Versatility**: Increases your damage and healing (including absorption effects), as well as reducing the damage you take. This is your strongest overall Secondary stat due to it benefitting every area of your kit. As a defensive stat it also becomes more valuable the more you have of it, due to the multiplicative nature of damage reduction effects.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

## Guardian Druid Rotation in Midnight Season 1

The Guardian Rotation is one of the most simple and easy to manage in the whole game.

We have only a few short cooldown spells we want to use as often as possible and no real debuffs or procs to track.

The main thing to focus on for Guardian is ensuring you have enough rage to use defensively when you require it.

## ~~~~ Guardian Druid Advanced Tips

Spending Rage

### Spending Rage

Whether you want to improve surviving or want to improve your damage. Spending rage will be the main focal point of both.

Pressing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ironfur.gif)Ironfur](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=192081/ironfur) when you don't actually need it defensively is now quite a significant dps loss.

As our goal is to stay above 80 rage with each cast of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_maul.gif)Maul](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=6807/maul) due to [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_bearclaw_nightmare.gif)Harnessed Rage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1253035/harnessed-rage), pressing Ironfur drains that rage quickly and stops the cycle of high rage expenditure.

This means we end up filling our GCD's with useless abilities like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_monsterclaw_03.gif)Swipe](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=213764/swipe) which generate no rage and deal very little damage. The more you are swiping the less DPS you are doing efficiently.

Alternatively, not pressing Ironfur when a large physical hit is coming or significant flow of melee swings, can easily lead to death. So finding a comfortable balance between these 2 is integral.

### Single Button Rotation Assistant

Assisted Highlight and Single-Button Assistant are two new tools designed to assist players with general combat rotation and how their class spells and abilities influence one another. For Guardian Druid, these tools are useful for beginners but something you should aim to avoid using long term. They do not factor in your defensive cooldown usage which is essential for tanks to survive and overall are a significant dps loss to use.

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Guardian Druid Beginners
