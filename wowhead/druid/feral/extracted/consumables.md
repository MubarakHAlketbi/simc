Feral Druid Gems
For maximum damage, you will want to use  Powerful Eversong Diamond  as your Eversong Diamond. The remaining slots are filled with either of the ones listed below:
Amethyst:  Flawless Quick Amethyst 
Peridot:  Flawless Masterful Peridot 
Garnet:  Flawless Masterful Garnet 
Lapis:  Flawless Masterful Lapis 
Which ones you use does not matter, however you'll want to use one of each colour. The gem selction can differ depending on your stats, so always use a simulation if you want the optimal result.

Feral Druid Flask
The best Flask for Feral Druids is bewteen  Flask of the Shattered Sun  and  Flask of the Magisters . Which one is better will usually depend on your gear, and its best to use a simulation to compare.
Feral Druid Combat Potions
While there are many options for potions, Feral Druids will use  Draught of Rampant Abandon . The void pools spawned by this poiton silence you, which means you need to actively run out of them. Switching to  Light's Potential  is advised if the void pool mechanism becomes overwhelming.
Feral Druid Health Potions
The best healing potion for Feral Druids is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Feral Druid Weapon Buff
For Feral Druids weapons the best option is listed below:
Main Hand:  Thalassian Phoenix Oil 
Feral Druid Augment Rune
With The War Within, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Feral Druid Food
In Midnight, Feral Druids will use Feasts where possible due to the relative strength of agility to Secondary stats. As always, sim your character to check which food is the best for your configuration of Secondary stats.
Feast:  Blooming Feast
Personal Food:  Crimson Calamari
It's also worth noting that you can use Taa's such as  Sanguithorn Tea alongside a regular food buff for a 5% speed increase.
