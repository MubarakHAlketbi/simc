BERSERK
 Berserk has both a simple % damage increase and an additional effect, causing you to gain a free Combo Point every 1.5 seconds and your generators to generate an additional Combo Point. While  Berserk is active, you gain a Combo Point buffer for the duration. Any Combo Points you gain over the cap of 5 are converted into a buff, and when you spend Combo Points and return to 0, that buff is converted into Combo Points.
When talented into  Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane it replaces  Berserk granting the same effects for 20 seconds but additionally reducing all energy costs by 25% and allowing you a single use of  Prowl in combat during the duration.
If talented into  Coiled to Spring the effect doesn't trigger until you exceed the overflow Combo Points.
For  Druid of the Claw the  Berserk window is a significant portion of your overall damage due to  Claw Rampage allowing you to generate significant extra  Ravage procs..

PANDEMIC AND SNAPSHOTTING
Pandemic and snapshotting are two core feral mechanics that aren't really mentioned explicitly when learning the class.
Pandemic
Pandemic is a hidden mechanic in the game whereby if you refresh a dot or certain buffs up to 30% of the duration of the buff/debuff is added onto the next cast.
So if you take  Rake which has a base duration of 15 seconds, if you refresh  Rake with 5 seconds left on the duration, just under 5 seconds will be added onto your next cast. It's important to note that the only thing that is added on is duration, this resets the snapshot for the full duration of the newly applied dot.
Snapshotting
What is snapshotting, snapshotting is for most classes historical. Feral however still has a few interactions.  Tiger's Fury and  Pouncing Strikes, when a dot is applied during these effects the effect is applied to the full duration of the spell. If you cast a  Rake, or  Rip during  Tiger's Fury then the entire duration of the dot will be affected by the buff, even when you no longer have the buff active.

#### AoE Priority

The builds used below are covered in the Talents page. Anywhere you see the  icon, you can hover over it for additional notes.

 Swipe during  Berserk for  Claw Rampage procs.

 Swipe to generate Combo points.

#### Pre-Combat Checks
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Feral Druids to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Mark of the Wild to buff you and your party with Versatility.
Shapeshift - be in  Cat Form, your abilities only work in  Cat Form.
Be in  Prowl - You want to be in stealth at the start of combat to maximize damage from  Pouncing Strikes.

Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of Alchemical Chaos  and Agility food.

#### Chomp
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Feral Druids to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Mark of the Wild to buff you and your party with Versatility.
Shapeshift - be in  Cat Form, your abilities only work in  Cat Form.
Be in  Prowl - You want to be in stealth at the start of combat to maximize damage from  Pouncing Strikes.
CONSUMABLES CHECK
Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of Alchemical Chaos  and Agility food.
Weapon Oils - Make sure to apply your Weapon Oils, it's small but matters
Have Some Healing - Always have some a  Healthstone and an  Invigorating Healing Potion !

CONVOKE THE SPIRITS
 Convoke the Spirits - This ability casts 16 spells, 10 of which are form specific spells, with the rest being distributed between non-form-specific spells. This ability does have a degree of variance to it, which can be frustrating.  Convoke the Spirits will try and avoid casting most DoTs if they are already applied to the target.
The spell casts a mix of:

Cat Specific
 Ferocious Bite
 Rake
 Shred
Generic
 Wrath
 Moonfire ( Lunar Inspiration)
 Rejuvenation
 Regrowth
It also has a 20% chance of casting an "ultimate" ability for each spec, and for Feral this is  Feral Frenzy.

Usage of  Convoke the Spirits is fairly straightforward when you break it down. Use it when all your debuffs are applied try to sync it with cooldowns like  Berserk and  Tiger's Fury.

CHOMP
 Chomp is a unique spell that might take some getting accustomed too. Experienced Ferals will be familiar with Pooling, ensuring you have enough energy for CDs or to generate after a finisher.  Chomp requires you to think similarly but in reverse. You'll need to ensure that, as  Chomp comes off cooldown, you're low enough energy to cast it on cooldown.

#### Single-Target Priority
BEST FERAL DRUID SINGLE TARGET ROTATION
Feral's single-target priorities are maintaining debuffs and using any extra resources for direct damage. These priorities are largely the same between both Hero Talents, though there are some differences. As such you can use the buttons below to switch between each,

 Please select a Hero Talent option to generate a Single Target priority

BERSERK
 Berserk has both a simple % damage increase and an additional effect, causing you to gain a free Combo Point every 1.5 seconds and your generators to generate an additional Combo Point. While  Berserk is active, you gain a Combo Point buffer for the duration. Any Combo Points you gain over the cap of 5 are converted into a buff, and when you spend Combo Points and return to 0, that buff is converted into Combo Points.
When talented into  Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane it replaces  Berserk granting the same effects for 20 seconds but additionally reducing all energy costs by 25% and allowing you a single use of  Prowl in combat during the duration.
If talented into  Coiled to Spring the effect doesn't trigger until you exceed the overflow Combo Points.
For  Druid of the Claw the  Berserk window is a significant portion of your overall damage due to  Claw Rampage allowing you to generate significant extra  Ravage procs..

PANDEMIC AND SNAPSHOTTING
Pandemic and snapshotting are two core feral mechanics that aren't really mentioned explicitly when learning the class.
Pandemic
Pandemic is a hidden mechanic in the game whereby if you refresh a dot or certain buffs up to 30% of the duration of the buff/debuff is added onto the next cast.
So if you take  Rake which has a base duration of 15 seconds, if you refresh  Rake with 5 seconds left on the duration, just under 5 seconds will be added onto your next cast. It's important to note that the only thing that is added on is duration, this resets the snapshot for the full duration of the newly applied dot.
Snapshotting
What is snapshotting, snapshotting is for most classes historical. Feral however still has a few interactions.  Tiger's Fury and  Pouncing Strikes, when a dot is applied during these effects the effect is applied to the full duration of the spell. If you cast a  Rake, or  Rip during  Tiger's Fury then the entire duration of the dot will be affected by the buff, even when you no longer have the buff active.

#### Chomp
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Feral Druids to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Cast Group Buffs - Use  Mark of the Wild to buff you and your party with Versatility.
Shapeshift - be in  Cat Form, your abilities only work in  Cat Form.
Be in  Prowl - You want to be in stealth at the start of combat to maximize damage from  Pouncing Strikes.
CONSUMABLES CHECK
Flasks & Food - Use  Flask of Alchemical Chaos  and Agility food.
Weapon Oils - Make sure to apply your Weapon Oils, it's small but matters
Have Some Healing - Always have some a  Healthstone and an  Invigorating Healing Potion !

CONVOKE THE SPIRITS
 Convoke the Spirits - This ability casts 16 spells, 10 of which are form specific spells, with the rest being distributed between non-form-specific spells. This ability does have a degree of variance to it, which can be frustrating.  Convoke the Spirits will try and avoid casting most DoTs if they are already applied to the target.
The spell casts a mix of:

Cat Specific
 Ferocious Bite
 Rake
 Shred
Generic
 Wrath
 Moonfire ( Lunar Inspiration)
 Rejuvenation
 Regrowth
It also has a 20% chance of casting an "ultimate" ability for each spec, and for Feral this is  Feral Frenzy.

Usage of  Convoke the Spirits is fairly straightforward when you break it down. Use it when all your debuffs are applied try to sync it with cooldowns like  Berserk and  Tiger's Fury.

CHOMP
 Chomp is a unique spell that might take some getting accustomed too. Experienced Ferals will be familiar with Pooling, ensuring you have enough energy for CDs or to generate after a finisher.  Chomp requires you to think similarly but in reverse. You'll need to ensure that, as  Chomp comes off cooldown, you're low enough energy to cast it on cooldown.
