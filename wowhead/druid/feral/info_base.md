## ~~~~Feral Druid Overview

Feral Druid is a shapeshifting-focused, leather-wearing DPS specialization on a hybrid class. Feral's toolkit has decent AoE and single-target output, emphasizing building and spending Combo Points and Energy. The spec predominantly focuses on dealing Bleed damage and has decent active defensive abilities while being relatively fragile passively.

Feral has extreme mobility, both at the baseline and with active abilities.

## Feral Druid Hero Talents: Wildstalker and Druid of the Claw

For their Hero Talent choice, Feral Druids have to pick between Wildstalker and Druid of the Claw. Wildstalker Feral Druids focus on [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_misc_herb_16.gif)Bloodseeker Vines](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=439531/bloodseeker-vines), a procced "Bleed" that buffs our damage dealt to a target. Druid of the Claw embraces the other half of Feral's damage kit by focusing on buffing direct damage and converting [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_ability_druidoftheclawdruid_ravage.gif)Ravage](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441583/ravage) (the artist formerly known as Massive Attack).

Between the two of them, our Hero Talent options cover all aspects of Feral Druid's damage profile. Both trees are very passive and don't significantly affect your rotational gameplay. Druid of the Claw is a stronger defensive option, while Wildstalker has stronger sustain. As the two trees are very comparable in gameplay, the main dividing factor between them is tuning. For Patch 12.0.1, Druid of the Claw is tuned marginally better in M+ or AOE content while Wildstalker is tuned better in single target.

- **Throughput & Damage Profiles:** **Druid of the Claw** is the strongest option for throughput. It has a very bursty damage profile and a fair chunk of randomness.

- **Defense:** **Druid of the Claw** is the clear winner for defensive value. Extra health, stronger [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_bearform.gif)Bear Form](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5487/bear-form), and [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/artifactability_feraldruid_openwounds.gif)Dreadful Wound](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=441809/dreadful-wound)

- **Utility:** Neither hero talent has strong utility options. Utility won't influence your choice between the two.

## ~~~~ Stats Explained

**Agility** is your Primary stat, found on leather gear. It increases your Attack/Spell Power and damage/healing done with abilities.

For Secondary stats, Feral Druid gains the following from each stat:

- **Critical Strike**: Chance for attacks and heals to deal double damage. Advanced Interactions

Critical Strike for Feral's has some additional interactions, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_racial_cannibalize.gif)Primal Fury](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=159286/primal-fury) causes your generator's to generate double combo points. If you're playing [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_1115_druid_jaguar.gif)Chomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1244258/chomp) it will instead deal 3x damage rather than double.

- **Haste**: Increases attack speed, energy generation, and the tick rate of your damage over time effects.

- **Mastery**: Increases the damage done by your Cat Form bleed and other periodic abilities and finishing moves.

- **Versatility**: Increases damage and healing, while also reducing damage taken.

## ~~~~ Diminishing Returns in Midnight

Accumulating too much of one Secondary stat from your gear leads to diminishing returns. This means that adding more of the same stat beyond certain thresholds results in reduced benefits. These thresholds are fixed and reduce the amount gained from more of that stat rating by 10% per breakpoint. Each stat has a different rating threshold where the reduction begins:

- **Critical Strike** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Haste** \- Above 1320 rating

- **Mastery** \- Above 1380 rating

- **Versatility** \- Above 1620 rating


**Example**

Let's assume you gain 1% Haste for 10 Haste Rating. When above the diminishing return breakpoint, 10 Haste Rating would give you 0.9% Haste instead, resulting in a 10% reduction from what you gained previously.

Note that this system **only counts stat rating**, meaning that percentage-based procs and effects like [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_nature_bloodlust.gif)Bloodlust](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=2825/bloodlust) aren't factored in and will always grant their full benefit.

You can learn more about stat diminishing returns in our detailed guide:

[What are Stat Diminishing Returns](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/diminishing-returns-on-secondary-stats-in-world-of-warcraft)

### Berserk

[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_berserk.gif)Berserk](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=106951/berserk) has both a simple % damage increase and an additional effect, causing you to gain a free Combo Point every 1.5 seconds and your generators to generate an additional Combo Point. While [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_berserk.gif)Berserk](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=106951/berserk) is active, you gain a Combo Point buffer for the duration. Any Combo Points you gain over the cap of 5 are converted into a buff, and when you spend Combo Points and return to 0, that buff is converted into Combo Points.

- When talented into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/spell_druid_incarnation.gif)Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=102543/incarnation-avatar-of-ashamane) it replaces [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_berserk.gif)Berserk](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=106951/berserk) granting the same effects for 20 seconds but additionally reducing all energy costs by 25% and allowing you a single use of [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_prowl.gif)Prowl](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=5215/prowl) in combat during the duration.

- If talented into [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_supriseattack.gif)Coiled to Spring](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=449537/coiled-to-spring) the effect doesn't trigger until you exceed the overflow Combo Points.

## ~~~~ Feral Druid Advanced Tips\
\
The main thing that most new Feral Druids struggle with in the rotation is adjusting to a spec where your pace is determined by resources rather than GCDs. As Feral you'll need to get used to not casting a spell with every GCD, at times you'll have to wait for energy to cast spells or maximise the effectiveness of spells.\
\

\
Chomp\

### Ferocious Bite\
\
[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) has a mechanic whereby it will deal bonus damage based on extra energy spent. By default [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) will spend 25 energy with up to 100% extra damage at 25 additional energy spent for a total of 50 energy spent. Currently this isn't a relevant mechanic for gameplay because you'll cast [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) whenever you hit 4 or more combo points and 25 or more energy. No pooling for [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_druid_ferociousbite.gif)Ferocious Bite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22568/ferocious-bite) here.\
\
### Chomp\
\
[![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_1115_druid_jaguar.gif)Chomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1244258/chomp) is a unique spell that might take some getting accustomed too. Experienced Ferals will be familiar with Pooling, ensuring you have enough energy for CDs or to generate after a finisher. [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_1115_druid_jaguar.gif)Chomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1244258/chomp) requires you to think similarly but in reverse. You'll need to ensure that, as [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv_1115_druid_jaguar.gif)Chomp](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1244258/chomp) comes off cooldown, you're low enough energy to cast it on cooldown.\
