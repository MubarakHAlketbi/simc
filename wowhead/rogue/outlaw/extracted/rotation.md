#### Single-Target Priority
BEST OUTLAW ROGUE SINGLE TARGET ROTATION
Outlaw in Single Target plays around a priority list, juggling both ability cooldowns and resources based on an order of power. This means with each GCD we go top to bottom on a list, and press the button with the current highest value.

#### Roll the Bones
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Outlaw Rogue to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Apply Weapon Poisons
Eat Food

ROLL THE BONES
How it works is that whenever you press the button, you are given a random stage of the 4 possible outcomes. Each stage gives you a bonus alongside all the previous stages.

Stage 1 is increased  Opportunity chance.
Stage 2 is  Sinister Strike generates an additional combo point.
Stage 3 is increased  Restless Blades effectiveness.
Stage 4 is increased Critical Strike chance.

#### Preparation
PRE-COMBAT CHECK
Before any combat happens, it is important for Outlaw Rogue to make sure the following are done. This applies to raids, dungeons, delves, or even open-world content.

Apply Weapon Poisons
Eat Food

PREPARATION
This ability, in most instances, can be fairly autopilot, just use it when the conditions are met. However, because this doesn't work with  Restless Blades, we can be a bit more nuanced in its usage on certain encounters. If a fight is, for example, only going to last 3 minutes, you always get 1 cast of  Preparation. This means we can use the spell at the best of an encounter, which could be a burn phase, or even an add phase to maximize AoE damage. Keep in mind that you don't want to lose casts by holding it for too long!
