## Hero Talent: Fatebound

### tab_group_0 — Tabs: ['Fatebound']

#### Fatebound
One of the key talents that the single target build plays is  Preparation, which resets the cooldown of a majority of your cooldowns. On paper this is only a 2-3% DPS increase, but on short encounters, or saving it for an important moment in an encounter could be crucial. However, make sure you aren't holding onto it for too long where it'd cause you to lose casts over an encounter.
