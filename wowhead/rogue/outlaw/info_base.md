## ~~~~Outlaw Rogue Overview

Want to play a Rogue who fears no one and jumps bravely into the fray? A combatant who uses One-Handed weapons to stand their ground in open combat and unleash a whirlwind of attacks against one or multiple enemies? A master of arms who can also rely on a concealed pistol to support them in combat and surprise the enemy? In that case, you may want to give Outlaw a try!

Like any Rogue spec, Outlaw uses **Energy** to attack targets. These attacks can usually be classified as either a **Builder**, which generates Combo Points, or a **Finisher**, which consumes these Combo Points for a powerful strike.

Outlaw is a very fast-paced spec where you can hit a lot of buttons for direct damage. It also has the iconic [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_warrior_punishingblow.gif)Blade Flurry](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=13877/blade-flurry) mechanic, allowing you to deal AoE damage by continuing with your standard single-target rotation, rather than relying on AoE-specific abilities and rotations.

Often seen as a somewhat deterring aspect of the spec, [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_rollthebones.gif)Roll the Bones](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=315508/roll-the-bones) is not a very transparent mechanic to newcomers but adds some variance to how it performs. You should not let that interfere with your enjoyment of this specialization!

## ~~~~ Best Stats for Outlaw Rogue

You'll want to prioritize pieces with higher Agility(item level is almost king). You still want to get haste-heavy pieces until 25% Haste for both Trickster and Fatebound. As for Critical Strike and Versatility, both are nearly even in value for most, though Critical Strike does pull slightly ahead. Remember to consider external or temporary sources of stats from weapon enchants or trinket procs when trying to understand why a stat might be good or bad for you on your gear. Having a large amount of crit from an on-use trinket, for example, may reduce the value of crit for your character.

## Outlaw Rogue Rotation in Midnight Season 1

Outlaw Rogue is a fairly simple spec once you get past the high APM requirements. The 3 key factors of the spec. Using your cooldowns on cooldown, making sure to efficiently spend combo points without overcapping, and maintaining high APM and not missing a potential global.
