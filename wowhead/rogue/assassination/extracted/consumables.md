 BEST ASSASSINATION ROGUE ENCHANTS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Below is a list of the recommended enchants to use on your Assassination Rogue gear during Midnight Season 1.
Slot	Best Enchant
Both Weapons	 Enchant Weapon - Berserker's Rage  (other secondaries are also acceptable)
Helm	 Enchant Helm - Empowered Hex of Leeching 
Chest	 Enchant Chest - Mark of the Worldsoul 
Shoulder	 Enchant Shoulders - Silvermoon's Mending 
Legs	 Forest Hunter's Armor Kit 
Boots	 Enchant Boots - Shaladrassil's Roots 
Ring	 Enchant Ring - Silvermoon's Alacrity 
Diamond	 Indecipherable Eversong Diamond 
Other Gems	 Flawless Quick Garnet 
Assassination Rogue Gems in Midnight Season 1
You'll want to use  Indecipherable Eversong Diamond  as your Diamond, and then stack  Flawless Quick Garnet  gems. The additional damage from the crit diamond is generally not worth it.

For the remaining gem slots, always prioritize Critical Strike, Haste, and Mastery when possible. Depending on your Gear, you might have to make swaps to gems that are Haste focused or Mastery focused, instead of the recommended  Flawless Quick Garnet  gems. This is simply a part of the min-maxing during gearing and can occur when you have too much of a certain stat. Make sure to sim yourself using RaidBots to optimize this.
Assassination Rogue Weapon Enchant in Midnight Season 1
You'll want to use  Enchant Weapon - Berserker's Rage  generally, as Haste is one of our most important secondary stats. Other secondary stat enchants, such as crit, can be better in some situations, depending on your gear. Make sure to sim yourself using RaidBots to optimize this.
 BEST ASSASSINATION ROGUE CONSUMABLES IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
There are many consumables that players can use in combat in Midnight. Here are the best flasks, potions, and weapon buffs that Assassination Rogue can use in PvE content.

While Versatility is generally recommended as the safest option for gearing characters and for the added benefit of smoothing out gameplay feel, you should ideally be simming your character using raidbots to determine the best configuration for you.

Assassination Rogue Flask in Midnight Season 1
The best Flask for Assassination Rogue is  Flask of the Shattered Sun , as it gives Critical Strike, which is one of our best stats.
Assassination Rogue Combat Potions in Midnight Season 1
The best combat potion for Assassination Rogue is  Light's Potential . As an alternative,  Potion of Recklessness  can be good, but due to how much Critical Strike we will have on gear, this will generally hit Diminishing Returns and not be as useful. Depending on what your gear looks like, this can be a slightly better option.
Assassination Rogue Health Potions in Midnight Season 1
The best healing potion for Assassination Rogue is  Silvermoon Health Potion .
Assassination Rogue Weapon Buff in Midnight Season 1
 Thalassian Phoenix Oil  will be the best weapon buff for Assassination Rogue, as it gives us our main secondary stats. Make sure to apply it to both daggers.
Assassination Rogue Augment Rune in Midnight Season 1
With Midnight, the new Augment Rune is  Void-Touched Augment Rune.
Assassination Rogue Food in Midnight Season 1
Assassination Rogue wants to eat feast buffs such as  Silvermoon Parade for the Agility. Secondary stat food is competitive, but you will want to ensure you're getting Critical Strike and Haste.
