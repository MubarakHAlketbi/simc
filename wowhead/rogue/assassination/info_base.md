## ~~~~ Assassination Rogue Overview

Assassination Rogue is a dual-wielding, leather-wearing DPS specialization specializing in Damage-over-Time (DoT) effects. This powerful Nature and Bleed based damage is augmented by a full arsenal of cooldowns to further assassinate any enemies in our way. Being a DoT-based specialization does make us slower than our fellow Subtlety and Outlaw Rogues, but don't mistake slower gameplay as less potent!

## Assassination Rogue Rotation in Midnight Season 1

The rotation for Assassination Rogue focuses on a simple build/spend rotation, but instead of optimizing around short cooldowns or swapping between buttons, Assassination is a slow spec, with purposeful downtime that rewards playing around the gaps in your rotation correctly.

Crimson tempest especially is a very expensive button in terms of energy, and knowing when AoE is going to happen in an encounter will allow you to plan around it with your cooldowns, bleeds, and resources. |

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ~~~~ | **Managing Envenom Uptime:** [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/inv12_apextalent_rogue_implacable.gif)Implacable](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=1265385/implacable) enables a pooling playstyle that will require players to drop the buff from [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_disembowel.gif)Envenom](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=32645/envenom) at regular intervals in order to get energy back. Managing this without overcapping resources will be the key to success. |
