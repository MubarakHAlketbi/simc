## ~~~~Subtlety Rogue Overview

Subtlety Rogue is a dual-wielding, leather-wearing damage specialization focusing on short cycles of high damage and a major cooldown for an extended burst period every 90 seconds. It is the Rogue spec that uses [![](https://wow.zamimg.com/images/wow/icons/tiny/ability_rogue_shadowdance.gif)Shadow Dance](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=185313/shadow-dance) as its signature ability. Subtlety Rogues emphasize stacking cooldowns and centering gameplay around smaller damage windows, which can lead to high burst damage. If playing around periods of high burst damage during cooldowns and lower output outside of them sounds appealing, Subtlety is the perfect choice for you.

## Subtlety Rogue Rotation in Midnight Season 1

The rotation for Subtlety Rogue is based on cooldowns combined with two resources you need to manage. The basis is a builder-and-spender system, where combo point builders generate combo points. Finishers are used to consume these combo points when close to the maximum amount (6 or 7 combo points). Energy is the limiting resource most of the time, and it's completely normal to feel resource-starved. Cooldowns allow you to accelerate the cycle and give access to stronger builders, creating a damage profile with serious damage during cooldowns and very low outside.
