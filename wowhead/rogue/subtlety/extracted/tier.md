 TIER SET BONUSES FOR SUBTLETY ROGUE
Tier set bonuses are usually standardized to a 2-set and 4-set bonus for each tier set, meaning that one bonus is activated when two pieces of the armor set are worn, while another is activated once four pieces are worn.

For more information on Midnight Season 1 gear and tier set bonuses, check out the Subtlety Rogue gear page!


Subtlety Rogue Gear Guide
Midnight Season 1 Tier Set Bonuses for Subtlety Rogue
The Season 1 Tier set is fairly simple; this is typical for the first tier of an expansion to give players room to adjust for potential new systems and talent changes. The two-set bonus is a simple increase of finisher damage; this is powerful. A lot of Subtleties damage is in finishing moves. The four set bonus increases the duration of our major cooldown, making it more reliable to fit two  Shadow Dance into it. The result is a very strong two-set with a good, but not outstanding four-set bonus.
Motley of the Grim Jest
 Rogue Subtlety 12.0 Class Set 2pc - Finishing moves deal 1.5% more damage per combo point spent.
 Rogue Subtlety 12.0 Class Set 4pc - Shadow Blades has a 4.0 sec increased duration and causes your attacks to deal an additional 6.0% damage as Shadow.

Ideally, you'll have Legs as your off-piece as the tier option is poorly itemized for Subtlety Rogue. This is stat-related. Versatility is the least desirable stat, and the Leg piece offers high amounts of it.

A rough estimate of the power level of the Tier set:
Two-Set Bonus: 9% dps increase.
Four-Set Bonus: 5% dps increase.
This is only a minor optimization; wearing five pieces of the tier is fine if you don't have a high item-level alternative. Fully Best-In-Slot setups will often choose to replace the Chest piece instead. Many options are possible for gearing on Assassination, and we urge players to simulate themselves for more accurate answers.
Tier Set Bonus Rotation Changes for Subtlety Rogue
Neither of the tier set bonuses changes the rotation; what they do is more or less passively increase damage and widen the major cooldown spell to fit multiple instances of  Shadow Dance more comfortably. The duration of such dances can be increased from talents, which would previously have overflowed but now fit within.

 HOW TO ACQUIRE YOUR SUBTLETY ROGUE TIER SET BONUS IN MIDNIGHT SEASON 1
Tier sets can be acquired in three distinct ways:
Raiding: Dropped by specific bosses in Midnight Season 1 Raids.
Great Vault: Obtained by all activities that give Great Vault rewards, including raiding, Mythic+ dungeons, and open world content.
Matrix Catalyst: Creating tier armor from non-set pieces of the same item level using the Matrix Catalyst crafting system.
In Midnight Season 1, the Matrix Catalyst is unlocked for everyone from the start of the season and generates one charge every two weeks.
