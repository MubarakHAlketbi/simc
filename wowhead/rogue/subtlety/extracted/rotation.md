SECRET TECHNIQUE‎
 Secret Technique is a finishing move that's split in three parts, and is the only cooldown on Subtlety that has a static cooldown, which is reduced by haste. The following information is good to know:
The initial hit deals physical damage, while the second and third hit is shadow damage.
The Shadow damage attacks count as Clone Attacks, talents interacting with Shadow Clones, like e.g.  Replicating Shadows typically work with them.
The Shadow Clone Attacks are delayed, the first is 1 second, the second is 1.2 seconds after the initial physical damage hit.
The spell is soft target capped at 5 targets (damage reduced to the 6th, 7th, ... target).
Shadow Clone attacks from  Secret Technique count as pet attacks. The combat log fails to assign the clone attacks to the player if the person logging is at least 100 yards away from you. This can be solved by logging the fight yourself.
OPTIMAL USE
The optimal use of  Secret Technique is during  Shadow Dance as soon as possible.

THE HASTE PROBLEM, AND HOW TO SOLVE IT.
 Deepening Shadows increases the duration of  Shadow Dance based on your haste value; the amount of extra time can be very low, making it important to use spells as soon as possible.

The following is advised in order to minimize impact:
As  Trickster: Use a macro with  Secret Technique and  Shadow Dance.
As  Deathstalker: Use a macro with  Shuriken Storm and  Shadow Dance. 
General: Don't use  Shadow Dance during a Global Cooldown.

SHADOW DANCE
 Shadow Dance is the defining cooldown of Subtlety and allows the use of  Stealth specific spells for its duration. Many talents hook into it, passively increasing damage output, the duration, or giving other benefits in addition to that. The list below highlights some unique aspects:
 Shadow Dance can't be used while in  Stealth.
Macros using  Shadow Dance and  Shadowstrike at the same time require two button presses to activate  Shadowstrike.
 Shadow Dance can't be used again while it's up.
 Shadow Dance applies a negative modifier to your aggro; this is why your healer friend will get aggro after using it when questing together.
The haste interaction from  Deepening Shadows considers  The First Dance, which means it will extend  Shadow Dance for a longer time when under the effect of  The First Dance.
The haste interaction from  Deepening Shadows only works on things increasing your baseline haste. This means effects that increase haste by a % like bloodlust, don't work on it, while trinkets that give you a certain amount of haste on use will.
OPTIMAL USE
You always want to use  Shadow Dance with either very low combo points or finishing combo points.
 Deathstalker - Prefers to start  Shadow Dance with low combo points.
 Trickster - Prefers to start  Shadow Dance with finishing combo points.
Only use it if  Secret Technique is ready (not on cooldown).
 Shadow Dance should be used a second time during  Shadow Blades without  Secret Technique ready; it is advised not to use  Shadow Dance when  Shadow Blades has less than 10 second remaining to ensure two casts when it's ready.
Optimization: Tier set 4p bonus in combination with  Light Company Guidon:
Delaying your second  Shadow Dance cast during  Shadow Blades is beneficial. Use  Shadow Dance once  Shadow Blades has 8 seconds of duration left.

THE HASTE PROBLEM, AND HOW TO SOLVE IT.
 Deepening Shadows increases the duration of  Shadow Dance based on your haste value; the amount of extra time can be very low, making it important to use spells as soon as possible.

SHADOW DANCE
 Shadow Dance is the defining cooldown of Subtlety and allows the use of  Stealth specific spells for its duration. Many talents hook into it, passively increasing damage output, the duration, or giving other benefits in addition to that. The list below highlights some unique aspects:
 Shadow Dance can't be used while in  Stealth.
Macros using  Shadow Dance and  Shadowstrike at the same time require two button presses to activate  Shadowstrike.
 Shadow Dance can't be used again while it's up.
 Shadow Dance applies a negative modifier to your aggro; this is why your healer friend will get aggro after using it when questing together.
The haste interaction from  Deepening Shadows considers  The First Dance, which means it will extend  Shadow Dance for a longer time when under the effect of  The First Dance.
The haste interaction from  Deepening Shadows only works on things increasing your baseline haste. This means effects that increase haste by a % like bloodlust, don't work on it, while trinkets that give you a certain amount of haste on use will.

 Trickster - Prefers to start  Shadow Dance with finishing combo points.
