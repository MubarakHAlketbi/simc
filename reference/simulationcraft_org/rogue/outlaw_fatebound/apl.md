# MID1_Rogue_Outlaw_Fatebound — Action Priority List

Source: upstream SimC report (reference only — our APL may differ)

```
apply_poison,nonlethal=none,lethal=instant
snapshot_stats
Snapshot raid buffed stats before combat begins and pre-potting is done.
stealth,precombat_seconds=2
adrenaline_rush,precombat_seconds=1,if=talent.improved_adrenaline_rush
slice_and_dice,precombat_seconds=1,if=talent.improved_adrenaline_rush

stealth
Restealth if possible (no vulnerable enemies in combat).
kick
Interrupt on cooldown to allow simming interactions with that.

variable,name=finish_condition,value=combo_points>=cp_max_spend-1-(hero_tree.fatebound&!cooldown.between_the_eyes.ready)
Use finishers if at -1 from max combo points, but Fatebound uses Dispatch at -2.
variable,name=blade_flurry_sync,value=spell_targets.blade_flurry<2&raid_event.adds.in>20|buff.blade_flurry.up
call_action_list,name=cds

Builders High priority Ambush with Hidden Opportunity.
blade_flurry,if=talent.deft_maneuvers&spell_targets>=4
With Deft Maneuvers, build CPs with Blade Flurry at 4+ targets.
coup_de_grace,if=buff.disorienting_strikes.up
Prioritize Coup de Grace if Unseen Blade is guaranteed after Killing Spree.
pistol_shot,if=talent.audacity&talent.hidden_opportunity&buff.opportunity.up&!buff.audacity.up
With Audacity + Hidden Opportunity, consume Opportunity to proc Audacity any time Ambush is not available.
pistol_shot,if=talent.fan_the_hammer&buff.opportunity.up&(buff.opportunity.stack>=buff.opportunity.max_stack|buff.opportunity.remains<2)
With Fan the Hammer, consume Opportunity if at max stacks or if it will expire.
pistol_shot,if=talent.fan_the_hammer&buff.opportunity.up&(combo_points.deficit>=(1+talent.quick_draw+(talent.quick_draw*talent.fan_the_hammer.rank))&(combo_points>1|rtb_buffs<2|!talent.deal_fate))
With Fan the Hammer, consume Opportunity if it will not overcap CPs. Fatebound with stage 2 RTB tries to avoid consuming PS at 1CP.
pistol_shot,if=!talent.fan_the_hammer&buff.opportunity.up&(energy.base_deficit>energy.regen*1.5|combo_points.deficit<=1|talent.quick_draw.enabled|talent.audacity.enabled&!buff.audacity.up)
If not using Fan the Hammer, then consume Opportunity based on energy, when it will exactly cap CPs, or when using Quick Draw.
pool_resource,for_next=1
Fallback pooling just so Hidden Opportunity builds do not skip Ambush at low energy.
ambush,if=talent.hidden_opportunity
sinister_strike
adrenaline_rush,if=!buff.adrenaline_rush.up&(!variable.finish_condition|!talent.improved_adrenaline_rush)&(raid_event.adds.remains>5|raid_event.adds.in<5|!raid_event.adds.exists|!raid_event.adds.count)
Cooldowns Maintain Adrenaline Rush. With Improved AR, use at low CPs. Has a cursory check to try not to send if immediate downtime is expected.
blade_flurry,if=spell_targets>=2&buff.blade_flurry.remains<gcd
Maintain Blade Flurry at 2+ targets.
preparation,if=cooldown.adrenaline_rush.remains>30&!cooldown.between_the_eyes.ready&(!cooldown.killing_spree.ready|!hero_tree.trickster)|fight_remains<30
Use Preparation to reset Adrenaline Rush, Between the Eyes, and Killing Spree if Trickster.
keep_it_rolling,if=rtb_buffs=2&buff.roll_the_bones.remains<cooldown.adrenaline_rush.remains&!buff.loaded_dice.up&(cooldown.preparation.remains|!talent.preparation)|rtb_buffs>=3
Use Keep it Rolling with at least stage 2 of RtB. Try not to KIR at stage 2 if your next roll is guaranteed to have Loaded Dice.
roll_the_bones,if=!buff.roll_the_bones.up|rtb_buffs=1
Use Roll the Bones if not active, or reroll for stage 2.
blade_rush,if=set_bonus.mid1_4pc&!buff.whirl_of_blades.up|spell_targets=1&energy.base_time_to_max>2|spell_targets>=2
Use Blade Rush if tier bonus is not active, or in AoE, or if you will not overcap energy within the gcd on ST.
vanish,if=!variable.finish_condition&talent.hidden_opportunity&!buff.audacity.up&!buff.opportunity.up
Hidden Opportunity builds use Vanish or Shadowmeld for an extra Ambush in between procs.
shadowmeld,if=!variable.finish_condition&talent.hidden_opportunity&!buff.audacity.up&!buff.opportunity.up
potion,if=buff.bloodlust.react|fight_remains<30|buff.adrenaline_rush.up
blood_fury
berserking
fireblood
ancestral_call
use_items,slots=trinket1,if=buff.between_the_eyes.up|trinket.1.has_stat.any_dps|fight_remains<=20
Default conditions for usable items.
use_items,slots=trinket2,if=buff.between_the_eyes.up|trinket.2.has_stat.any_dps|fight_remains<=20
dispatch,if=!buff.slice_and_dice.up
Finishers
between_the_eyes,if=cooldown.adrenaline_rush.remains>30|buff.adrenaline_rush.up|!talent.supercharger|!talent.zero_in
With Supercharger and Zero In, hold BtE for an upcoming Adrenaline Rush
```
